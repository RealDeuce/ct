## Compatibility notice

Cepheus Trader v0.7.12 advances the player protocol from **CT-RPC 7 to 8**,
retains administrator and sysop protocol 2, and introduces the separate
**CT-League 1** protocol. The client-core C ABI remains at 1. The retained
outcome codec advances from 14 to 21, the Flight Plan proposal and snapshot
codecs advance from 2 to 4, the encounter-record codec advances from 1 to 4,
and the ship-record codec advances from 1 to 3. Those codecs retain documented
legacy readers, but the readers do not migrate a format-1 database.

The storage manifest advances from 1 to 2 and the BBS-polity generator from 1
to 4. There is no storage migration from v0.7.10: a v0.7.12 server deliberately
refuses that database. Stop and back up the v0.7.10 installation, install the
v0.7.12 server and complete client kit, initialize a fresh game store, and then
provision BBS and League credentials. Existing player accounts, ships, and
universe state are not imported.

CT-RPC 7 doors cannot connect to the v0.7.12 server. Although the administrator
and sysop protocol counters and client-core ABI are unchanged, mixed product
releases are not a supported deployment. Upgrade the server, door, sysop and
League Coordinator utilities, and `cepheus-trader-client-core` library
together.

The v0.7.11 tag failed required release CI and was never published. v0.7.12
contains the intended v0.7.11 release plus the arrival-combat correction that
made that failure reproducible.

## Highlights

- Added a distinct League Coordinator role with a League PSK, revisioned League
  naming and membership controls, one-time member-BBS credential handoff, League
  names alongside polity names, and new League BBSs placed near their peers.
- Made frontier expansion reuse suitable explored systems, grow alongside the
  existing frontier, preserve irregular three-dimensional placement, and keep
  every newly materialized BBS connected to explored Sol space.
- Added named gas-giant and wilderness-water fuel sources, explicit collection
  and refining times, refined-by-default Flight Plans, standalone refining, and
  proportional unrefined-fuel power use and misjump risk.
- Reworked hostile contacts to present only earned sensor and transponder
  intelligence, show capacity-limited pirate demands, transfer freight and the
  most valuable cargo that fits, and apply pursuit rules whether the player or
  contact runs.
- Hardened encounter and global scheduling so client submissions cannot crash
  the server, delayed work enters the single authoritative queue only when due,
  and the live clock remains responsive during catch-up.
- Preserved completed arrival checkpoints after surviving an automatically
  resolved combat instead of restoring an already-consumed approach leg.
- Added durable, censored command-loss reports on the next login, with captain,
  crew, cargo, ship, standing-order, recovery, and chronological outcome details
  before successor creation.
- Added operator-hosted universe atlases, six-parsec frontier surveys, persistent
  asteroid mining and field refining, capable-yard ship commissions, and the
  Leavitt pathfinder and Humboldt Foundry progression ships.
