## Compatibility notice

Cepheus Trader v0.7.16 advances the player protocol from **CT-RPC 11 to 12**.
The administrator and sysop protocols remain at 2, CT-League remains at 1,
and the client-core C ABI remains at 1. The storage manifest remains at 2;
the clock format, player-setup revision, and every generator and sampler
version are unchanged.

CT-RPC 11 doors cannot connect to a v0.7.16 server, and CT-RPC 12 doors cannot
connect to a v0.7.15 server. Back up and stop the v0.7.15 server, deploy the
v0.7.16 server and complete client kit together, then restart. The v0.7.15 and
v0.7.16 administrator, sysop, and League Coordinator utilities remain mutually
wire-compatible because their protocols are unchanged. Do not mix packaged
client executables with a different version of `cepheus-trader-client-core`.

Retained outcomes advance from codec 30 to 32. Codec 31 adds the outstanding
origin export tariff to docked snapshots, and codec 32 adds universal price
distributions to retained market leads; both retain the codec-30 reader needed
for an in-place upgrade. Ship records remain at codec 6, merchant work and
market leads at codec 2, and queued commands at codec 4. No universe migration
or reinitialization is required. Rolling back to v0.7.15 after v0.7.16 writes a
codec-31 or codec-32 retained outcome is unsupported.

## Highlights

- Kept every game, administrator, sysop, and League listener alive across
  aborted connections and other `accept()` errors instead of terminating the
  server during normal network traffic.
- Added the outstanding origin export tariff to Docked Operations so captains
  can see that departure charge before clearing the berth.
- Restored universal minimum, quartile, median, and maximum price landmarks for
  negotiated cargo offers while marking the actual quote in that distribution.
- Limited cargo-exchange selection prompts to eligible lots and improved
  arrival-wait and market-status presentation.
- Kept completed, failed, and cancelled market research visible as history
  until the captain explicitly dismisses it.
