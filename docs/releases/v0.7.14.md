## Compatibility notice

Cepheus Trader v0.7.14 advances the player protocol from **CT-RPC 9 to 10**.
The administrator and sysop protocols remain at 2, CT-League remains at 1,
and the client-core C ABI remains at 1. The storage manifest remains at 2,
the clock format and player-setup revision remain at 1, the local
player-identity registry remains at 5, and the browser-alert service database
remains at schema 1. Initial universe generation, stellar distribution,
celestial generation, observed coverage, BBS coverage, CNS5 distribution and
sampling, settlement capacity, frontier arrival, traffic ordering, place
naming, person naming, and ship-condition modeling all remain at version 1;
BBS-polity generation remains at version 4.

The ship-record codec advances from 3 to 4, Flight Plan proposal and snapshot
codecs advance from 4 to 6, the encounter-record codec advances from 4 to 5,
the interception-watch codec advances from 2 to 3, and the retained-outcome
codec advances from 22 to 28. Each retains the v0.7.13 reader needed for an
in-place upgrade. The redirected-flight trajectory codec remains at 1. New
operational-damage reports, encounter-policy defaults, and account-journal
entries use codec 1. Opening an existing format-2 universe creates the new
databases and performs one idempotent carried-forward account reconciliation;
no universe migration or reinitialization is required.

Stop the v0.7.13 server, back up the store, replace the server and player door
together, and restart. CT-RPC 9 doors cannot connect to the v0.7.14 server,
and CT-RPC 10 doors cannot connect to a v0.7.13 server. The v0.7.13 and
v0.7.14 administrator, sysop, and League Coordinator utilities remain mutually
wire-compatible because their protocols are unchanged. Deploy the complete
v0.7.14 client kit, including its matching `cepheus-trader-client-core`
library, rather than mixing packaged client files. A rollback to v0.7.13 after
the new server has written version-4 ship records, version-5 encounter records,
version-6 Flight Plans, version-3 interception watches, version-28 outcomes, or
the new codec-1 records is not supported.

## Highlights

- Added durable operational-damage reports that require acknowledgement on
  sign-on or while connected, while consolidating related combat damage to
  avoid a flood of alerts.
- Reworked arrival contacts around concrete actions, immediate routine-traffic
  resolution, declared vessel classes, and a Fight option for every contact.
- Made Flight Plans fully editable outside immutable Jump transit, including
  standing encounter orders selected by estimated combat outlook, distinct
  arrival/departure loci, private arrival points, and stationary onboard fuel
  processing.
- Added universal Accounts with a durable transaction journal and pending
  income grouped by estimated remittance day, and reorganized the universal
  command surface into focused submenus.
- Focused Message Management on useful correspondence, applied filters to the
  list itself, and removed redundant offers and low-value generated reports
  from the captain's normal review workload.
- Restored per-source distance and travel-time comparisons for frontier fuel,
  added warnings that departure cancels active repairs, and suppressed stale
  deadline warnings once a delivery is certified for settlement.
- Improved terminal presentation with adjacent centered QR pairing URLs,
  calmer option-menu colors, and expanded beginner and expert help for the
  changed operational screens.
- Rejected unknown protocol enum values instead of accepting malformed state.
