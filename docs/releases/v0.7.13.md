## Compatibility notice

Cepheus Trader v0.7.13 advances the player protocol from **CT-RPC 8 to 9**.
The administrator and sysop protocols remain at 2, CT-League remains at 1,
and the client-core C ABI remains at 1. The retained outcome codec advances
from 21 to 22 to preserve effective fuel capacity; its version-21 reader
treats that previously absent value as unknown. The Flight Plan proposal and
snapshot codecs remain at 4, the encounter-record codec remains at 4, and the
ship-record codec remains at 3. The new redirected-flight trajectory record
uses codec 1. The local player-identity registry remains at 5, and the new
browser-alert service database begins at schema 1.

The storage manifest remains at 2 and all generator versions are unchanged.
The v0.7.13 server opens a v0.7.12 game store directly; no migration or
reinitialization is required. Stop the server, back up the store, replace the
server and player door together, and restart. A rollback to v0.7.12 after the
new server has committed version-22 outcomes or redirected-flight trajectory
records is not supported.

CT-RPC 8 doors cannot connect to the v0.7.13 server, and CT-RPC 9 doors cannot
connect to a v0.7.12 server. Upgrade those two components as one deployment.
The v0.7.12 and v0.7.13 administrator, sysop, and League Coordinator utilities
remain mutually wire-compatible with both server releases because their
protocols are unchanged. Deploy the complete v0.7.13 client kit, including its
matching `cepheus-trader-client-core` library, rather than mixing packaged
client files.

## Highlights

- Added captain-scoped browser push alerts with expiring enrollment links,
  portable Synchronet communicator pages, QR pairing, device revocation, and
  retained delivery notices.
- Made underway Flight Plans redirectable throughout ordinary in-system
  flight, with bounded-thrust vector trajectories and recalculated arrival
  times; physical Jump remains the sole immutable travel phase.
- Rejects plans that cannot meet their projected Jump-fuel requirements and
  clears completed plans and stale arrival checkpoints from the door.
- Replaced the misleading Jump Reserve display with effective tank capacity
  and permits exact fractional-ton dockside fuel purchases, including a
  complete fill.
- Expanded Beginner and Expert context help across operational screens and
  made menu prompts page safely without losing input, visual intensity, or
  delivered waypoint alerts.
- Added an explicit Voyage Status refresh action while preserving a stable,
  non-scrolling periodic view.
- Reports rejected PSK credentials clearly and hardens server restart and
  universe-reset behavior uncovered by the reduced simulation fixtures.
