# Tender Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 202*

## Identity and architecture

Tender is an independent 30-ton / approximately 420 m³ TL9 standard ship's
boat, 17.5 × 8 × 5.2 m. One pilot, one week of fuel, `sJ`/`sJ` thrust 6,
16.7 tons cargo, no armor, no Jump drive, and one unused hardpoint define it.

A raised three-pane cab, broad rounded utility body, closed two-leaf freight
shutter, separate inspection and cab hatches, shallow keel, and integrated
four-aperture/two-grille stern distinguish it from Dory and Lighter. One dorsal
3–4 m ring remains under a smooth cover. No weapon, accommodation, scoop,
processor, external tank, or exposed cargo may appear.

Concord warm ivory `#F1E3C2`, royal blue `#2354A3`, vermilion `#E44B2D`, and
polished chrome organize the cargo cell, service hatches, datum rails, covered
hardpoint, and six-gravity drive block in bright 1970s/1980s technical art.
