# Xanadu Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 071*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-71`; `ship-71` Xanadu
- Shipyard path: Path 1, Concord Exchange Yards
- Hull: 400 displacement tons / approximately 5,600 m³
- Dimensions: 65 m long, 23 m wide, and 15 m high
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11; non-standard design
- Protection: no armor
- Drives: Jump-D / Jump-2, one Jump; maneuver J and power J / thrust 4
- Endurance: two weeks of power-plant fuel
- Control and complement: holographic standard bridge, basic military
  electronics, Model/3 computer, and eight crew
- Hotel accommodation: nineteen staterooms, galley for 28, recreation space
  for 100, six garden spaces, gymnasium, two offices, twelve pool/spa spaces,
  sixteen theater seats, two emergency low berths, and one medical bed
- Working fit: repair drones and a 69-ton baggage/commercial hold; no hangar,
  carried craft, fuel processor, or scoop
- Defense: one mixed beam/sand/missile triple turret, four point-defense lasers,
  twelve standard missiles, and twenty sand canisters
- Hardpoint capacity: one occupied station and three unused fire-control
  stations, represented by exactly four exterior coordinates

Xanadu is a luxury passenger ship for secure, well-serviced routes. Its hotel,
garden, lounge, spa, theater, office, medical, and recreation spaces dominate
the exterior identity. The turret and point defense are discreet safeguards,
not a claim to naval status.

## Independent resort-spindle chassis

The 65 × 23 × 15 m streamlined hull is a graceful “resort spindle”: softly
faceted rounded command prow, long broad elliptical hotel body, shallow
integral promenade shoulders, raised midship conservatory lantern, lower port
spa/lounge observation bay, windowless aft theater/service block, narrowed
machinery waist, and clean tapered thrust-four stern. All raised volumes are
occupied hull rather than wings, pods, tanks, rings, or separate craft. The
tapered envelope plausibly contains approximately 5,600 m³.

Xanadu is independent of the 300-ton Verne family. Never copy Verne's packet-
liner spearhead, twelve-leaf commercial insert, three-socket dorsal row,
garage/hangar reservations, or scoop. Never copy Argosy's cargo whale,
Magellan's exploration caravel, or Stevenson geometry. Concord lineage comes
from component finish and orderly service detail, not silhouette reuse.

A low nine-pane bridge brow crosses the rounded prow. Exactly ten of nineteen
small equal stateroom windows form one gently rising visible port promenade
row; nine cabin positions are hidden starboard. Two separate larger office
windows sit forward beside the formal passenger airlock. The office ports,
bridge panes, and public-space glazing are not stateroom windows.

One segmented oval conservatory lantern crowns the midship hotel volume and
expresses six garden spaces through blue-green privacy glazing. One broad
segmented panoramic recreation/lounge gallery runs beneath the cabin row.
One separate low oval spa/pool observation boundary uses deep blue framing and
dark privacy louvers. The aft theater block remains windowless. None of these
public spaces is a bridge, hangar, cargo door, weapon bay, or exposed room.

One closed crew/service airlock remains separate from the formal passenger
airlock. One visible emergency-berth panel and its hidden starboard mate, one
plain medical-service hatch, one gym service panel, and one repair-drone slot
remain closed and unmarked. No panel carries a cross, badge, text, or emblem.

One closed 8 × 4 m two-leaf baggage/cargo shutter occupies the lower aft-port
hotel body. Its polished frame, low sill, and handling guides serve the 69-ton
hold. Xanadu has no hangar or carried craft; never open the shutter or show
cargo, vehicle, luggage train, or launch in the recognition plate.

## Hardpoint and discreet-defense map

Exactly four turret hardpoint coordinates cover complementary arcs:

1. visible lower-aft port flank at the spa/theater transition, occupied by the
   mixed triple turret;
2. visible forward upper-port shoulder, closed and empty;
3. hidden upper-aft starboard shoulder, closed and empty; and
4. hidden lower-forward starboard/ventral station, closed and empty.

The front-port plate therefore exposes two of four hardpoint coordinates but
only one installed turret. Its low shared housing contains exactly three unlike
faces: one narrow beam-laser optic, one closed missile-rack shutter, and one
blunt four-port sandcaster projector. Ammunition remains internal. The turret's
lower-flank location provides port, aft, and ventral coverage without spoiling
the hotel crown or turning every hardpoint into a dorsal showpiece.

Four point-defense laser coordinates form a balanced coverage shell:

- visible forward upper-port cheek;
- visible lower-aft port keel;
- hidden upper-aft starboard shoulder; and
- hidden lower-forward starboard/ventral cheek.

Each node is a 0.4–0.8 m clean optical lens in a discreet vermilion/chrome
collar, much smaller than a turret ring. The plate shows exactly two of four.
Never arm bridge or cabin glazing, conservatory ribs, lounge/spa frames, office
ports, airlocks, cargo latches, sensors, Jump panels, or drive details.

## Jump, drive, and unarmored service landmarks

The internal Jump-D drive reads through one elegant segmented transverse
service/radiator band around the aft waist and a bilateral pair of low oval
coil-service blisters, one visible and one hidden. There is no external ring,
glow, or luminous portal.

Four broad maneuver apertures are visible in the port stern bank and four
matching starboard apertures hidden. Two smaller separate power grilles serve
the J/J thrust-four machinery. There are no processors and no scoop.

Xanadu has no armor. Preserve its smooth light commercial shell, fine rails,
polished frames, gentle component reveals, and broad uninterrupted hotel
surfaces. Never add heavy belts, citadel plates, military shutters, or armored
prow geometry merely because defensive weapons are present.

## Concord recognition finish

- Principal pressure hull, hotel body, conservatory surround, theater block:
  warm ivory `#F1E3C2`
- Lower hotel datum band, lounge/spa frames, baggage shutter, selected Jump
  and engineering panels: royal blue `#2354A3`
- Passenger/crew airlocks, service and recovery details, turret and PD collars,
  drive accents: vermilion `#E44B2D`
- Bridge, cabin, office, lounge, conservatory, spa, access, cargo, hardpoint,
  Jump, landing and drive interfaces: polished bright chrome/aluminum
- Glazing, privacy glass, recesses, shutter seams, slats and grilles: dark
  teal/blue-green

Aouda supplies Concord finish vocabulary only. Xanadu retains its independent
resort spindle, conservatory, public-space glazing, exact cabin map, baggage
hold, discreet lower-flank turret, no-armor shell, and J/J stern.
