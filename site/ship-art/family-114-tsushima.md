# Tsushima Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production singleton family 114*

## Mechanical identity

- Record: `ship-114` Tsushima; Admiralty Line (P7)
- Hull: 2,000 displacement tons / approximately 28,000 m³; standard;
  108 × 40 × 28 m
- Fit: TL11, armor ten, reinforced structure, armored bridge/drives, improved
  magazine, Jump Q / Jump-2, Y/Y thrust 4, three weeks endurance, advanced
  electronics, command/hardened holographic bridge, dual Model 3 bis/fib
- Complement: 124 crew, twelve staterooms, thirteen crew berthings, two
  armories, two briefing rooms, two emergency berths, two gyms, six offices,
  four-place brig, workshop, three-bed medical bay, repair drones, 127.666 tons
  cargo
- Craft: one Argo boarding launch and one Wayfarer Cargo in a 50-ton hangar
- Weapons: three triple-beam, three triple-missile, two triple-sandcaster
  turrets; four meson and four particle bays; twenty PD lasers

Tsushima is an independent command-bastille cruiser: blunt octagonal docking
prow, eight-pane command brow, polygonal saddle, four diagonal occupied battery
buttresses, protected waist, asymmetric hangar shoulder, and squared stern. It
is not a stretched Fisher, Themistocles, or Jellicoe.

## Sixteen-coordinate coverage map

Eight used hardpoints are visible and eight hidden. Four buttress endplates
carry visible bays: upper-forward and upper-aft particle apertures use blue-
white insulated faces; lower-forward and lower-aft meson bays use deep dark
throats. Hidden starboard buttresses repeat two particle and two meson bays.

Four visible turrets are two beam triples on centerline-forward and forward-
flank rings, one three-shutter missile turret on the lower-mid port flank, and
one three-head perforated sandcaster on the aft upper shoulder. Hidden stations
complete one beam, two missile, and one sandcaster turret. Coverage spans
centerline, forward, aft, upper, lower, and flank arcs without a dorsal row.
Twenty PD coordinates split ten visible/ten hidden.

One closed 14 × 7 m shutter serves internal shaped berths for the 16.5 × 7 ×
4.8 m Argo and 18.8 × 8.4 × 4.0 m Wayfarer Cargo. Both remain internal.
Separate cargo/support doors, docking collar, six of twelve stateroom windows,
and one of two emergency panels remain closed and unarmed.

Fourteen processors split seven visible/seven hidden beside a protected scoop.
Internal Jump-Q machinery uses a segmented service band and six flush coil
housings, never a ring/glow. Four visible/four hidden maneuver apertures and
separate power grilles form the Y/Y stern. Armor ten and protected structure
read through deep buttresses, layered prow, redundant frames, chrome load paths
and armored openings.

Admiralty finish is navy `#203B69`, warm white `#E8E5D4`, red `#C43C35`,
chrome/aluminum, and gunmetal in a complete front-port late-1970s/early-1980s
gouache-and-airbrush technical plate. No text, logo, watermark, open door,
external craft, weapon fire, planet, modern CGI, wing, tail, truss, or Jump ring.

## Production and provenance

established the bastille hull. Production preserved the 4-bay/4-turret count;
three scoped edits corrected the upper-aft particle face, replaced projecting
missile barrels with three shutters, and replaced the aft barrels with three
perforated sandcaster heads.
