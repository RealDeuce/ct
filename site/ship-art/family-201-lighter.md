# Lighter Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 201*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-201`; `ship-201` Lighter
- Hull: 40 displacement tons / approximately 560 m³; 20.5 × 9 × 5.5 m
- Configuration: standard; TL9 standard design; no armor
- Performance: `sK` maneuver, `sL` power / thrust 5; one week; no Jump drive
- Control: one pilot in a one-person control cabin; Model/1 computer
- Payload: 25 tons internal cargo; no external load or accommodation
- Armament: none; one unused hardpoint/fire-control station

## Independent Concord pinnace

Lighter is a fast non-streamlined cargo transfer craft with a three-pane
single-place cab, huge replaceable freight pressure body, shallow keel, and
broad four-corner maneuver block. One closed two-leaf port shutter occupies
almost the full centerbody and represents 25 tons / approximately 350 m³ of
payload. Its cab hatch, cargo sockets, protected rails, and six landing doors
remain closed. There are no passenger windows or accommodation openings.

One dorsal 3–4 m ring over the forward freight frame remains under a smooth
flush cover. Never add a turret, emitter, launcher, PD node, or decorative gun.
Four large maneuver apertures and one substantial power grille make five-
gravity performance visibly distinct from Dory’s low-output stern. Fuel is
internal for one week; there is no Jump drive, scoop, processor, or tank.

## Concord finish

Concord warm ivory `#F1E3C2`, royal blue `#2354A3`, vermilion `#E44B2D`, and
polished chrome organize the cargo shutter, cab, load frame, hardpoint cover,
and drive collars. Bright period color communicates maintained high-speed port
utility rather than a drab military tender.

## Invariants

Preserve the 20.5 × 9 × 5.5 m hull; three-pane cab; enormous two-leaf shutter
and frame; cab hatch; covered dorsal hardpoint; cargo sockets and rails; four
maneuver apertures; one power grille; landing doors; seams; camera; crop;
backdrop; and lighting. Never arm the craft, open the hold, expose cargo, add
accommodation, Jump/fuel hardware, external craft or tank, wings, fins, flame,
text, logo, or watermark.
