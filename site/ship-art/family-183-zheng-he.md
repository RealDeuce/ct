# Zheng He Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 183*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-183`; `ship-183` Zheng He
- Hull: 400 displacement tons / approximately 5,600 m³
- Dimensions: 62 m long, 22 m wide, and 15 m high
- Configuration: standard; technology level 11
- Protection: four armor points from one crystaliron layer
- Drives: Jump-D / Jump-2, maneuver F, and power F / thrust 3
- Endurance: one Jump and four weeks of power-plant fuel
- Control: advanced electronics, hardened holographic bridge, and fib/bis
  Model/2 computer
- Mission support: 30 crew, armory, two brigs, two-bed medical bay, workshop,
  repair drones, boarding spaces, and five tons of cargo/evidence
- Boat facilities: one 30-ton standard hangar carrying one Grapnel (`ship-211`)
- Main battery: one missile bank and two particle-beam barbettes
- Secondary battery: one mixed beam/beam/sand triple turret
- Point defense: two miniguns and two lasers
- Ammunition: 120 missiles and 40 sand canisters
- Fuel handling: one scoop and five processors

## Canonical patrol hull

Zheng He occupies a 62 × 22 × 15 m integrated standard pressure hull. A
clear protected command prow, deep rectangular mission core, shielded boarding
waist, strong lower keel, pinched service section, and compact high-output aft
block produce Marque clipper-like longitudinal order without becoming an
atmospheric lifting body. It has no wings or decorative fins.

The hardened bridge sits beneath a brow with exactly six narrow panes. Paired
advanced cheek faces, one circular instrument collar, and one fine antenna grid
occupy fixed sensor sockets and remain barrel-free. Two protected boarding
airlocks, handholds, folded grapples, and shielded docking gear form a working
waist rather than ornamental piracy cues.

## Missile bank

One large forward/lower port frame carries exactly twenty-four small closed
missile shutters in four rows of six. The corrected grid is the production
standard: no fifth row, partial edge cell, exposed tube, or visible missile.
Its burgundy-and-gold outer frame remains fixed and clearly separate from the
small cargo hatch and aft hangar.

The bank holds a much larger ready/magazine system than a turret, but it is not
a 51-ton capital bay. The closed cell field and 120-missile inventory communicate
sustained anti-raider fire without turning the whole flank into an open launcher.

## Barbette, turret, and PD map

Two particle-beam barbettes form one bilateral pair. The standard view shows
one 7–9 m semi-recessed port-shoulder fairing with one insulated particle
emitter; the starboard twin is occluded. It remains substantially larger than a
turret cup and does not project a long conventional barrel.

The ship has one triple turret, outward-canted on the forward dorsal/port
shoulder for broad forward, dorsal, port, and aft traverse. Its compact cup has
exactly two beam optics and one blunt sandcaster aperture. No second turret is
hidden or implied.

Four PD nodes form two complementary pairs. The plate shows one tiny blue-white
laser lens on the forward port shoulder and one tiny dark rotary minigun
aperture on the aft port/ventral lip. Their laser and minigun partners are
hidden on starboard/lower surfaces. Both node types remain much smaller than
the turret.

The missile bank and paired barbettes dominate axial/lateral attack. The canted
turret and mixed PD types provide cross-axis coverage without sacrificing the
boarding waist or moving every trainable component to the roof.

## Grapnel hangar and access

One closed aft-port standard-hangar shutter protects at least 21 m internal
axis, 10 m beam, and 6 m height for the canonical 18.6 m Grapnel armored
boarding boat. The 30-ton craft remains inside and never appears beside Zheng
He in the catalog plate. The hangar remains separate from the small closed
five-ton cargo/evidence hatch and all weapon frames.

Boarding airlocks, folded grapples, docking gear, repair access, and medical
interfaces carry no barrels and must not be mistaken for extra weapons.

## Drives and fuel recognition

Jump-D uses one compact non-glowing segmented upper-aft radiator and two paired
field-coil/service housings. Four recessed maneuver apertures and two power
grilles identify the F/F thrust-three stern.

One explicit protected scoop intake projects beneath the lower forward keel.
Exactly five small processor grilles form one orderly protected bank. Unlike
recent no-scoop capital ships, Zheng He must retain both systems; neither may be
duplicated or confused with a launch cell.

## Marque finish

Marque Marine Yards uses emerald `#13705B`, cream `#EFE0B5`, burgundy
`#8D2E3C`, gold-toned chrome, and bright steel. Emerald carries the hull and
thrust lines; cream identifies command and mission panels; burgundy defines
boarding, weapon, magazine, and hangar boundaries; gold/chrome protects turret,
barbette, airlock, docking, and edge-rib collars. The result is handsome licensed
authority, visibly armed but not naval gray.
