# Nausicaa Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 125*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-125`, `ship-125` Nausicaa
- Shipyard path: Path 1, Concord Exchange Yards
- Hull: 30 displacement tons / approximately 420 m³
- Dimensions: 19.5 m long, 7.6 m wide, and 4.5 m high
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11
- Protection: one point of titanium-steel armor and radiation shielding
- Performance: `sE` maneuver and power / thrust 3; two-week endurance
- Control/occupancy: two-person cockpit, four acceleration seats, one airlock
- Working fit: 16.75-ton cargo hold
- Limitations: no weapon, ammunition, Jump drive, scoop, or sleeping space

Nausicaa is a cargo-heavy ship's boat, landing craft, and lifeboat. It is a
separate Concord design, not a Wayfarer variant despite sharing displacement.

## Canonical architecture

The boat uses a deep oval/lozenge pressure body with a rounded wedge prow, high
shoulder chine, short full-height cargo cabin, slightly pinched waist, and an
integrated blunt aft drive fairing. Approximately 63 percent of its bounding
box is filled, plausibly enclosing the required 420 m³ while retaining landing
gear and aerodynamic fairing volume.

One low two-person cockpit has exactly four protected panes. Exactly two paired
passenger-window bays follow on the visible port flank, representing four
acceleration seats. One rectangular pressure airlock sits aft of those bays.
One broad closed two-leaf cargo shutter, approximately 4.8 × 2.9 m, dominates
the aft flank and makes the 16.75-ton hold legible.

A continuous double-skin shielding band surrounds the occupied and cargo body.
Two recessed maneuver-field apertures and one central power grille serve the
`sE`/`sE` drive fit. Six flush landing-gear door seams, two recovery/tie-down
sockets, one emergency service panel, and a flush commercial sensor strip
complete the chassis. There is no hardpoint ring, scoop, tank, or Jump landmark.

## Concord finish

- Upper pressure body and command prow: warm ivory `#F1E3C2`
- Lower hull and aft drive fairing: royal blue `#2354A3`
- Airlock, cargo edge, recovery marks, and emergency panel: vermilion
  `#E44B2D`
- Shielding band, windows, doors, drive collars, edges, and datum line:
  polished aluminum/chrome
- Glazing: dark bronze
- Character: maintained scheduled-port equipment with restrained cargo-sill
  wear and visible lifeboat markings, not military camouflage
