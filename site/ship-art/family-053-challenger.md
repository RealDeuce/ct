# Challenger Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 053*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-53`; `ship-53` Challenger
- Shipyard path: Path 4, Civic Survey Works
- Hull: 120 displacement tons / approximately 1,680 m³
- Dimensions: 43 m long, 12.5 m wide, and 8.5 m high
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11; non-standard design
- Protection: four whole points of crystaliron armor
- Drives: Jump-B / Jump-2, one Jump; maneuver E and power E / thrust 5
- Endurance: two weeks of power-plant fuel
- Control and complement: standard bridge, basic military electronics,
  Model/3 computer, and four crew
- Accommodation: four staterooms and one emergency low berth
- Mission support: one fuel processor, one standard five-ton hangar, and only
  2 tons cargo
- Armament: one mixed double turret with one beam laser and one sandcaster;
  twenty internal sandcaster canisters

Challenger is an armed survey courier and a starting player design. It carries
reconnaissance results, mail, dispatches, and compact valuable cargo between
frontier systems. Its small unassigned hangar supports utility work but does
not authorize an invented carried craft.

## Canonical survey-compass chassis

The 43 × 12.5 × 8.5 m hull is a long “survey compass”: narrow rounded chisel
prow, deep protected command capsule, two occupied swept sensor shoulders,
recessed waist, long faceted expedition keel, compact port utility-bay
shoulder, and tapered engineering stern. The envelope plausibly encloses about
1,680 m³.

One shallow circular/elliptical instrument crown is integrated into the
forward waist. Its ringed dark-teal optical flats form a survey sensor, never a
turret, docking ring, weapon bay, or exposed craft. Three other large flush
dark-teal sensor facets occupy the prow and shoulders. Civic symmetry and clean
instrument collars distinguish the design from a fighter.

Challenger is independent of Warden and Galileo. Never copy Warden's blunt
customs shield, enormous Caduceus cell, no-Jump stern, or turret coordinate;
never copy Galileo's standard observatory hull, bilateral Dory cells,
laboratory modules, or three-turret map.

Exactly five panes form the command brow. Two small stateroom ports are visible
on the port waist; two starboard positions are occluded. One closed formal
docking/airlock hatch and one separate emergency-berth service panel sit low
and forward. One much smaller closed 1.5 m square locker hatch serves the
two-ton cargo allocation.

## Empty utility hangar and weapon map

One closed ribbed 4.5 × 3.2 m utility-hangar shutter occupies the mid-to-aft
port shoulder, with at least a 9 m internal axis and five tons contained
capacity. No carried craft is catalogued. Keep the hangar empty/unassigned:
never show a craft, drone, vehicle, external clamp, or open shutter.

The sole 3–4 m ring lies on the mid upper port flank/shoulder, canted outward
and physically separate from both the survey crown and hangar. One shared low
mixed double cup contains exactly one narrow dark-teal/cyan beam optic and one
blunt four-port sandcaster projector. The plate shows the one of one turret;
zero weapon mounts are hidden. All twenty canisters remain internal. Never arm
the survey crown or add point defense, a second ring, missile shutter, or
gun-like sensor.

## Fuel, Jump, and drive landmarks

One square processor grille and adjacent flush circular refueling coupling lie
on the lower aft port keel. There is no scoop. Jump-B / Jump-2 is expressed by
one segmented transverse heat-rejection band across the aft upper waist and
two oval recessed coil/service blisters immediately ahead of it. These do not
glow and never become an external Jump ring.

Four maneuver apertures form port/starboard vertical pairs at the stern; two
port apertures are visible and two starboard apertures occluded. Smaller ribbed
power grilles remain separate. Two flowing interlocking armor bands protect the
command capsule, sensor shoulders, keel, and utility bay.

## Civic Survey recognition finish

- Main pressure body, command capsule, expedition keel: warm white `#F4F0D8`
- Command/sensor bands, hangar and cargo frames, Jump band, stern panels:
  cyan `#25A9B8`
- Processor, emergency panel, hangar latches, survey status, recovery and
  turret collar: lime `#A8C83E`
- Armor, docking, instrument, glazing, hangar/cargo, Jump, fuel and drive
  interfaces: polished chrome/aluminum
- Optical glass, recesses, slats and grilles: dark teal/gunmetal

Warden supplies finish vocabulary only; Challenger retains its independent
survey-compass geometry and mixed two-aperture side turret.

changed only that face to a four-port 2 × 2 grid while preserving the beam
optic, shared turret housing, and every chassis coordinate.

cargo and fuel boundaries; unarmed survey crown/facets; one two-aperture mixed
turret; Jump band/blisters; 2/2 maneuver map; armor, landing/service detail;
camera; crop; backdrop; and lighting.
