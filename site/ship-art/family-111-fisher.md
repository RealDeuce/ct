# Fisher Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production singleton family 111*

## Mechanical identity

- Record: `ship-111` Fisher; Admiralty Line (P7)
- Hull: 1,800 displacement tons / approximately 25,200 m³; standard
- Dimensions: 100.0 × 42.0 × 28.0 m
- Fit: TL11, eight armor points, reinforced structure, armored bridge and
  drives, improved magazine, Jump P / Jump-2, maneuver T and power Z / thrust
  4, two weeks endurance, advanced electronics, command/hardened holographic
  bridge, and dual Model 3 bis/fib computers
- Complement: eighty-eight crew, ten staterooms, ten crew berthings, armory,
  two briefing rooms, two emergency berths, two gyms, five offices, four-place
  brig, workshop, three-bed medical bay, repair drones, and 36.7 tons cargo
- Craft: one Wayfarer Cargo and one Proteus Surveyor in one 80-ton full hangar
- Weapons: nine mixed beam/missile/sandcaster triple turrets, four meson bays,
  five particle-beam bays, and eighteen point-defense lasers

Fisher is an independent broadside fleet cruiser, not a Themistocles variant.
Its broad armored-anvil body, overhanging hammer prow, buried bridge bunker,
continuous casemate shoulders, four corner battery sponsons, pinched hangar
waist, and squared stern establish a separate Admiralty hull.

## Canonical architecture

The 100 × 42 × 28 m standard hull is a deep rectangular/lozenge pressure body
with a blunt overhanging hammer prow, recessed eight-pane bridge, thick axial
spine, continuous upper and lower port casemates, deep keel, and four occupied
corner sponsons. About eight deck levels, fine plating, human-scale windows,
and large recessed systems communicate capital volume. It is integrated but
non-aerodynamic: never add wings, tail, lifting-body curves, or external truss.

One closed 15 × 7 m aft-waist shutter serves shaped internal berths for the
18.8 × 8.4 × 4.0 m Wayfarer Cargo and 26 × 9.5 × 5.4 m Proteus Surveyor.
Both craft remain internal. A separate cargo shutter, docking collar, five of
ten stateroom windows, and one of two emergency-berth panels remain closed and
unarmed. Crew berthing is windowless.

## Eighteen-coordinate coverage map

Nine hardpoints are visible and nine hidden:

1. Centerline bow: one particle-beam bay.
2. Upper-forward port casemate: one deep meson-gun bay.
3. Lower-forward port keel/sponson: one particle-beam bay.
4. Upper-aft port sponson: one particle-beam bay.
5. Lower-aft port casemate: second visible meson-gun bay.
6. Forward upper-port shoulder: one mixed triple turret.
7. Midship port flank: second mixed triple turret.
8. Aft lower-port flank: third mixed triple turret.
9. Aft upper-center/port shoulder: fourth mixed triple turret.

The hidden half supplies two meson bays, two particle bays, and five mixed
turrets. Every mixed housing combines one cyan beam optic, one closed missile
shutter, and one perforated sandcaster projector. The odd centerline particle
bay explains why five of nine bays are visible; the ninth mixed turret is on an
occluded ventral/far-side coordinate. The visible stations cover forward, aft,
upper, lower, centerline, and flank arcs rather than forming a dorsal display.
Eighteen PD coordinates split nine visible/nine hidden.

Bay apertures remain deeply rooted hull architecture and dwarf turret rings.
Doors, docking collar, processor grilles, scoop, sensors, Jump housings, and
stern apertures are not weapons.

## Machinery, armor, finish, and plate

Fourteen processors split seven visible/seven hidden beside a protected
ventral scoop. Internal Jump-P machinery reads as a segmented transverse band
and six flush coil housings, never an external ring or glow. Three visible and
three hidden maneuver apertures sit beside larger power grilles in the T/Z
thrust-4 stern. Eight armor, reinforced structure, protected bridge/drives, and
improved magazines read through massive sponsons, recessed apertures, redundant
frames, bright load paths, and armored shutters.

Admiralty finish uses deep navy `#203B69`, warm white `#E8E5D4`, signal red
`#C43C35`, bright aluminum/chrome, and gunmetal. Use the complete front-port
near-orthographic view, charcoal stars, faint teal grid, and original late-
1970s/early-1980s gouache-and-airbrush technical-art finish. No text, logo,
watermark, open door, external craft, weapon fire, planet, modern CGI, wing,
tail, or Jump ring.

## Production sequence and provenance

Generated on 2026-08-21 with OpenAI image generation in built-in mode. The
neutral anchor established the armored-anvil hull. The first production pass
preserved four mixed turrets but made only four bays unmistakable; one scoped
single-object edit activated the reserved lower-forward particle bay and
completed the five-bay/four-turret standard-view map.
