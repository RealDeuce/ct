# Myrmidon Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 029*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-29`; `ship-29` Myrmidon
- Shipyard path: Path 8, Redoubt Shipbuilding
- Hull: 40 displacement tons / approximately 560 m³
- Dimensions: 24.5 m long, 9 m wide, and 5.6 m high
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11; non-standard design
- Protection: four whole points of small-craft crystaliron armor
- Drives: maneuver sL and power sL / thrust 6; no Jump drive
- Endurance: one week of power-plant fuel
- Control and complement: four-person control cabin; two pilots, one turret
  gunner, and one other crew member
- Accommodation: two small-craft staterooms
- Mission support: one airlock and 6 tons of cargo/stores
- Armament: one trainable single turret with one beam laser; no ammunition

Myrmidon is an armored strike boat rather than an enlarged cockpit fighter.
Its cabin, two staterooms, airlock, and stores let four crew patrol and strike
beyond immediate carrier recovery range. It remains carrier- or base-dependent
for interstellar movement and long-duration logistics: there is no Jump drive,
fuel scoop, or processor.

## Canonical armored-beetle chassis

The 24.5 × 9 × 5.6 m pressure body is one compact streamlined “armored beetle”:
a short blunt hammer/ram prow, high arched central carapace, thick occupied side
buttresses, deep faceted belly, subtle service waist, and very short broad
engineering stern. The buttresses are integral pressure volume, not wings or
external pods. The filled envelope plausibly encloses approximately 560 m³.

One buried five-pane control brow crosses the forward carapace: two panes to
port, one slightly wider center pane, and two panes to starboard. Exactly two
smaller protected stateroom ports lie on the visible port upper flank behind
the bridge. A human-scale closed pressure airlock occupies the forward-lower
port flank. A separate closed rectangular shutter on the aft-lower port flank
serves the six-ton hold. Keep the bridge, cabin ports, airlock, and cargo
shutter visually and dimensionally distinct.

Four armor points appear as two interlocking bright edge/shoulder bands and
overlapping accessible carapace plates. Landing-pad door seams, recovery
sockets, tie-downs, handholds, and fine maintenance panels establish the scale.
All crew, stateroom interiors, and stores remain inside the closed hull.

## Weapon station and coverage

The single 3–4 m trainable ring occupies the mid upper port flank/shoulder. It
is visibly off the dorsal centerline and canted outward. The production fit
installs one compact cup with exactly one short narrow dark-teal/cyan optical
emitter. This port three-quarter view exposes the sole fitted turret; there is
no hidden mate, second ring, dorsal mount, ventral mount, gun-like sensor, or
ammunition aperture.

The flank station gives the one weapon useful port, forward, dorsal, and much
of the aft coverage while accepting unavoidable ventral and far-starboard
blind regions. Myrmidon must not gain invented mounts to imply complete
spherical fire. Conversely, this singleton's one-mount layout is not a model
for larger combatants: ships with roughly eight or more hardpoints require the
guide's distributed dorsal, ventral, flank, forward, and aft station maps.

## Drives and absent systems

Four large recessed maneuver-field apertures form two vertical pairs around
two smaller separate ribbed power-service grilles at the broad stern. All four
apertures and both grilles are visible in the raised front-port view. They are
not missile cells or cargo openings. There is no Jump ring, Jump radiator,
coil housing, fuel scoop, processor grille, external tank, flame, or drive
glow.

## Redoubt recognition finish

- Main carapace and shoulder armor: fire red `#B83B2E`
- Lower and selected edge panels: orange `#E9782E`
- Inset service and accommodation panels: bone `#D8CCA9`
- Interlocking armor bands, prow guards, and component collars: heavy
  stainless with restrained chrome
- Recesses, grilles, landing details, and turret mechanics: gunmetal
- Glazing and beam-laser optic: very dark teal with a restrained cyan highlight

This is the same shipyard vocabulary used by Bellerophon, not the same hull.
Myrmidon keeps its larger beetle pressure body, cabins, airlock, cargo shutter,
four-point carapace, flank beam mount, and patrol endurance. Saturated enamel
and bright metal should read as rugged 1970s/1980s military-industrial future,
not gray contemporary stealth styling or decorative neon.

## Production sequence and provenance

1. Generate the neutral beetle chassis with fixed bridge, accommodation,
   access, cargo, armor, hardpoint, and machinery coordinates.
2. Repartition only the control brow from six panes to exactly five.
3. Save that corrected result as the family anchor.
4. Derive Myrmidon from the anchor using Bellerophon only as the Redoubt finish
   reference; install the one beam-laser cup in the existing flank ring.
5. Verify the one-visible/zero-hidden weapon map and all fixed coordinates at
   full resolution before web conversion.
