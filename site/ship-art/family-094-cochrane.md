# Cochrane Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production singleton family 094*

## Mechanical identity

- Record: `ship-94` Cochrane; Marque Marine (P5)
- Hull: 1,000 displacement tons; standard; 82.0 × 31.0 × 22.0 m
- Fit: TL11, eight armor points, reinforced structure, armored drives, Jump K /
  Jump-2, P/P thrust 4, four-week endurance, hardened holographic bridge, and
  dual Model 3 bis/fib computers
- Complement: seventy crew including thirty marines; thirty-place barracks,
  two armories, two briefing rooms, four-bed medical bay, six emergency berths,
  gymnasium, workshop, probe and repair drones, ATV hangar, and 48 escape pods
- Craft: one Charon and one Proteus Surveyor in one 60-ton full hangar
- Weapons: two particle-beam bays; four triple-beam, two triple-missile, and two
  triple-sandcaster turrets; ten point-defense lasers; no unused hardpoint

Cochrane is an independent boarding-bastion family, not a stretched Marque or
Hawkwood clipper. Its tall troop citadel, deep weapon cheeks, protected waist,
and broad craft shoulder establish a separate Marque assault hull.

## Canonical architecture and support fit

The filled 82 × 31 × 22 m standard hull has a broad armored seven-pane command
prow, paired forward weapon cheeks, recessed boarding waist, tall troop/mission
citadel, asymmetric port craft-and-vehicle shoulder, and deep thrust-four stern.
It is neither streamlined nor a freighter, truss, tank train, or module carrier.

One closed 14 × 8 m hangar shutter opens into a branched 31 m × 16 m internal
bay for the approved 26 × 9.5 × 5.4 m Proteus Surveyor and 12.5 × 5.2 × 3.8 m
Charon. Both remain internal. A separate 4.5 × 3.2 m ATV door stays closed.
Three of six stateroom windows show portside. Roughly half the 48 small flush
escape-pod hatches are readable on the near side; two port hatches are occluded
by the hangar shoulder and all starboard partners are hidden. These rescue
panels, barracks access, medical, armory, briefing, workshop, drone, airlock,
and docking fittings are nonweapon systems.

## Ten-coordinate weapon map

Five large stations are visible and five hidden:

1. Lower-forward port cheek: one architectural 51-ton particle-beam bay with a
   deep insulated blue-white emitter throat.
2. Forward upper-port shoulder: triple-beam turret with three cyan optics.
3. Midship port flank: second triple-beam turret with three cyan optics.
4. Lower-port/ventral mid-aft: triple-missile turret with three closed shutters.
5. Aft upper-port shoulder: triple-sandcaster turret with three perforated blunt
   projector heads.

The hidden half repeats one bay, two beam turrets, one missile turret, and one
sandcaster. This provides overlapping fore, aft, upper, lower, and flank fields
without a dorsal gun row. Ten PD nodes split five visible/five hidden; each is a
tiny single-optic gimbal, well below turret scale. The command-side circular
fitting is a docking collar, not another turret.

Five processors use three visible/two hidden ribbed grilles plus a ventral
scoop. Internal Jump-K machinery reads as two transverse service bands and four
flush coil housings, never an external ring or glow. Two visible/two hidden
maneuver apertures and separate power grilles mark the stern. Eight armor points
and reinforcement read through doubled datum ribs, deep recesses, armored drive
bulkheads, and overlapping serviceable plates.

## Marque finish and plate

Use emerald `#13705B`, cream `#EFE0B5`, burgundy `#8D2E3C`, gold-toned chrome,
bright steel, and gunmetal. The ship is bright, legitimate, maintained, and
dangerous rather than gray or camouflaged. Use the standard complete front-port
three-quarter view, restrained near-orthographic perspective, charcoal stars,
faint teal grid, and original late-1970s/early-1980s gouache-and-airbrush
technical-art finish. No text, logo, watermark, open door, external craft,
weapon fire, planet, modern CGI, wing, tail, or Jump ring.

## Production sequence and provenance

Generated on 2026-08-21 with OpenAI image generation in built-in mode. The
neutral anchor was accepted. The production pass established the bay and hull
finish but added one extra flank weapon and miscounted several turret faces.
Scoped single-object edits reduced the extra mount to PD scale and restored the
beam, missile, and sand systems to triple mounts.
