# Proteus Family Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 020*

## Mechanical identity

- Family: `family-20`, Proteus
- Members: `ship-20`, `ship-21`, `ship-24`, `ship-159`, `ship-188`, and
  `ship-209`
- Hull: 50 displacement tons / approximately 700 m³
- Shared drives: `sK` maneuver and power
- Shared central architecture: interchangeable thirty-ton / approximately
  420 m³ module cradle between control and drive blocks
- Streamlined members: ships 20, 21, 24, 159, and 188
- Standard-hull member: ship 209, the unfaired modular chassis
- Shared limitation: no Jump drive; all are unarmed

The module interface is the family identity. The forward control block, four
longitudinal clamp rails, module end frames, aft drive block, landing sockets,
and primary load-path seams remain aligned across every fit. The standard
Proteus Modular exposes those elements; streamlined members add one common
aerodynamic shoulder/belly fairing around them.

## Canonical family architecture

The unfaired chassis is 25.5 m long, 8.4 m wide, and 5.0 m high. Its forward
control wedge is 5.5 m long, the central module bay is 14.0 m long by 6.0 m wide
by 5.0 m high, and the aft drive block is 6.0 m long. The module's rectangular
pressure shell encloses approximately 420 m³. The remaining control, drive,
fuel, structure, and service volumes plausibly complete the 700 m³ craft.

Streamlined fits add common rounded shoulders and a shallow lifting belly,
producing a 26.0 × 9.5 × 5.4 m aerodynamic envelope. The fairing has one
fixed port personnel-hatch boundary, one continuous module-side aperture grid,
and paired aft field apertures. Ship 209 removes the fairing but retains every
chassis coordinate and the same sealed module shell.

The two-person streamlined control block has a low five-pane canopy; the
standard TL9 chassis has a smaller three-pane control cab in the same forward
volume. Four bright clamp rails run along the module corners. Two circular aft
field housings flank one ribbed power grille. Six recessed landing pads align
with the forward frame, module end frames, and drive block.

### Chassis invariants

All variants preserve:

- forward control-block, central module, and aft drive-block lengths;
- the 14.0 × 6.0 × 5.0 m module pressure shell and both end frames;
- four longitudinal clamp rails and six landing-pad locations;
- paired aft field housings, central power grille, and drive-block seams;
- module aperture grid, camera, attitude, backdrop, perspective, and lighting;
- no weapon mount, barrel, external tank, Jump hardware, or extra module.

The five streamlined fits additionally preserve the exact 26.0 × 9.5 ×
5.4 m fairing silhouette, five-pane cockpit, port hatch boundary, shoulder and
belly seams, and module-side aperture boundary.

## Variant deltas

### `ship-20` Proteus Cargo

- Path 1, Concord Exchange; streamlined; TL11; two pilots; one airlock
- One-week endurance, two armor points, 34.55 tons cargo
- Module: almost entirely freight volume; one broad two-leaf cargo door fills
  the visible module side, with no passenger windows
- Finish: ivory `#F1E3C2`, vermilion `#E44B2D`, royal blue `#2354A3`, chrome

### `ship-21` Proteus Passenger

- Path 1, Concord Exchange; streamlined; TL11; two pilots; one airlock
- Twenty-four seats, fuel processor, 22.05 tons cargo
- Module: six paired narrow passenger-window groups above a lower cargo/service
  door; processor grille recessed under the forward module frame
- Finish: same Concord palette, with a longer ivory occupancy band

### `ship-24` Proteus Prospector

- Path 3, Outer Reach; streamlined; TL11; two pilots; one airlock
- Two workshops, mining drones, 16.55 tons cargo
- Module: two rugged workshop doors, one closed drone-bay shutter, work lights,
  and exposed service conduits within the module aperture boundary
- Finish: avocado `#7A9238`, ochre `#C9902C`, brick red `#A63F2F`, brushed
  aluminum; no invented grapple or weapon

### `ship-159` Proteus Mercy

- Path 4, Civic Survey; streamlined; TL11; two pilots; two airlocks
- Two-week endurance; two armor points; nineteen-bed ward, two operating
  theaters, four emergency low berths; 1.25 tons stores
- Module: clean sealed clinical body, the shared forward airlock plus one module
  pressure hatch, seven small ward windows, four low-berth service panels,
  and protected medical utility ports
- Finish: warm white `#F4F0D8`, cyan `#25A9B8`, lime `#A8C83E`, clean chrome

### `ship-188` Proteus Surveyor

- Path 4, Civic Survey; streamlined; TL11; two pilots; one airlock
- Two-week endurance; five compact staterooms, laboratory, ten-person galley
  and recreation, one air/raft hangar, 6.75 tons cargo
- Module: five paired cabin windows, circular instrument collar, fine antenna
  grid, and one closed air/raft hangar door with human/vehicle scale
- Finish: Civic Survey white/cyan/lime/chrome; precise rather than militarized

### `ship-209` Proteus Modular

- Path 1, Concord Exchange; standard hull; TL9; one pilot plus one passenger;
  no armor and no airlock
- One-week endurance; sealed empty thirty-ton module berth; 1.3 tons external
  stores; one unused fire-control station but no weapon
- Remove only the common aerodynamic fairing. Expose the invariant four clamp
  rails, end frames, service umbilicals, landing pads, module shell, and drive
  block. Use a smaller three-pane control cab and blank module sides.
- Finish: restrained early Concord ivory, vermilion, royal blue, polished
  aluminum; modular industrial clarity, not a cargo fit

## Production prompts

### Neutral unfaired chassis anchor

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Create the original neutral Proteus family chassis,
a 50-displacement-ton modular cutter architecture, exactly 25.5 m long by 8.4 m
wide by 5.0 m high before aerodynamic fairing. Arrange three axial volumes: a
5.5 m forward control wedge, a sealed rectangular central module pressure shell
exactly 14.0 m long by 6.0 m wide by 5.0 m high, and a 6.0 m aft drive block.
The module shell plausibly encloses 420 m³. Put a neutral five-pane control-cab
socket forward, four bright-metal-ready longitudinal clamp rails on the module
corners, strong end frames at both module ends, service umbilicals inside the
frames, six recessed landing pads aligned to the frames, two circular aft field
housings, and one central ribbed power grille. Keep the module side blank except
for a precise rectangular aperture grid and one closed personnel-hatch boundary.
Warm unpainted aluminum, original 1970s/1980s gouache-and-airbrush technical
plate, complete front-port three-quarter, 12 degrees above, restrained near-
orthographic, 3:2, charcoal sparse starfield, faint teal plotting grid. No outer
aerodynamic fairing, paint, text, logo, weapon, cargo contents, extra module,
external tank, Jump hardware, exposed landing strut, other craft, flame, motion
blur, modern gray styling, or franchise resemblance.
```

### Shared streamlined edit rule

```text
Edit the approved Proteus chassis into a streamlined fit. Preserve exactly the
forward, module, and drive-block lengths; 14.0 x 6.0 x 5.0 m module shell; both
module end frames; four clamp-rail coordinates; six landing-pad locations;
paired aft field housings; central power grille; load-path seams; camera, crop,
perspective, backdrop, and lighting. Add the common rounded shoulder and shallow
lifting-belly fairing to make an exact 26.0 x 9.5 x 5.4 m envelope. Use the same
five-pane cockpit, one fixed port hatch boundary, common shoulder/belly seams,
and one module-side aperture boundary in every streamlined fit. Change only the
listed module doors, windows, service features, path trim, and livery. Original
1970s/1980s gouache-and-airbrush catalog art. No weapon, extra hull, second
module, external tank, Jump hardware, separate wing, exposed gear, other craft,
text, logo, watermark, flame, motion blur, redesign, or franchise resemblance.
```

### Cargo fit

```text

and a standardized chrome cargo-door frame. Keep the craft entirely unarmed.
```
