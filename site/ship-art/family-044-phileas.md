# Phileas Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 044*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-44`; `ship-44` Phileas
- Shipyard path: Path 2, Venture Passage Works
- Hull: 100 displacement tons / approximately 1,400 m³
- Dimensions: 41 m long, 11 m wide, and 7.5 m high
- Configuration: streamlined; TL11; non-standard; four armor points
- Drives: Jump-A / Jump-2, one Jump; maneuver C and power C / thrust 6
- Control: hardened standard bridge, basic military electronics, Model/3,
  Jump Control/2, Fire Control/1, Point Defense/2, and four crew
- Service: three staterooms, one emergency low berth, one fuel processor, one
  additional airlock, two weeks endurance, and 14 tons priority cargo
- Armament: one mixed triple turret with missile rack, sandcaster, and beam
  laser; one independent point-defense laser
- Ammunition: twelve standard missiles and twenty sandcaster canisters

Phileas is a fast armored dispatch, mail, intelligence, and priority-cargo
courier. It trades Pym's bulk hold for six-gravity acceleration, processing,
additional access, point defense, and a third cabin.

## Independent armored spearcoach

The 41 × 11 × 7.5 m hull is a long narrow “armored spearcoach”: sharply
clipped spear prow, deep forward command block, long occupied center body with
recessed waist, twin thick lower chines, strong swept shoulder rails, and short
narrow thrust stern. The partly tapered envelope plausibly encloses 1,400 m³.

Equal tonnage, path, role, and mixed armament do not make Phileas a Poe/Pym
variant. Never substitute Pym's 31 × 14 m broad lozenge, high five-pane bridge,
full-width waist, dorsal turret, four-bay strip, broad cargo door, paired
three-slat Jump bulges, or drive shoulders. Pony Express geometry is likewise
independent.

Exactly four wide panes form the deeply recessed hardened bridge. Exactly three
small stateroom ports line the visible port waist. Two separate closed airlock
stations—forward inspection/crew and mid-aft service/rescue—have heavy collars
and handholds. A small closed emergency-berth panel lies between them. One
separate closed 5 × 2.8 m two-leaf shutter serves the 14-ton priority hold.

## Weapon and service map

One 3–4 m ring on the mid upper port shoulder, off centerline and canted
outward, carries one mixed triple cup with exactly three unlike apertures: one
closed missile shutter, one blunt multi-port sandcaster mouth, and one narrow
dark-teal beam optic. One separate 0.5 m lower-aft-port-chine node carries one
barrel-free point-defense laser lens.

The plate shows one of one mixed turret and one of one point-defense node; zero
weapon stations are hidden. The lower defense node complements the turret's
upper/flank arcs. Never add a second cup or lens, expose ordnance, or mistake
the processor coupling and Jump collars for weapons.

One square ribbed processor grille and adjacent flush refueling coupling lie
on the lower port keel. There is no scoop. Jump-A / Jump-2 uses two inset
diagonal slatted heat-rejection veins and two recessed circular service
collars—not Pym's longitudinal bulges and never an external glowing ring.
Two visible stern maneuver apertures flank one smaller power grille; their
starboard counterparts are occluded. Chrome shoulder/chine rails and layered
plates express four armor points.

Pym supplies finish and mixed-aperture vocabulary only. The long spearcoach
geometry, side stations, access map, and diagonal Jump veins remain Phileas.
