# Pony Express Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 198*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-198`; `ship-198` Pony Express
- Hull: 100 displacement tons / approximately 1,400 m³; 38 × 13.5 × 8.5 m
- Configuration: streamlined; TL9 standard design; two armor points
- Drives: Jump-A / Jump-2, one Jump, B/B maneuver and power; four weeks
- Service: three crew, four staterooms, one emergency low berth, 16 tons cargo
- Fuel handling: two processors and **no fuel scoop**
- Armament: none; one unused hardpoint; no ammunition

## Independent Venture courier

Pony Express is a forward-driving clipped lozenge with a five-pane protected
bridge brow, compact priority-cargo cell, four-window cabin group, smooth keel,
and strong four-aperture stern. The closed two-leaf port shutter and chrome
frame identify the 16-ton mail/priority hold. The emergency-berth panel,
boarding hatch, sensor cheeks, and landing doors remain closed and separate.

One dorsal 3–4 m ring behind the bridge stays under a smooth flush cover. Never
add a turret, emitter, launcher, PD node, or decorative gun. With one hardpoint,
distributed battery mapping does not apply, but the sealed coordinate is fixed.

## Drives, fuel, and finish

Jump-A uses a compact segmented radiator and paired field housings. Four
maneuver apertures and two power grilles identify B/B performance. Exactly two
processor banks are visible. Despite its streamlined hull, Pony Express has no
scoop: the lower keel stays smooth except for a refuelling coupling and landing
seams; never add an intake or ventral mouth.

Venture Passage Works uses sunflower `#F2C230`, cobalt `#174A8B`, signal
orange `#E96D2F`, and bright chrome. Saturated command and shoulder yellow,
strong blue occupancy/cargo bands, orange service boundaries, and protected
chrome rails make expensive frontier dispatch service legible without weapons.

## Invariants

Preserve the 38 × 13.5 × 8.5 m lozenge; five bridge panes; four cabin windows;
closed priority-cargo shutter, emergency panel, and boarding hatch; covered
hardpoint; smooth no-scoop keel; two processors; Jump hardware; four maneuver
apertures; two power grilles; seams; camera; crop; backdrop; and lighting.

Never install a weapon or scoop, open a door, expose cargo, add external craft
or tank, wing, fin, glowing Jump ring, flame, text, logo, or watermark.
