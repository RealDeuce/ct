# Hannibal Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production singleton family 113*

## Mechanical identity

- Record: `ship-113` Hannibal; Admiralty Line (P7)
- Hull: 2,500 displacement tons / approximately 35,000 m³; streamlined
- Dimensions: 122.0 × 50.0 × 32.0 m
- Fit: TL11, armor four, Jump T / Jump-2, Z/Z thrust 4, three weeks endurance,
  advanced electronics, command/hardened holographic bridge, improved
  magazine, and dual Model 3 bis/fib computers
- Complement: 149 crew, twenty-three staterooms, fifteen crew berthings,
  200-place barracks, twenty armories, four briefing rooms, twenty emergency
  berths, workshop, four offices, four-place brig, probe and repair drones,
  five ATV hangars, twenty-bed medical ward, and 4 tons cargo
- Craft: ten Icarus II fighters in a 100-ton hangar and two flight decks; two
  Goliath assault transports plus one Jason in a separate 210-ton hangar
- Weapons: thirteen triple-beam, five triple-missile, five triple-sandcaster
  turrets, two particle-beam bays, and twenty-five point-defense lasers

Hannibal is an independent assault carrier, not a Lilienthal, Enyo, or enlarged
Togo. Its broad streamlined twin-deck-whale body, buried visor, low command
island, paired recessed launch trenches, deep assault belly, and wide integrated
stern form a distinct Admiralty carrier hull.

## Canonical architecture

The 122 × 50 × 32 m wingless lifting body is one continuous atmospheric
envelope with a blunt rounded wedge prow, ten deck levels, thick occupied
shoulders, broad central pressure hull, tapered afterbody, and no open
catamaran gap. Two upper launch trenches each contain five closed fighter cells
and internal service lanes. All ten 11.8 × 5.8 × 2.9 m Icarus IIs remain
internal.

One closed 18 × 9 m lower-port shutter serves the separate 210-ton bay for two
30 × 14.5 × 7 m Goliaths and one 21 × 7.6 × 4.1 m Jason. Five ATV doors,
cargo shutter, docking collar, twelve of twenty-three stateroom windows, and
ten of twenty emergency-berth panels remain closed and unarmed. Barracks and
crew berthing are windowless.

## Twenty-five-coordinate coverage map

Thirteen hardpoints are visible and twelve hidden. The visible lower-forward
port-chine particle bay has one hidden starboard partner. Twelve of twenty-
three turrets show:

- seven beam triples: one centerline forward crown and six distributed
  forward, midship, aft, upper, flank, and lower port stations;
- two missile triples: forward-lower and aft-upper port stations; and
- three sandcaster triples: midship-lower, aft-lower/ventral, and aft-center
  stations.

The hidden eleven are six beam, three missile, and two sandcaster turrets. This
asymmetry accounts for the odd totals through centerline placements. Visible
mounts surround but never occupy flight lanes and span all principal arcs
rather than forming a dorsal row. Twenty-five PD coordinates split thirteen
visible/twelve hidden.

Three flat crown disks are unarmed Jump/service faces. Flight cells, hangars,
doors, docking, sensors, and machinery are also unarmed. The particle bay is
hull architecture and materially larger than every turret.

## Machinery, armor, finish, and plate

Internal Jump-T machinery uses a segmented service/radiator band and eight
flush coil housings, never an external ring or glow. Four visible/four hidden
maneuver apertures and separate power grilles form the Z/Z thrust-4 stern.
Armor four and protected command systems read through the buried visor,
self-contained flight cells, recessed weapons, redundant ribs, and bright
load paths without destroying the streamlined envelope.

Admiralty finish uses navy `#203B69`, warm white `#E8E5D4`, signal red
`#C43C35`, bright aluminum/chrome, and gunmetal. Use the complete front-port
near-orthographic view, charcoal stars, faint teal grid, and original late-
1970s/early-1980s gouache-and-airbrush technical art. No text, logo, watermark,
open cell/door, external craft, weapon fire, planet, modern CGI, wing, tail,
truss, or Jump ring.

## Production sequence and provenance

Generated on 2026-08-21 with OpenAI image generation in built-in mode. The
neutral anchor established the carrier envelope and two internal flight groups.
The production pass exposed fifteen turret housings; two scoped edits converted
three surplus aft/centerline crown mounts to flat unarmed service disks, leaving
exactly twelve visible turrets and eleven hidden.
