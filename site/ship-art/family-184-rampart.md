# Rampart Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 184*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-184`; `ship-184` Rampart
- Hull: 400 displacement tons / approximately 5,600 m³
- Dimensions: 58 m long, 24 m wide, and 15.5 m high
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11
- Protection: ten crystaliron armor points, radiation shielding, and armored
  bulkheads around bridge, drives, and magazines
- Drives: no Jump drive; maneuver M and power M / thrust 6
- Endurance: eight weeks of power-plant fuel
- Control: advanced electronics, hardened holographic bridge, and fib/bis
  Model/3 computer
- Mission support: 31 crew, armory, two brigs, troop/boarding support, two-bed
  medical bay, workshop, repair drones, and 7.25 tons cargo/evidence
- Boat facilities: one 30-ton full hangar carrying one Grapnel (`ship-211`)
- Armament: one triple-beam turret, one mixed missile/missile/sand triple
  turret, two particle-beam barbettes, and four laser PD nodes
- Ammunition: 144 missiles and 60 sand canisters
- Fuel handling: eight processors and no scoop

## Canonical citadel

Rampart occupies a 58 × 24 × 15.5 m compact streamlined Redoubt citadel.
A clipped armored prow, broad faceted shoulders, short protected load paths,
deep mission block, thick fuel belly, recessed flank systems, and oversized
stern produce an atmospheric-capable lifting body without wings, tail, or
aircraft styling.

Ten-point armor uses deep overlapping plate edges, thick shoulders, protected
recesses, a radiation seam band, and visibly armored command/drive/magazine
boundaries. The hardened bridge has exactly six narrow panes beneath a heavy
brow. Paired cheek sensors and one circular glazed prow collar occupy protected
sockets; the collar is explicitly a sensor, not a second turret ring.

## Weapon map and coverage

Two particle-beam barbettes form one bilateral pair. The plate exposes one long
7–9 m port-flank channel with a recessed insulated emitter; its starboard twin
is hidden. It remains substantially larger than a turret and carries no long
conventional barrel.

Two triple turrets occupy complementary stations:

- the visible outward-canted port-shoulder cup carries exactly three narrow
  beam emitters and covers forward, dorsal, port, and aft approaches;
- the hidden ventral/starboard/aft cup carries two closed missile racks and one
  sandcaster, covering the opposite and lower hemisphere.

This division puts the visible trainable mount on the shoulder rather than the
crown and gives the hidden mixed turret responsibility for lower/starboard/aft
blind zones. Do not expose both turrets in the standard view or turn the prow
sensor collar into another gun.

Four PD lasers form two complementary pairs. The plate shows one tiny forward-
port lens and one tiny aft-port/ventral-lip lens; their partners are occluded.
All remain much smaller than the turret cup.

## Grapnel hangar and boarding fit

One closed aft-port full-hangar shutter provides at least 21 m internal axis,
10 m beam, and 6 m height for the canonical 18.6 m Grapnel. The 30-ton armored
boarding boat remains inside. A separate closed 7.25-ton cargo/evidence hatch,
two boarding airlocks, handholds, recessed grapples, medical access, and repair
interfaces remain unarmed and distinct from the boat bay.

## Drives, endurance, and fuel

Six huge recessed maneuver apertures and four dark power grilles identify the
M/M six-gravity stern. Broad flush belly-tank service boundaries account for
eight weeks of internal power fuel without external tanks. Exactly eight small
processor grilles form one protected bank.

Rampart has no scoop and no Jump drive. Never add a lower intake, Jump radiator,
coil housing, ring, or glow. Strategic dependence is a required recognition
feature, not an omission to be repaired by generic starship detail.

## Redoubt finish

Redoubt Shipbuilding uses fire red `#B83B2E`, orange `#E9782E`, bone
`#D8CCA9`, heavy stainless, and restrained gunmetal. Fire red carries the armor
fields; orange marks shoulders, drives, and hazards; bone identifies command
and mission inserts; stainless protects edges, collars, and door frames;
gunmetal fills deep machinery recesses. The result is a visible promise of
local survivability, not gray camouflage.

hangar; two boarding airlocks; eight processor grilles; belly-fuel boundaries;

## Catalog plate and reusable prompts

Use a complete front-port three-quarter view about 12 degrees above centerline,
restrained near-orthographic perspective, 3:2 landscape, warm upper-front-port
key, weak cool aft rim, charcoal sparse starfield, and faint teal plotting grid.
Render original late-1970s/early-1980s gouache-and-airbrush technical art with
bright opaque enamel, heavy stainless, and gunmetal. No planet, motion blur,
modern gray rendering, or recognizable franchise design.
