# Warden Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 041*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-41`; `ship-41` Warden
- Shipyard path: Path 4, Civic Survey Works
- Hull: 100 displacement tons / approximately 1,400 m³
- Dimensions: 38 m long, 13.5 m wide, and 8.2 m high
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11; non-standard design
- Protection: four whole points of crystaliron armor
- Drives: maneuver C and power C / thrust 6; no Jump drive
- Endurance: two weeks of power-plant fuel
- Control and complement: standard bridge, basic military electronics,
  Model/3 computer, and eight crew
- Accommodation and custody: four staterooms, four low berths, one emergency
  low berth, two brig cells, and a one-bed medical bay
- Mission support: two fuel processors, 13 tons cargo/evidence stores, and one
  standard 20-ton hangar carrying Caduceus (`ship-7`)
- Armament: one triple turret with three beam lasers; no ammunition

Warden is a fast in-system customs cutter. Its armor, six-gravity performance,
protected launch, brig, medical support, and evidence hold let it intercept,
inspect, board, detain, and assist traffic. It cannot Jump and depends on a
local base or carrier for interstellar deployment.

## Canonical customs-shield hull

The 38 × 13.5 × 8.2 m streamlined pressure body is a compact “customs shield”:
blunt clipped inspection prow, broad filled bilateral shoulders, low raised
command spine, deep armored keel, subtly cinched working waist, and short
squared engineering stern. A long internal launch cell occupies the port
shoulder while a balanced starboard mission shoulder preserves calm public-
service massing. The shoulders are occupied hull rather than wings or pods.
The tapered envelope plausibly encloses approximately 1,400 m³.

Exactly five dark panes form the protected command brow. Exactly two small
crew-stateroom ports are visible on the port command flank; their starboard
partners are occluded. One closed inspection docking hatch with handholds and
a bright instrument collar lies forward and low. Brig spaces have no windows.
Two small adjacent closed panels serve low-berth and medical systems; they are
plain and unlabelled, with no cross or other medical emblem.

Four armor points read through two interlocking protective bands, deep hangar
frames, protected component reveals, and overlapping accessible plates. Calm
sensor blisters, ceramic radome forms, landing doors, bumpers, tie-downs,
handholds, and maintenance panels establish the operational scale.

## Caduceus launch cell and cargo

One closed segmented hangar shutter dominates the mid-to-aft port shoulder.
Its clear external boundary is approximately 7.2 m wide × 5.2 m high, with a
reinforced sill, recovery fittings, and at least a 19 m internal longitudinal
axis. That clearance is derived from the canonical 17.5 × 6.4 × 4.6 m
Caduceus family envelope and the standard-hangar allowance. The launch remains
entirely internal and invisible.

One separate closed 4 × 2.5 m rectangular shutter on the lower aft-port keel
serves the 13-ton evidence/stores hold. It is clearly smaller than the hangar
and cannot be mistaken for a second launch cell. Never expose the Caduceus,
duplicate the hangar, enlarge the cargo door to launch scale, or open either
boundary in a recognition plate.

## Weapon, fuel, and drive map

The sole 3–4 m turret ring occupies the forward upper port shoulder, off the
dorsal centerline, canted outward, and clear of the hangar. One shared low cup
carries exactly three short narrow parallel beam-laser optics. The standard
view exposes this one of one turret; there is no hidden mate, dorsal row,
ventral weapon, point-defense node, or gun-like sensor.

Two fuel processors form a bilateral pair. The plate shows one square ribbed
port-belly processor grille and adjacent flush refueling coupling; the equal
starboard processor is occluded. Warden has no fuel scoop. Four stern maneuver-
field apertures form port/starboard vertical pairs: two port apertures are
visible and two starboard apertures hidden. Smaller separate ribbed grilles
serve the C/C power plant. There is no Jump radiator, coil housing, ring,
external tank, flame, or glow.

## Civic Survey recognition finish

- Principal symmetric pressure hull: warm white `#F4F0D8`
- Command surround, launch shoulder and shutter, cargo frame, stern panels:
  saturated cyan `#25A9B8`
- Processor, medical-service inserts, recovery and status details, turret
  collar: lime `#A8C83E`
- Armor bands, docking, hangar sill, radomes, sensors, processor and drive
  interfaces: polished chrome/aluminum
- Glazing, recesses, and grilles: dark teal/gunmetal

Purposeful sensors, clean docking interfaces, white ceramic forms, and bright
accountable color establish Civic Survey lineage. Galileo is a finish reference
only; Warden must retain its independent streamlined customs-shield geometry,
single Caduceus cell, no-Jump machinery, and one-turret layout.
