# Pullman Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 197*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-197`; `ship-197` Pullman
- Hull: 300 displacement tons / approximately 4,200 m³; 72 × 19 × 13.5 m
- Configuration: standard; TL9 standard design; two armor points
- Drives: Jump-B / Jump-1, one Jump, B/B maneuver and power; four weeks
- Control: basic civilian electronics and Model/2 computer
- Service: 35 staterooms, 20 low berths, seven crew, and 46 tons cargo
- Fuel handling: one scoop and two processors
- Armament: none; three unused hardpoints; no ammunition

## Independent Concord liner

Pullman is a long non-aerodynamic two-deck scheduled liner with a rounded
six-pane command prow, repeated framed occupancy bays, shallow keel, and compact
stern. The repeated upper/lower glazing is an exterior occupancy index, not a
literal window-per-stateroom deck plan. Grouped five-position low-berth service
panels remain distinct from passenger glazing. Two boarding hatches and the
closed aft 46-ton freight shutter use standardized bright-metal collars.

## Empty hardpoints

Three hardpoint coordinates remain unused. The standard front-port view shows
one smooth covered forward port/upper-shoulder ring; the starboard-upper and
ventral/aft covers are occluded. Never add a turret, emitter, launcher, PD node,
or decorative gun. The three-station map is below the large-battery threshold,
but reserves complementary upper, far-side, and lower/aft arcs for later fits.

## Drives and finish

Jump-B uses a compact segmented radiator and paired field housings. One lower
scoop, two processor banks, two maneuver apertures, and two power grilles mark
the modest B/B machinery. Concord warm ivory `#F1E3C2`, royal blue `#2354A3`,
vermilion `#E44B2D`, and polished chrome make scheduled passenger service bright
and maintained rather than militarized.

## Invariants

Preserve the 72 × 19 × 13.5 m silhouette; six bridge panes; repeated two-deck
occupancy bays; low-berth panels; two boarding hatches; freight shutter; one
visible/two hidden covered hardpoints; scoop; two processors; Jump and B/B
machinery; seams; camera; crop; backdrop; and lighting. Never arm the ship,
open a door, expose passengers or cargo, add external craft or tank, wings,
fins, glow, flame, text, logo, or watermark.
