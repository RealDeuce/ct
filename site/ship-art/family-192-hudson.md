# Hudson Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 192*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-192`; `ship-192` Hudson
- Hull: 200 displacement tons / approximately 2,800 m³
- Dimensions: 48 m long, 19 m wide, and 11.5 m high
- Configuration: standard; technology level 9; standard design
- Protection: two armor points from one titanium-steel layer
- Drives: Jump-B / Jump-2, one Jump, maneuver A, and power B / thrust 1
- Endurance: four weeks of power-plant fuel
- Control: basic civilian electronics and Model/2 computer
- Accommodation: ten staterooms, twenty low berths, and three crew
- Trade capacity: 53 tons cargo; no external load
- Fuel handling: one scoop and two processors
- Armament: none; two unused fire-control stations and no ammunition

## Independent Concord route packet

Hudson is a 48 × 19 × 11.5 m non-aerodynamic standard hull. A rounded clipped
rectangular command prow, long straight-sided centerbody, shallow structural
keel, and neatly faired aft drive block form a compact scheduled-route packet.
The bridge has exactly six narrow panes. Flush cheek sensors, standardized door
spacing, a simple boarding hatch, and closed landing-door seams support rapid
port turnaround without military detailing.

One long upper port occupancy band serves the ten staterooms. Its repeated
glazing is a legible occupancy index rather than a literal one-window-per-room
diagram. Four lower flush service banks each represent five low berths; they
are maintenance access panels, not passenger windows, and no berth or body is
visible.

The broad closed two-leaf freight shutter and rectangular chrome collar mark
the 53-ton hold. A smaller closed high-value mail/service hatch sits aft. The
freight door, mail hatch, occupancy band, and low-berth banks remain distinct
so mixed passenger, mail, and speculative-cargo service reads immediately.

## Empty hardpoints

Hudson has two hardpoints and two unused fire-control stations, but no installed
weapon. The standard view shows one forward port/upper-shoulder 3–4 m ring
under a smooth flush armored cover. Its complementary ventral/starboard ring is
occluded. Neither cover may acquire a turret cup, emitter, missile shutter,
sandcaster, or PD lens. Service ports, scoop details, and sensors are also
barrel-free.

This two-station fit does not require the broad arc distribution used by ships
with large batteries. If Hudson is ever armed as a later variant, the two fixed
coordinates remain complementary rather than migrating both to the crown.

## Drives and fuel recognition

Jump-B uses one compact segmented non-glowing upper-aft radiator and paired
field-coil/service housings. Two modest maneuver apertures and two orderly
power grilles identify the low-output A/B stern. The lower forward keel carries
one broad flush scoop field, while exactly two protected processor grille banks
sit in separate service positions. The 48-ton fuel allocation remains internal;
there is no exposed tank or glowing Jump ring.

## Concord finish

Concord Exchange uses warm ivory `#F1E3C2`, royal blue `#2354A3`, vermilion
`#E44B2D`, and polished aluminum/chrome. Ivory dominates; the long blue band
organizes passenger and service occupancy; vermilion edges the freight, mail,
low-berth, scoop, and drive interfaces; chrome protects collars and leading
edges. The result is bright, prosperous, maintained scheduled transport in the
period-art tradition, not a gray tramp freighter.
