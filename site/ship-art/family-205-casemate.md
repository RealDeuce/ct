# Casemate Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 205*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-205`; `ship-205` Casemate
- Shipyard path: Path 8, Redoubt Shipbuilding
- Hull: 400 displacement tons / approximately 5,600 m³
- Dimensions: 61 m long, 25 m wide, and 15 m high
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 9; standard design
- Protection: eight titanium-steel armor points and fib Model/2 computer
- Drives: no Jump drive; maneuver M and power M / thrust 6
- Endurance: four weeks of power-plant fuel
- Mission support: 19 crew, ten staterooms, five emergency low berths, armory,
  three fuel processors, ten marines, and 107 tons cargo
- Armament: two triple-beam turrets and two triple-missile turrets
- Ammunition: 360 smart missiles
- Fuel handling: surface collection and three processors; no installed scoop

Casemate is an independent local-defense boat, not a Rampart derivative.
Rampart is a specialized mission citadel with barbettes and a boat hangar;
Casemate is a simpler TL9 mobile bunker whose large protected hold supports
patrol stores, rescue loads, prizes, and boarding operations.

## Canonical mobile bunker

The 61 × 25 × 15 m hull is a low, broad, wingless lifting body with one
clipped faceted prow, thick horizontal shoulder slabs, a deep central
freight/mission block, chamfered belly, short waist, and very broad blunt
stern. Four overlapping titanium-steel layers, accessible panel divisions,
and protected recesses communicate eight-point armor without modern stealth
camouflage.

A recessed six-pane bridge brow sits below the forward armor terrace. Five
small protected port cabin panes represent approximately half of the ten
staterooms. One closed personnel/boarding hatch, one emergency-berth/armory
service panel, and one enormous closed 12 × 5 m port cargo shutter expose the
boat's human and freight scale. The hold remains internal; the shutter is not
a hangar and no carried craft may appear.

Exactly six large recessed maneuver-field apertures identify the M-drive's
six-gravity stern. Three visible ribbed power-service grilles and one hidden
partner identify the matching power plant. Three separate processor grilles
support collected unrefined fuel. Casemate has no Jump drive and no installed
fuel scoop: never add a Jump ring, radiator, coil housing, vane, glow, external
tank, or gas-giant intake grid. Surface collection uses ordinary closed service
connections rather than a prominent flight scoop.

## Hardpoint and firing-arc map

Four trainable hardpoints form complementary pairs instead of a dorsal row:

- the visible forward upper-port shoulder ring carries one triple-beam turret
  with exactly three short narrow optical emitters;
- the visible aft lower-port flank/sponson ring carries one outward-canted
  triple-missile turret with exactly three closed rectangular launcher faces;
- a hidden upper-aft starboard shoulder ring carries the second triple-beam
  turret; and
- a hidden forward ventral/starboard ring carries the second triple-missile
  turret.

The standard port view therefore exposes exactly two of four turrets. The
visible upper and lower stations overlap across forward, port, dorsal, ventral,
and aft approaches; the hidden partners close the starboard and opposite-axis
blind regions. No fifth ring, centerline turret, point-defense node, bay,
barbette, fixed gun, exposed missile, or decorative weapon may appear.

## Redoubt finish and invariants

Fire red `#B83B2E` covers the armor fields; orange `#E9782E` identifies weapon
collars, drive rims, and hazard edges; bone `#D8CCA9` marks the bridge, access
inserts, and cargo shutter; heavy stainless and restrained chrome protect
leading edges, rings, frames, and datum guards; gunmetal remains inside deep
machinery recesses. This is the common Redoubt language applied to a distinct
family chassis: boldly colored, rugged, and field-repairable.
