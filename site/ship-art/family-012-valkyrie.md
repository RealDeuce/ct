# Valkyrie Family Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 012*

## Mechanical identity

- Family: `family-12`, Valkyrie
- Members: `ship-12` Skuld and `ship-15` Sigrun
- Hull: 10 displacement tons / approximately 140 m³
- Configuration: streamlined
- Technology level: 11
- Shared maneuver drive: `sC` / thrust 6
- Shared control: one-person cockpit
- Shared electronics: basic military and model-2 computer
- Shared software: Evade/1 and Fire Control/1
- Shared hardpoint: one single mount on the dorsal centerline
- Shared endurance: one week
- Shared limitations: no Jump drive, no airlock, no passenger space, and no
  independent operational endurance

The two craft are weapon and shipyard fits of one aerospace-fighter chassis.
The pressure body, cockpit, control surfaces, hardpoint center, maneuver-field
housings, recovery geometry, and principal seams are invariant. Neither fit may
inherit the wingless Icarus silhouette merely because it has the same tonnage.

## Canonical family anchor

The Valkyrie is a compact arrowhead aerospace fighter with maximum dimensions
12.4 m long, 6.8 m wide, and 2.8 m high. A flattened rhomboid pressure body runs
from the chisel nose to a broad aft drive block. Two short, thick cropped-delta
lifting shoulders are structural continuations of the pressure body rather
than thin contemporary aircraft wings. The filled hull and shoulder roots
occupy approximately 60 percent of the bounding envelope, plausibly enclosing
the required approximately 140 m³.

The single pilot sits forward beneath a low two-pane bronze-tinted canopy. The
canopy seam is the only personnel access and is not a pressure airlock. A
recessed oblong universal hardpoint lies on the dorsal spine immediately aft of
the cockpit. The weapon housing may change with the fit, but its center, outer
socket boundary, and below-deck feed trunk do not.

The rear quarter has two hexagonal maneuver-field apertures separated by a
narrow vertical power-service grille. A pair of shallow ventral recovery hooks
and four flush landing pads support carrier and planetary operation. There are
no passenger windows, cargo doors, side hatches, external missiles, separate
tailplanes, or exposed landing skids.

### Invariants

Both production fits preserve exactly:

- the 12.4 × 6.8 × 2.8 m arrowhead silhouette, thick cropped-delta
  shoulders, chisel nose, and broad drive block;
- the two-pane cockpit canopy, canopy access seam, and paired nose sensor
  facets;
- the dorsal oblong hardpoint center, socket boundary, and feed-trunk fairing;
- the twin hexagonal aft field apertures, central power grille, four landing
  pads, two recovery hooks, and primary seams;
- camera, crop, perspective, backdrop, and lighting; and
- the absence of airlock doors, passenger windows, cargo doors, exposed
  ordnance, additional mounts, Jump hardware, thin aircraft wings, separate
  tailplanes, and landing skids.

## Variant delta: `ship-12` Skuld

- Native path: 9, Tempest Arsenal
- Role: missile fighter for local interception and carrier strike
- Drives: `sC` maneuver and `sC` power
- Protection: one point of whole-hull crystaliron armor
- Weapon: one compact trainable missile-rack turret in the shared dorsal socket
- Magazine: twelve standard missiles, entirely internal beneath the socket
- Cargo: 0.475 tons of residual stores
- Armor reading: one continuous violet edge band around the nose, cockpit,
  shoulder roots, field apertures, and hardpoint feed trunk
- Path treatment: sharpen the existing shoulder seams toward the nose and fit
  an abrupt low launch shutter with a bright chrome collar; do not add missile
  cells or external ordnance
- Livery: ultramarine `#3046A6` body, violet `#6B3F91` armor band, warning
  yellow `#F2C84B` shutter and recovery marks, bright chrome collars

## Variant delta: `ship-15` Sigrun

- Native path: 7, Admiralty Line Works
- Role: fixed-beam aerospace-defense fighter
- Drives: `sC` maneuver and `sG` power
- Protection: no armor
- Weapon: one fixed beam laser in the shared single-mount socket; express it as
  a low axial emitter fairing with one dark forward aperture, not a trainable
  turret or protruding barrel
- Magazine: none
- Control fit: fiber-optic computer backup and one additional fire-control
  station, both internal
- Cargo: none
- Path treatment: formal axial panel order, circular bright-metal emitter
  collar nested inside the unchanged oblong socket, repeated datum lines, and a
  slightly wider power grille inside its unchanged opening
- Livery: deep navy `#203B69` body, warm white `#E8E5D4` cockpit and shoulder
  panels, signal red `#C43C35` emitter and recovery marks, bright aluminum trim
- Prohibited: armor band, missile shutter, visible missile, moving turret,
  second weapon, changed cockpit, enlarged hull, or altered lifting surfaces

## Cross-path family rule

Family geometry outranks shipyard language. Skuld must read as Tempest through
its armor seams, weapon shutter, finish, and violet/ultramarine livery. Sigrun
must read as Admiralty through its orderly axial panels, standardized emitter
collar, datum lines, and navy/white livery. Those treatments may not move or
redesign any invariant chassis landmark.

## Catalog plate

- Canvas: 3:2 landscape
- View: complete front-port three-quarter, 12 degrees above centerline
- Perspective: restrained near-orthographic
- Lighting: warm upper front-port key, weak cool aft rim
- Backdrop: charcoal-black sparse starfield and faint teal plotting grid
- Medium: original 1970s/1980s gouache-and-airbrush technical catalog plate
- Prohibited: text, labels, logos, watermarks, other craft, weapon fire, motion
  blur, planets, dramatic lens distortion, modern gray stealth-aircraft styling,
  or recognizable franchise design
