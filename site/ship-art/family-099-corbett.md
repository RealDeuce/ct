## Canonical architecture

Four habitable volumes follow one axial load path: a faceted command wedge with
eight bridge panes, two parallel rectangular mission/cargo bodies, and a broad
squared aft engineering block. Record 99 exposes long aluminum trusses and
large voids between them. Record 107 joins the same volumes with shorter,
heavier armored lattice webs and narrow bridge panels; it must never become a
smooth enclosing hull.

Seven fixed dorsal turret centers are fully occupied. Record 99 carries two
blue-optic beam triples, two broad-mouth missile triples, and three short-pot
sandcaster triples. Record 107 changes one sandcaster station to a third beam
triple. No closed or spare circular hardpoint may appear.

The eight barbettes use four readable near-side channels and four mirrored
far-side/ventral channels: three visible blue particle-beam channels imply six
particle barbettes, while one visible copper rail channel implies two railgun
barbettes. Channel collars and service fittings are not turret rings.

A common red-rimmed 10 × 5.8 m aft hangar shutter opens onto an approximately
25 m branching apron sized for two 30-ton Wayfarers and the 8.4 m Archimedes.
All three craft remain inside. Ten fuel processors are internal; neither ship
has a fuel scoop.

The internal Jump drive is marked only by one dark segmented radiator and two
recessed coil-service housings inside the aft engineering boundary. Never add a
glowing ring, external coil loop, or invented exhaust. Record 107 may show one
second, smaller emergency-power service grille.

## Shipyard finish

Admiralty Line Works uses deep navy `#203B69`, warm ivory `#E8E5D4`, signal
red `#C43C35`, bright aluminum, and restrained chrome. Record 99 may show more
field wear and exposed structure. Record 107 has cleaner standardized panels
and more formal finish while remaining recognizably the same shipyard product.
