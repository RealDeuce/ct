## Canonical architecture

The Duncan is a compact 76 × 30 × 20 m integrated fleet citadel with about six
occupied deck levels. A blunt faceted command prow and recessed six-pane bridge
sit between broad forward weapon shoulders. A deep central mission block leads
to a clearly separated squared engineering battery. The standard hull is
non-aerodynamic but continuous: never streamline it or break it into a truss.

Eight-point crystaliron armor produces deep equipment recesses, layered edges,
an armored bridge visor, and protected drive apertures rather than arbitrary
tank plates. Repeated transverse frames and a strong centerline keel establish
Admiralty axial order.

### Weapon map

Four 51-ton installed bay volumes dominate the forward architecture:

- two paired axial meson-gun bays use deep red-edged apertures in the blunt
  prow, one to either side of the centerline; and
- two particle-beam bays use long blue insulated emitter channels in the port
  and starboard shoulders.

Six fixed 3-4 m turret rings are all occupied and all readable from the catalog
view. The forward pair carries particle-beam triples with heavy insulated
barrels, the central pair carries beam-laser triples with narrow blue optics,
and the aft pair carries sandcaster triples with short blunt ejector pots.
There are no missile launchers and no spare turret rings.

Ten point-defense lasers occupy five mirrored pairs around the command, waist,
and engineering arcs. Six tiny near/dorsal red lenses may be readable in the
standard port view; four far/ventral partners are hidden. Each is less than one
quarter of a turret-ring diameter and must never read as another turret.

### Hangar, fuel, and drives

A closed 9.0 × 5.2 m aft-port full-hangar shutter and approximately 23 m
internal apron contain one 21.0 × 7.6 × 4.1 m Jason. The boat is never shown
outside the ship. A ventral scoop grid and five grouped processor-service
grilles serve the shared fuel plant.

The internal Jump drive reads through one segmented transverse radiator band
and two flush coil-service housings inside the aft boundary. Four recessed
maneuver-field apertures and two power grilles complete the stern. Do not add a
Jump ring, decorative glow, or invented exhaust.
