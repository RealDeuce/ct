# Antaeus Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 047*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-47`; `ship-47` Antaeus
- Shipyard path: Path 7, Admiralty Line Works
- Hull: 200 displacement tons / approximately 2,800 m³
- Dimensions: 48 m long, 18 m wide, and 10 m high
- Configuration: streamlined; TL11; non-standard; four armor points
- Drives: maneuver C and power C / thrust 4; no Jump drive
- Service: five crew, three staterooms, 25 steerage berths, three additional
  airlocks, two weeks endurance, and 113 tons cargo
- Armament: two single sandcaster turrets and forty internal canisters

Antaeus moves heavy loads, work parties, and steerage travelers between
surfaces, orbit, and nearby installations. Cargo is the design's purpose. It
must be carried between systems or remain within one planetary economy.

## Independent armored lifting barge

The 48 × 18 × 10 m hull is a broad low “armored lifting barge”: blunt shovel
prow, wide command shoulders, huge deep rectangular cargo citadel with clipped
aerodynamic corners, paired thick landing/drive chines, and short squared
engineering stern. The tapered pressure envelope plausibly encloses 2,800 m³.

Antaeus is not a larger Goliath. Never replace this independent cargo citadel,
shovel prow, port freight wall, landing chines, or access map with Goliath's
30 m assault wedge, central stern ramp, two-airlock layout, or turret geometry.

Exactly six panes form the bridge brow. Two small port crew-cabin windows are
visible; the third position is hidden on starboard. Five ribbed environmental
vent groups index the 25 steerage berths without becoming passenger glazing.
Two of the three fitted additional-airlock doors appear on the port side; the
third is assigned to the occluded starboard cargo shoulder. All are closed.

One enormous closed 12 × 5 m two-leaf shutter dominates the port cargo
citadel. Its central split, overlapping armor, low reinforced sill, and vehicle/
pallet handling sockets serve the 113-ton hold. No ramp, person, or cargo is
exposed. Six heavy landing-pad groups support a loaded barge.

## Sandcaster and machinery map

Two single-sandcaster stations cover complementary axes:

- one visible forward upper-port flank/shoulder turret has one blunt four-port
  projector face; and
- one hidden lower-aft-starboard/ventral turret closes the far-side and lower
  blind region.

The standard plate therefore shows one of two turrets—approximately half—and
never exposes the hidden station. All forty canisters are internal. There is no
beam optic, missile shutter, point defense, third ring, or crown row.

Four maneuver apertures form stern port/starboard vertical pairs; two port
apertures are visible and two starboard apertures hidden. Smaller ribbed power
grilles remain distinct. Antaeus has no Jump hardware, scoop, processor,
external tank, or container.

## Admiralty recognition finish

- Landing chines, command surround, cargo shoulders, stern blocks: deep navy
  `#15355F`
- Principal pressure hull and cargo citadel: warm white `#F0E7D0`
- Cargo boundary/split, airlocks, steerage status, weapon, tow/recovery:
  signal red `#B33432`
- Armor bands, prow, glazing, access, cargo, vents, landing and drive edges:
  bright steel/chrome
- Recesses and grilles: gunmetal

Goliath supplies Admiralty finish vocabulary only. Antaeus keeps its freight-
first geometry and one-visible/one-hidden sandcaster map.
