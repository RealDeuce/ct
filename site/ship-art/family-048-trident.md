# Trident Family Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 048*

## Mechanical identity

- Family: `family-48`, Trident
- Members: `ship-48` Triton, `ship-55` Nereus, and `ship-56` Glaucus
- Shipyard path: Path 8, Redoubt Shipbuilding
- Hull: 95 displacement tons / approximately 1,330 m³
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11
- Shared protection and performance: four armor points, `sW` maneuver and
  power / thrust 6, one-week endurance, and no Jump drive
- Shared crew fit: four-person control cabin, three compact staterooms, one
  emergency low berth, fuel processor, and exactly two pressure airlocks
- Shared hardpoint: one deep dorsal single-mount well

Triton fits a missile rack, sixty missiles, and 30.75 tons cargo. Nereus fits
one particle beam, no ammunition, and 35.75 tons cargo. Glaucus fits stealth
coating, one missile rack, forty-eight missiles, and 31.75 tons cargo. Weapon
and coating treatments may change; the hull and socket coordinates may not.

## Canonical family architecture

The Trident is 31.0 m long, 13.5 m wide, and 6.5 m high with two occupied decks.
It is a compact streamlined citadel: blunt faceted prow, broad armored shoulders,
short protected waist, and a deep aft drive block. Thick shoulder roots and
chamfered belly plausibly enclose approximately 1,330 m³. It is a large escort
boat, not a thin fighter or Jump-capable trader.

A recessed seven-port control brow sits in the prow. Three small stateroom windows
form one protected strip behind it. Exactly two rectangular pressure-airlock
doors lie on the visible port flank, one forward and one aft, each behind a
stainless edge guard. Between them lies one closed 7.0 × 3.2 m cargo shutter;
one small emergency-berth service panel sits below the forward airlock.

One 3.5 m circular deep turret well lies on the dorsal centerline. Its outer
ring, armored feed trunk, and recess are invariant. Missile fits use one low
closed gridded shutter box with no visible ordnance. Nereus replaces only that
box with one compact insulated particle-beam emitter and substantial field
collar in the same well.

Two deeply recessed hexagonal maneuver apertures, a wide heat-stained central
power grille, six armored landing-pad doors, paired recovery hooks, overlapping
bay shutters, two redundant sensor blisters, and heavy stainless leading-edge
guards complete the chassis. There is no Jump band, coil blister, vane, or ring.

### Invariants

All variants preserve exactly:

- the 31.0 × 13.5 × 6.5 m compact citadel silhouette;
- seven bridge ports, three stateroom windows, two airlocks, one cargo shutter,
  and one emergency-berth panel;
- dorsal well center, ring, feed trunk, and recess;
- aft drive block, paired maneuver apertures, power grille, landing-pad doors,
  recovery hooks, sensor blisters, bay shutters, edge guards, and main seams;
  and
- camera, crop, perspective, backdrop, and lighting.

Do not add a second weapon, exposed missile, second cargo door, third airlock,
additional occupied window, external tank, Jump hardware, thin aircraft wing,
exposed landing gear, or altered hull.

## Variant deltas

### `ship-48` Triton missile escort

- One closed low missile-rack shutter box in the shared well
- Deep internal sixty-round magazine; no visible missile or reload hatch
- Fire red `#B83B2E` body, orange `#E9782E` weapon/drive accents, bone
  `#D8CCA9` command and access panels, stainless edge guards and collars

### `ship-55` Nereus particle escort

- Replace only the missile box with one compact insulated particle-beam emitter
  in the exact well, with one heavy dark aperture and stainless field collar
- No ammunition shutter or magazine marking
- Same Redoubt color-block boundaries; use orange heat shielding around the
  emitter and stronger heat staining at the power grille

### `ship-56` Glaucus stealth escort

- Retain the exact Triton missile box and all hull geometry
- Apply low-sheen stealth coating inside the same Redoubt blocks: deep fire-red
  main panels, muted orange accents, subdued bone command panels, darkened
  stainless guards; remain colorful rather than gray or black
- Break bright reflections across panel interiors without deleting guards,
  seams, doors, windows, or drive apertures
