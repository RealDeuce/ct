# Albatross Family Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 022*

## Mechanical identity

- Family: `family-22`, Albatross
- Members: `ship-22` and `ship-151`
- Shipyard path: Path 1, Concord Exchange Yards
- Hull: 40 displacement tons / approximately 560 m³
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11
- Shared drives: `sK` maneuver and power
- Shared control: two-person cockpit or control cabin; one pressure airlock
- Shared accommodation: sixteen acceleration seats
- Shared fittings: aerofins, Model/1 computer, and one-week endurance
- Shared protection: four points of crystaliron armor
- Shared limitations: no Jump drive, weapon, hardpoint fit, or sleeping berth

The two records describe the same fitted merchant pinnace. Their source-derived
cargo allowances differ by three tons because the armor is represented once as
whole points and once as a layer, but the active construction records specify
the same protected hull and all exterior-visible equipment agrees. They
therefore use one exact exterior plate rather than inventing a false variant.

## Canonical family architecture

The Albatross is 23.5 m long, 9.2 m wide, and 4.8 m high: a long, low
streamlined pinnace with a rounded chisel prow, shallow lifting belly, and two
neatly faired aft maneuver housings. Its filled volume plausibly encloses
approximately 560 m³ without reading as either a narrow airliner or an
oversized ship's boat.

A wide five-pane two-person cockpit occupies the prow. Behind it, one long
horizontal occupancy band contains exactly eight passenger-window bays, one
for each pair of the sixteen seats, without suggesting private cabins. One
rectangular port pressure airlock lies immediately aft of the window band. A broad closed
aft-port freight door with a low sill serves the ten-to-fourteen-ton hold.
Shallow aerofin chines run along the lower flanks rather than becoming separate
aircraft wings. Two rectangular chrome drive collars, a central ribbed power
grille, four flush landing-pad doors, and restrained commercial service panels
complete the hull.

### Invariants

Preserve exactly the 23.5 × 9.2 × 4.8 m silhouette; rounded chisel prow;
five-pane cockpit; eight window bays; one port airlock; aft freight door
and sill; lower aerofin chines; paired aft maneuver housings and rectangular
chrome collars; central power grille; landing-pad doors; principal seams;
camera; crop; backdrop; perspective; and lighting.

Do not add a weapon socket, turret, projecting barrel, second airlock, private
cabin row, external tank, Jump hardware, separate aircraft wing, exposed
landing gear, cargo contents, or another craft. The armor is expressed only by
close panel spacing and protected leading edges, not military slab plating.
