# Bellerophon Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 025*

## Mechanical identity

- Family: `family-25`, Bellerophon
- Member: `ship-25` Bellerophon
- Shipyard path: Path 8, Redoubt Shipbuilding
- Hull: 15 displacement tons / approximately 210 m³
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11
- Protection: two points of whole-hull crystaliron armor
- Performance: `sE` maneuver and power / thrust 6; one-week endurance
- Control: two-person cockpit, basic military electronics, model-2 computer
  with fiber-optic backup, Evade/1, and Fire Control/1
- Weapon: one single trainable turret carrying one missile rack
- Magazine: twelve standard missiles, entirely internal
- Limitations: no Jump drive, no pressure airlock, no passenger accommodation,
  and only 1.925 tons cargo

This is a two-crew anti-ship strike fighter, not an enlarged interceptor or a
small independent boat. Its dimensions define the largest craft handled by the
Mitchell carrier's flight and recovery decks.

## Canonical architecture

The Bellerophon is 14.8 m long, 7.2 m wide, and 3.4 m high. Its blunt faceted
wedge prow, broad armored shoulders, short protected center body, chamfered
belly, and deep aft drive block form a wingless compact citadel that plausibly
encloses approximately 210 m³.

Two crew sit beneath one low four-pane armored cockpit canopy. The canopy is an
access hatch rather than a pressure airlock; there is no side personnel door.
One circular trainable turret well lies on the dorsal centerline immediately
behind the cockpit. It carries one low closed missile-rack drum with abrupt
gridded shutters and no exposed ordnance. The twelve-round magazine is internal
beneath the well.

Exactly four recessed maneuver-field collars surround one central ribbed
power-service grille across the aft block. Paired military sensor blisters lie
at the nose. Six armored landing-gear door seams, paired carrier-recovery hooks,
flush tie-down sockets, heavy stainless leading-edge guards, and accessible
armor seams complete the Redoubt chassis. There is no Jump landmark, scoop,
external tank, thin wing, tailplane, passenger glazing, or cargo shutter.

### Invariants

Any later Bellerophon fit preserves exactly:

- the 14.8 × 7.2 × 3.4 m wingless armored-dart silhouette;
- one four-pane two-crew cockpit canopy and no airlock door;
- one dorsal turret well, one low missile-rack drum, and the internal magazine
  bulge;
- four maneuver-field collars, one power grille, two nose sensor blisters, six
  landing-gear door seams, two recovery hooks, and the tie-down sockets;
- main armor seams, camera, crop, perspective, backdrop, and lighting; and
- the absence of visible missiles, a second weapon, Jump hardware, wings,
  tailplanes, passenger windows, and exposed landing gear.

## Redoubt finish

- Main armor blocks: fire red `#B83B2E`
- Weapon, drive, and recovery accents: orange `#E9782E`
- Cockpit and access panels: bone `#D8CCA9`
- Guards and component collars: heavy stainless with restrained chrome
- Glazing: dark bronze
- Character: compact, repairable, and difficult to disable, with modest carrier
  handling wear and bright period color rather than modern gray camouflage
