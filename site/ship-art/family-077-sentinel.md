# Sentinel Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production singleton family 077*

## Mechanical identity

- Record: `ship-77` Sentinel
- Path: Redoubt Shipbuilding (P8)
- Hull: 300 displacement tons / approximately 4,200 m³; standard configuration
- Dimensions: 38.0 m long, 24.0 m wide, and 18.0 m high
- Fit: TL11, eleven armor points, one reinforced-structure increment, maneuver
  H, power J, no Jump drive, two weeks endurance, and advanced electronics
- Weapons: one 51-ton meson-gun bay, one particle-beam barbette, and one
  beam/beam/sand triple turret; no point-defense weapons
- Support: eight crew, two staterooms, one crew berthing, emergency low berth,
  repair drones, fuel scoop and processor, and 76.75 tons cargo

Sentinel is an independent system-monitor family. It must not reuse Monitor's
offset casemate ram, Aegis's streamlined shield wedge, Bastion's launch bunker,
or another Redoubt hull. Shared armor construction and finish identify the yard;
the watchtower cruciform identifies Sentinel.

## Canonical architecture

The hull is a 38.0 × 24.0 × 18.0 m “orbital watchtower cruciform”: a blunt
near-vertical shield prow, tall central faceted sensor citadel with a deeply
buried four-pane command slit, four short thick occupied armor quadrants, deep
lower cargo block and keel, and short broad machinery stern. Chamfered handling
corners do not make this standard hull aerodynamic. It is a compact filled
multi-deck fortress rather than an aircraft, watercraft, tank, or pod cluster.

One broad armored faceted sensor crown and three redundant low domes serve the
system-monitor mission. Two planar antenna grids and narrow optical flats
complete the advanced suite. Every dome and instrument is unarmed. Exactly one
of two stateroom windows appears on the port command quadrant; crew berthing is
windowless. One personnel airlock, plain emergency-berth panel, repair-drone
slot, and ordinary service hatches remain closed and unmarked.

One closed 9.0 × 4.5 m four-leaf cargo/patrol-store shutter occupies the lower
port cargo block. It serves the 76.75-ton hold and remains entirely separate
from the meson channel. There is no hangar or carried craft.

## Heavy-weapon architecture

Three hardpoint-consuming installations cover complementary arcs:

1. Visible axial lower-forward keel: one architectural 51-ton meson-gun bay.
2. Visible upper port-forward quadrant: one particle-beam barbette inside a
   6.5 m recessed channel.
3. Hidden lower-aft starboard quadrant: one beam/beam/sand triple turret.

The 10.5 × 5.5 m meson channel uses straight load paths, a deep feed trunk,
overlapping radiation baffles, cooling panels, and two insulated annular field-
coil collars. Exactly one deeply recessed dark emitter terminus lies inside the
forward collar. It is hull architecture, not a projecting cannon barrel, and
has no second aperture, beam, glow, flame, or exposed magazine.

The particle barbette is one short heavy insulated emitter with one narrow
cobalt aperture and stainless/orange collar, contained wholly inside its port
channel. It is smaller than the meson architecture and is not a rotating
turret. The hidden triple turret does not migrate to the crown for display.
There are no point-defense nodes; never arm a sensor dome, optical flat,
processor, scoop, docking fitting, door latch, or service grille.

## Fuel, drive, armor, and structure

One ribbed processor grille and coupling appear on the port side. One broad
recessed lower-port/ventral scoop grid is protected by armor ribs and remains
recognizable as a field-grid intake rather than a hangar or weapon.

Sentinel has no Jump drive. Never add a radiator/service band, coil housing,
ring, hoop, portal, external tank, or glow. Three deep port maneuver apertures
and three hidden starboard apertures flank two ribbed power grilles in the
short stern.

Eleven armor points and reinforced structure read through four overlapping
shield shells around the citadel, heavy quadrant edges, deep component reveals,
redundant keel ribs, and thick neutron-shadow plates. These are integral load
paths rather than detachable armor blocks.

## Redoubt finish and catalog plate

- Fire red `#B83B2E`: quadrants, citadel, shield, cargo block, and stern
- Orange `#E9782E`: meson and barbette collars, scoop and recovery accents,
  processor details, and selected drive interfaces
- Bone `#D8CCA9`: command surround, cargo shutter, sensor housings, access
  panels, and neutron-shadow plates
- Bright stainless steel: quadrant edges, armor load paths, opening frames,
  and service interfaces
- Gunmetal/dark teal: glazing, recesses, apertures, grilles, and seams

Use a complete front-port three-quarter view about 12 degrees above with
restrained near-orthographic perspective, 3:2 landscape, warm upper-front-port
key, cool aft rim, charcoal stars, and faint teal grid. Render as original
late-1970s/early-1980s gouache-and-airbrush technical art. No text, logo,
watermark, open door, weapon fire, planet, modern CGI, or franchise styling.

## Production sequence and provenance

Generated for Cepheus Trader on 2026-08-20 with OpenAI image generation in
built-in mode. The neutral pass divided the command slit too finely; one scoped
correction reduced it to four panes while preserving the entire watchtower
chassis. The final plate derives from that corrected anchor and uses Monitor
only as a Redoubt finish reference.
