# Congreve Family Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 035*

## Mechanical identity

- Family: `family-35`, Congreve
- Members: `ship-35` and `ship-36`
- Shipyard path: Path 9, Tempest Arsenal
- Hull: 80 displacement tons / approximately 1,120 m³
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11
- Shared protection: four armor points
- Shared performance: thrust 5, four-person control cabin, basic military
  electronics, Model/2 computer, and no Jump drive
- Shared accommodation: three staterooms and one pressure airlock
- Shared weapon center: one dorsal hardpoint and a two-bank missile-launch
  cassette whose outer socket, feed trunk, and cell coordinates never move

Ship 35 is the deep-magazine strike fit: two fixed missile racks, 144 internal
missiles, Fire Control/2, three escape pods, two-week endurance, and 10 tons of
mission cargo. Ship 36 is the escort fit: one trainable missile rack, twelve
missiles, Fire Control/1, one emergency low berth, one-week endurance, and 32.3
tons cargo. The magazine and tank reductions become internal cargo; they do not
change the pressure hull, doors, or drive geometry.

## Canonical family architecture

The Congreve is 29.0 m long, 11.5 m wide, and 5.8 m high with two occupied
decks. It is a thick spear-shaped lifting body: a blunt chisel command prow, a
deep central magazine spine between two cropped armored shoulders, and a broad
aft drive wedge. The shoulders are filled continuations of the pressure hull,
not thin aircraft wings. The tapered envelope plausibly encloses approximately
1,120 m³ and must read as a large long-endurance boat rather than a fighter.

The command prow has a protected six-port bridge brow. Exactly three small
stateroom windows occupy one recessed port strip behind it, followed by one
rectangular pressure-airlock door with a bright handrail. Below the occupancy
strip, a common three-position service grid supports either three escape-pod
hatches or the escort's low-berth panel and two blanks. One broad closed cargo
door lies farther aft under the port shoulder.

One standardized 3.5 m dorsal hardpoint ring and deep feed trunk carry a low
rectangular missile cassette with exactly two adjacent four-shutter banks. The
outer cassette, ring, feed trunk, both bank boundaries, and all eight shutter
coordinates are family invariants. Ship 35 activates both banks and locks
the cassette forward with two axial braces. Ship 36 retains the exact cassette
but activates only the port bank, covers the other bank with one smooth armored
blank, and omits the fixed braces so the mount can train. All missiles remain
behind closed shutters.

Two sharply faired sensor cheeks flank the prow. Two long hexagonal maneuver
apertures and one heat-stained ribbed power grille fill the aft wedge. Six flush
landing-pad doors, two carrier-recovery hooks, abrupt armor shutters, and bright
datum rails complete the chassis. There is no Jump service band, coil blister,
field vane, or ring.

### Invariants

Both fits preserve exactly:

- the 29.0 × 11.5 × 5.8 m thick spear lifting-body silhouette;
- chisel command prow, six bridge ports, three stateroom windows, one airlock,
  common three-position service grid, and one cargo door;
- dorsal hardpoint center, ring, feed trunk, two-bank cassette envelope, bank
  boundaries, and eight closed shutter coordinates;
- prow sensor cheeks, aft drive wedge, paired maneuver apertures, power grille,
  landing-pad doors, recovery hooks, datum rails, and primary armor seams; and
- camera, crop, perspective, backdrop, lighting, and Tempest palette.

Do not add an exposed missile, third launcher bank, second hardpoint, gun
barrel, second airlock, extra occupied window, external tank, Jump hardware,
thin aircraft wing, separate tailplane, exposed landing gear, or changed hull.

## Variant deltas

### `ship-35` Congreve Strike

- Both four-shutter banks are active behind warning-yellow gridded doors
- Two short chrome axial lock braces make the cassette visibly fixed forward
- Three brick-violet escape-pod hatches fill the common lower service grid
- Deep internal magazine; no exterior missile or reload door is added
- Main body ultramarine `#3046A6`, armor and magazine spine violet `#6B3F91`,
  launch shutters and recovery marks warning yellow `#F2C84B`, bright chrome

### `ship-36` Congreve Escort

- Preserve the exact cassette, both bank boundaries, and all eight shutter positions
- Activate only the port four-shutter bank behind warning-yellow gridded doors
- Cover the starboard bank with one smooth violet armored blank inside its
  unchanged boundary; remove only the two fixed axial lock braces
- In the common lower service grid fit one emergency-berth service panel and
  two smooth armored blanks; do not show escape-pod hatches
- Retain the exact Tempest palette and color-block boundaries
