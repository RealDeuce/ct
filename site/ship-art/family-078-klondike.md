# Klondike Family Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 078*

## Mechanical identity

- Members: `ship-78` Bonanza and `ship-79` Skagway
- Paths: Outer Reach (P3) and Rogue Tide (P6)
- Hull: 800 displacement tons / approximately 11,200 m³; distributed
- Drives: Jump/maneuver/power K, Jump-2, thrust 2, two weeks endurance
- Protection: two armor points
- External load: exactly 110 tons on one 300-ton docking clamp
- Carried craft: two 50-ton Proteus Cargo boats and one 10-ton Charon launch
- Shared weapons: two double missile and two double sandcaster turrets
- Bonanza: two double beam and two single pulse-laser turrets
- Skagway: four double beam-laser turrets

Neither record has a hangar. The three carried craft are secured to an external
rack and therefore appear in the recognition plate. All craft were approved
before this parent family.

## Canonical architecture

The Klondike is 76.0 m long, 32.0 m wide, and 18.0 m high overall. Four separate
pressure volumes hang from a heavy open truss: a faceted five-pane command drum,
two parallel long module pods, and a broad aft engineering drum. Open voids
remain visible between all four volumes. Each pod has three fixed large module
shutters; cargo/mining and troop/assault leaves occupy the same exact frames.

One deeply braced 300-ton clamp supports a port-side rack with two equal large
cradles and one small cradle. The Proteus boats and Charon retain their
recognizable relative scale, remain secured and unpowered, and never merge into
the parent frame.

The standard view exposes five turret sockets; three mirrored starboard sockets
are occluded, for eight total. Bonanza's visible sockets show one pulse laser,
one beam pair, one missile pair, one sandcaster pair, and the second pulse
laser. Skagway replaces the visible pulse fits with beam pairs. Hidden partners
complete the catalog totals.

The internal Jump drive reads through two ribbed aft field-vane housings and a
segmented radiator. Four maneuver apertures and two power grilles occupy the aft
drum. A distributed hull has no fuel scoop. Light armor protects individual
volumes without filling the truss voids.

### Invariants

Preserve exactly the four volumes, truss and voids, two three-shutter pod grids,
five visible and three hidden turret centers, three-cradle rack, clamp arms,
three carried-craft positions, Jump/drive landmarks, landing pads, camera, crop,
backdrop, and lighting. Never add a smooth enclosing shell, scoop, hangar, or
fourth craft.
