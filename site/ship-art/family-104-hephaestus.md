## Canonical parent architecture

The Hephaestus parent is a 94 × 36 × 24 m integrated non-aerodynamic carrier
citadel with about seven occupied decks. A blunt faceted command block and
recessed eight-pane bridge lead into broad shoulders and a long rectangular
cargo/troop core. A squared engineering block closes the stern. Redoubt's short
load paths, redundant sensor cheeks, layered stainless guards, and accessible
panels remain exact; the light one-point armor does not become a deep
battleship shell.

Two continuous heavy structural rails run along each flank, one upper and one
lower. These four rails and their attachment points are family structure. On
the visible port flank, five modular positions divide into the corresponding
five hidden starboard positions. Clamp machinery may change, but the parent
hull, rails, cargo doors, and station coordinates do not.

Two broad closed freight shutters serve the cargo core. A separate 7.2 × 5.2 m
full-hangar shutter and approximately 19 m internal apron contain one 17.5 ×
6.4 × 4.6 m Caduceus. The launch remains inside on both plates.

### Shared weapon map

Fourteen triple turrets form seven mirrored pairs. The elevated port plate
shows exactly seven bases: three forward beam-laser triples, two amidships
missile-rack triples with closed shutters, and two aft sandcaster triples.
Their seven far/ventral mirrors produce the fitted totals of six beam, four
missile, and four sandcaster turrets. There is no barbette or weapon bay.

Fourteen point-defense lasers likewise form seven mirrored pairs. Seven tiny
near/dorsal lenses may be visible and seven are hidden. A PD fixture stays less
than one quarter of a turret-ring diameter.

## Clamp and drive fits

### Enyo — record 104

Five compact visible 90-ton clamp heads and five mirrored hidden heads occupy
the ten family positions. Every head is an empty short stainless saddle with
folded jaws; Enyo carries no external load. The ventral scoop reservation is
blank. One segmented Jump radiator and two flush coil-service housings identify
the internal Jump-K drive. Moderate maneuver apertures and two power grilles
remain inside fixed stern boundaries.

### Ares — record 105

Remove the ten compact heads without moving the family rails. Fit four
continuous 300-ton cradles: upper and lower port cradles plus two starboard
mirrors. Each carries three 95-ton Tritons end-to-end, or 285 tons per cradle.
The port view therefore shows six full-scale 31.0 × 13.5 × 6.5 m boats in two
rows of three; the six craft on the starboard cradles are hidden. All twelve
are rigidly docked, never flying or miniaturized.

Ares has no Jump drive, so the radiator and coil housings are blanked flush
inside their inherited boundaries. The ventral scoop and ten processor grilles
are active. Maneuver and power apertures enlarge only within the common stern
stations for W/W drives.
