# Faraday Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 191*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-191`; `ship-191` Faraday
- Hull: 2,500 displacement tons / approximately 35,000 m³, plus 80 tons
  external load
- Dimensions: 132 m long, 46 m wide, and 27 m high
- Configuration: standard; technology level 11
- Protection: two crystaliron armor points and a self-sealing hull
- Drives: Jump-T / Jump-2, two consecutive Jumps, maneuver T, and power T /
  thrust 2
- Endurance: two weeks of power-plant fuel
- Control: advanced electronics, holographic controls, Capital Core/3,
  emergency power, and improved magazine
- Mission support: 66 crew, four laboratories, cartography, sensor control,
  gardens, offices, workshops, repair drones, medical support, and two tons
  stores
- Internal craft: one 600-ton full hangar containing two 300-ton Tizard
  long-range scouts (`ship-190`)
- External craft: one 90-ton clamp carrying one 50-ton Proteus Surveyor
  (`ship-188`) and one 30-ton clamp carrying one 30-ton Wayfarer Cargo
  (`ship-187`)
- Battery: twenty double-beam turrets, five mixed missile/sand double turrets,
  and twenty-five point-defense lasers; no unused hardpoints
- Ammunition: 120 missiles and 200 sand canisters
- Fuel handling: one scoop and twenty processors

## Independent Civic carrier

Faraday is a 132 × 46 × 27 m non-aerodynamic exploration carrier, not a
streamlined cruiser. A broad pentagonal command-and-instrument prow, long
carrier centerbody, deep structural keel, and compact aft engineering block
form calm symmetric primary volumes. The bridge uses exactly eight narrow
panes. Circular instrument collars, ceramic radomes, fine antenna grids, teal
optical glass, observation bands, probe ports, and chrome laboratory frames
interrupt that symmetry only where scientific work requires them.

The defining centerbody feature is one 600-ton full hangar expressed as two
parallel long dorsal launch-lane shutter sets divided by one central load rail.
Both lanes open into the same hangar volume and are long and wide enough for
the two canonical 58 × 20 × 12.5 m Tizard scouts. Both shutter sets remain
closed in the catalog plate; neither scout nor any hangar interior is visible.

## Distributed defensive battery

Faraday's twenty-five trainable hardpoints prioritize overlapping coverage and
minimal blind spots over a readable dorsal row. Their fixed station map is:

- five port-flank gallery stations and five matching starboard-flank stations;
- three separated dorsal/upper-shoulder stations around the hangar;
- five ventral stations along the keel and lower shoulders;
- three forward cheek/axial stations; and
- four aft-quarter/stern stations.

The standard front-port view exposes thirteen station centers: all five port-
flank stations, three dorsal stations, one port-side ventral-lip station, two
forward stations, and two aft stations. Twelve remain occluded: five starboard,
four deeper ventral, one forward-starboard, and two far-aft stations. Clamp
arms, instrument collars, antenna bases, processor grilles, and engine
apertures never count as weapon stations.

Ten visible stations carry low double-beam cups with exactly two short optical
emitters. Three visible aft-biased stations carry one closed missile shutter
and one blunt sandcaster aperture: the port-aft flank, aft dorsal shoulder, and
aft-port quarter. The hidden battery contains the other ten double-beam and two
mixed stations. Thus all twenty beam and all five mixed turrets cover forward,
aft, dorsal, ventral, port, and starboard approaches without migrating onto the
roof. No missile is exposed.

Twenty-five much smaller PD laser nodes follow the same coverage logic. About
thirteen tiny cobalt lenses are readable in the standard plate and twelve are
occluded. They remain visibly subordinate to the 3–4 m turret rings and never
become decorative gun cups.

## Carried-craft recognition

The larger forward-lower port foundation is the 90-ton clamp. It carries one
26 × 9.5 × 5.4 m Proteus Surveyor parallel to Faraday, nose forward. At 19.7
percent of mothership length it preserves the canonical streamlined Proteus
fairing, five-pane cockpit, survey instrument collar, antenna grid, cabin-
window groups, closed air-raft door, and Civic white/cyan/lime/chrome finish.
It is unarmed; its only projecting connections are short blunt chrome clamp
arms.

The smaller aft-upper port foundation is the 30-ton clamp. It carries one 18.8
m Wayfarer Cargo parallel and nose forward, approximately 14.2 percent of
Faraday's length. It preserves the established Wayfarer utility-boat body,
seven-window passenger rail, closed freight fittings, and Concord ivory,
vermilion, royal-blue, and chrome finish. It is also unarmed.

Both craft remain visibly separate from Faraday and clear of the bridge,
hangar shutters, turrets, sensors, radiator, scoop, and engines. There is no
third external craft. The two much larger Tizards remain inside the closed
hangar.

## Drives, fuel, and mission recognition

Jump-T / Jump-2 uses one segmented non-glowing aft radiator band and paired
field-coil/service housings. The T/T two-gravity stern uses orderly maneuver
apertures and power grilles rather than flames. Faraday's two-Jump allocation
accounts for 1,032 tons of Jump fuel and its power allocation adds 36 tons;
the broad carrier body contains these tanks without exposed spheres.

One broad flush scoop field occupies the lower forward keel. Twenty processors
appear as protected segments within distributed grille banks, not twenty
identical giant intakes. Laboratory window bands, observation bays, probe
ports, garden-light panels, cartography antennas, and the sensor-control crown
make exploration support visible while remaining barrel-free.

## Civic Survey finish

Civic Survey Works uses warm white `#F4F0D8`, saturated cyan `#25A9B8`, lime
`#A8C83E`, clean chrome, and restrained gunmetal. White dominates the public
service hull; cyan identifies hangar and mission bands; lime marks instruments,
clamps, and safety interfaces; chrome protects turret, laboratory, door, and
docking collars. The separately built Wayfarer retains Concord colors. Bright
opaque enamel and chrome preserve the period illustration language without
making the vessel look militarized.

## Catalog plate and reusable prompts

restrained near-orthographic perspective, 3:2 landscape, warm upper-front-port
key, weak cool aft rim, charcoal sparse starfield, and faint teal plotting grid.
Render original late-1970s/early-1980s gouache-and-airbrush technical art with
bright opaque enamel and clean chrome. No planet, motion blur, modern gray
rendering, or recognizable franchise design.
