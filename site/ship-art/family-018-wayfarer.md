# Wayfarer Family Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 018*

## Mechanical identity

- Family: `family-18`, Wayfarer
- Members: `ship-18`, `ship-134`, `ship-165`, `ship-181`, `ship-187`, and
  `ship-212`
- Hull: 30 displacement tons / approximately 420 m³
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11
- Shared drives: `sJ` maneuver and power / thrust 6; two-week endurance
- Shared control: two-person control cabin; one pressure airlock
- Shared protection: one crystaliron layer / four armor points
- Shared cargo: four tons
- Shared limitations: no Jump drive and no sleeping accommodation

This is a broad utility ship's-boat chassis, not the long Argonaut clipper.
Ships 134 and 165 are exterior-identical Marque armed boats. Ships 181 and 187
are exterior-identical Concord cargo boats. Exact plate reuse is required for
each of those pairs.

## Canonical family anchor

The Wayfarer is 18.8 m long, 8.4 m wide, and 4.0 m high: a broad bevelled
lifting body with a blunt rounded prow, full-width single-deck cabin, shallow
equipment belly, and two boxed aft maneuver nacelles blended into the hull. Its
filled volume is about two thirds of the bounding envelope, plausibly enclosing
approximately 420 m³.

A wide five-pane two-person control cabin occupies the prow. One 2.4 m port
airlock lies immediately aft of it. Seven reserved narrow occupancy bays form a
straight port strip. One large 3.0 × 2.3 m aft-port cargo door and low loading
sill express the shared four-ton hold. A circular dorsal hardpoint socket lies
over the center of mass. Two square recessed field apertures and a central
ribbed power grille occupy the stern. Four flush landing pads and two tie-down
sockets remain invariant.

### Invariants

Preserve exactly the 18.8 × 8.4 × 4.0 m silhouette; five-pane control
cabin; port airlock; seven occupancy-bay boundaries; cargo door and sill;
dorsal hardpoint socket; aft nacelles, square field apertures, power grille,
landing pads, tie-downs, principal seams, camera, crop, and lighting. Do not add
another door, weapon socket, cargo opening, external tank, Jump hardware,
aircraft wing, or exposed landing gear.

2. Produce Utility, then Armed from the anchor and Utility.
3. Produce Cargo from the anchor, retaining a closed hardpoint.
4. Produce Boarding from the anchor and Armed.
5. Compare all four plates; reuse Armed for 134/165 and Cargo for 181/187.
