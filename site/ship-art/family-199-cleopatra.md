# Cleopatra Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 199*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-199`; `ship-199` Cleopatra
- Hull: 100 displacement tons / approximately 1,400 m³; 40 × 15 × 8.5 m
- Configuration: streamlined; TL9 standard design; two armor points
- Drives: Jump-A / Jump-2, one Jump, A/A maneuver and power; four weeks
- Service: three crew, six staterooms, three emergency low berths, two luxury
  allocations, and 12 tons cargo
- Fuel handling: two processors and **no fuel scoop**
- Armament: none; one unused hardpoint; no ammunition

## Independent Concord yacht

Cleopatra is a broad flattened almond rather than a Pony Express derivative. A
graceful clipped prow with five bridge panes, sweeping shoulders, six tall
observation-salon bays in two groups of three, smooth belly, and quiet compact
stern make privacy and diplomatic travel visible. The salon is an occupancy
and luxury index, not a literal one-window-per-stateroom deck plan.

One small 12-ton cargo shutter, three emergency-berth service panels, formal
boarding hatch, luxury-service collars, sensors, and landing doors remain
closed. One aft-dorsal 3–4 m hardpoint stays beneath a smooth polished cover;
never add a turret, emitter, launcher, PD node, or decorative weapon.

## Drives, fuel, and finish

Jump-A uses one compact segmented radiator and paired low field housings. Two
small maneuver apertures and two power grilles identify modest A/A performance.
Exactly two processor banks are present. Despite the streamlined hull,
Cleopatra has no scoop: preserve the smooth belly with only a refuelling
coupling and landing seams.

Concord warm ivory `#F1E3C2`, royal blue `#2354A3`, vermilion `#E44B2D`, and
extensive polished chrome create high-status period travel. The blue salon band,
ivory shoulders, red prow/service accents, and chrome observation frame remain
bright and glamorous rather than modern minimalist white.

## Invariants

Preserve the 40 × 15 × 8.5 m almond; five bridge panes; six salon bays; formal
boarding hatch; cargo shutter; three emergency panels; luxury collars; covered
hardpoint; smooth no-scoop belly; two processors; Jump and A/A machinery;
seams; camera; crop; backdrop; and lighting.

Never install a weapon or scoop, open an exterior boundary, expose passengers
or cargo, add external craft or tank, wing, fin, glowing Jump ring, flame,
text, logo, or watermark.
