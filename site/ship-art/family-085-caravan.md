# Caravan Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production singleton family 085*

## Mechanical identity

- Record: `ship-85` Caravan
- Path: Venture Passage Works (P2)
- Hull: 1,000 displacement tons / approximately 14,000 m³; standard
- Dimensions: 74.0 m long, 31.0 m wide, and 20.0 m high
- Fit: TL11, two armor points, Jump H / Jump-2, maneuver and power H / thrust
  2, two weeks endurance, advanced electronics, and holographic controls
- Accommodation: sixteen staterooms, two crew berthings, twenty low berths,
  emergency low berth, recreation, office, and two-bed medical bay
- Freight and route support: 498 tons cargo, five fuel processors, scoop,
  repair drones, and one Charon in a 10-ton full hangar
- Weapons: four double-beam turrets, three double missile/sandcaster turrets,
  and three unused fire-control stations; no point defense

Caravan is an independent armed-freighter family. It must not reuse Argosy's
rounded cargo-whale, Verne's packet spearhead, Hanse's three-volume frame, or
Ithaca's modular freighter. The forward-driving lozenge, protected flanks,
commercial turret cups, and bright livery establish Venture manufacture.

## Canonical architecture

The hull is a 74.0 × 31.0 × 20.0 m “frontier freight lozenge”: a blunt clipped-
wedge command prow, broad filled cargo citadel, two thick protective flank
rails recessed behind the leading surfaces, narrowed protected service waist,
and broad maneuver stern. Chamfered handling corners do not make the standard
hull aerodynamic. It is a filled multi-deck spacecraft rather than an aircraft,
watercraft, external-tank assembly, or detachable module train.

Seven narrow protected panes form one low command brow. Exactly eight of
sixteen stateroom windows appear in one upper port strip and eight are hidden
starboard. Ten identical closed low-berth service panels form one visible port
row and ten matching panels are hidden. Low-berth panels are neither windows
nor weapons. One formal airlock, office/service panel, plain emergency-berth
panel, plain unmarked medical hatch, and repair-drone slot remain closed.

The 498-ton hold reads through one enormous closed 27 × 7 m six-leaf freight
shutter. Five internal divisions sit inside one continuous protected chrome
frame with handling rails and a low sill; it is one hold access rather than six
modules. A separate closed 6.2 × 4.6 m two-leaf full-hangar shutter occupies
the upper-aft port shoulder. Its approximately 14 m internal axis clears the
approved 12.5 × 5.2 × 3.8 m Charon, which remains internal.

## Ten-coordinate coverage map

Ten hardpoint coordinates divide evenly between the visible and occluded hull
halves. Five visible coordinates contain four weapons and one blank cover:

1. Forward upper-port shoulder: double-beam turret with exactly two cobalt
   optics.
2. Upper-aft port shoulder, separate from the hangar: the second visible
   double-beam turret with exactly two optics.
3. Lower-forward port flank: mixed double turret with one closed missile
   shutter and one blunt four-port sandcaster.
4. Lower-aft port flank: the second visible mixed double turret with the same
   two unlike faces.
5. Dorsal-mid centerline: one closed unused hardpoint cover.

The hidden half contains two double-beam turrets, one missile/sand turret, and
two unused covers across starboard and ventral arcs. The plate therefore shows
roughly half the ten coordinates and four of seven installed weapons without
forming a dorsal gun row. Hidden stations never migrate to the crown.

There are no point-defense nodes. Processors, coupling, scoop fittings, berth
panels, cargo and hangar latches, sensors, landing doors, and service covers
remain unarmed. All missiles and sand canisters remain internal.

## Fuel, Jump, drive, and armor

Five processors use three round ribbed port grilles and two hidden starboard
grilles, plus one manifold and coupling. One broad protected lower-port/ventral
scoop grid is an intake/field-grid region rather than a hangar or weapon.

The internal Jump-H drive reads through two segmented transverse radiator/
service bands at the aft waist and four flush oval coil-service blisters, two
visible and two hidden. There is no exterior Jump ring, hoop, portal, glow, or
tank. Two broad port maneuver apertures and two hidden starboard apertures flank
two separate power grilles at the thrust-two stern.

Two armor points read through one continuous protected edge band, reinforced
cargo, hangar, and command frames, shielded chrome rails, and overlapping
serviceable plates. Caravan remains a protected commercial ship rather than a
layered naval citadel.

## Venture finish and catalog plate

- Sunflower `#F2C230`: command, upper pressure body, and cargo-citadel fields
- Cobalt `#174A8B`: lower belly, all six cargo leaves, hangar shutter, and
  engineering panels
- Signal orange `#E96D2F`: airlock, low-berth panels, service status, turret
  collars, and selected drive accents
- Bright protected chrome/aluminum: flank rails, cargo and hangar frames,
  window, hatch, weapon, scoop, Jump, and drive interfaces
- Dark teal/gunmetal: glazing, recesses, shutter seams, slats, and grilles

The character is bright, expensive, and prepared for an unreliable frontier
without reading as a naval vessel. Use a complete front-port three-quarter
view about 12 degrees above, restrained near-orthographic perspective, 3:2
landscape, warm upper-front-port key, cool aft rim, charcoal stars, and faint
teal grid. Render as original late-1970s/early-1980s gouache-and-airbrush
technical art. No text, logo, watermark, open door, weapon fire, planet, modern
CGI, or franchise styling.

## Production sequence and provenance

Generated for Cepheus Trader on 2026-08-21 with OpenAI image generation in
built-in mode. The neutral pass rendered five cargo leaves; one scoped edit
subdivided the unchanged opening into six. The production pass correctly
exposed four weapons and one blank cover, then required scoped weapon-face
edits so both lower cups resolved to one closed missile shutter plus one four-
port sandcaster. The final plate derives from the corrected anchor and uses
Argosy only as a Venture finish reference.
