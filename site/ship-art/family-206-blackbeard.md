# Blackbeard Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 206*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-206`; `ship-206` Blackbeard
- Shipyard path: Path 6, Rogue Tide Yards
- Hull: 600 displacement tons / approximately 8,400 m³
- Dimensions: 76 m long, 28 m wide, and 18.5 m high
- Configuration: standard; technology level 11; standard design
- Protection: eight titanium-steel armor points and fib Model/3 computer
- Drives: Jump F / Jump-2; maneuver M and power M / thrust 4
- Endurance: one Jump-2 and four weeks of power-plant fuel
- Mission support: 24 crew, twelve staterooms, six emergency low berths,
  armory, four detention cells, six fuel processors, scoop, and 55 tons cargo
- Flight facilities: one 50-ton internal hangar carrying two Thermopylae
  fighters (`ship-200`) and one Tender ship's boat (`ship-202`)
- Armament: six triple-beam turrets; all six hardpoints occupied

Blackbeard is an independent armed command rather than a conversion of another
merchant hull. Its silhouette is merchant-like at distance, but armor,
boarding access, a dense flight block, detention services, and an unusually
complete beam battery reveal its prize-taking role at catalog range.

## Canonical dockyard predator

The 76 × 28 × 18.5 m standard hull is deliberately non-streamlined: a blunt
faceted command prow, deep rectangular pressure body, offset upper service
spine, protected boarding waist, stepped port flight block, and broad square
engineering stern. Four titanium-steel layers appear as overlapping armor
bands and reinforced shoulders. A five-pane buried bridge brow and six small
port stateroom panes establish occupied scale.

One closed boarding airlock, a separate closed 55-ton cargo shutter, one
armory-service panel, and paired detention-service panels remain human-scale
and unarmed. Mismatched replaceable plates and false seams are intentional
Rogue Tide service language, not stealth coating or battle damage.

## Internal three-craft hangar

One 50-ton hangar occupies the stepped port flight block. Its exactly three
closed shutters reflect the approved carried-craft envelopes:

- two equal upper cells, each with approximately 7.5 × 4.2 m clear frontage
  and at least a 13.5 m internal axis, each contain one canonical
  11.8 × 6.4 × 3.2 m Thermopylae; and
- one larger lower cell, approximately 10 × 6.5 m at the shutter and at least
  19 m deep, contains the canonical 17.5 × 8 × 5.2 m Tender.

This is one dense internal hangar, not three construction components or
external clamps. All shutters remain closed and all three carried craft remain
invisible. Never substitute a single giant bay door, show a craft on the hull,
or repaint an exposed craft in Rogue Tide colors.

## Hardpoint and firing-arc map

Six trainable triple-beam hardpoints form a three-visible/three-hidden coverage
map. The standard port view exposes:

- one forward upper-port shoulder turret;
- one midship outward-canted port-flank turret; and
- one lower forward-port/ventral-lip turret.

Each visible mount has exactly three short narrow optical emitters in one
unambiguous row. The hidden half is deliberately aft-weighted: one upper-aft
starboard shoulder station, one aft starboard-flank station, and one aft
ventral station. Together the rotating mounts overlap forward, aft, dorsal,
ventral, port, and starboard approaches despite the visible lower station
lying farther forward than originally planned. Do not add a fourth visible
turret, migrate the battery to a dorsal row, expose the hidden half, or confuse
machinery, shutters, and processor fittings with weapons.

## Drive, fuel, and Rogue Tide recognition

Jump F / Jump-2 uses one long non-glowing segmented upper-aft radiator and two
armored coil/service housings. Four large stern maneuver apertures and two
broad power grilles identify the four-gravity M/M plant. One recessed lower
port/ventral scoop grid and six small processor grilles support independent
fuel collection. Never add a glowing Jump ring, flame, external tank, second
scoop, or generic intake.

Rogue Tide Yards uses deep aubergine `#4D294F`, hot vermilion `#D94B35`,
mustard `#D4A62A`, and blackened chrome. Aubergine dominates armor; vermilion
marks turret, boarding, hangar, and machinery edges; mustard identifies
replaceable service panels; blackened chrome protects rings, rails, scoop,
Jump-service, and drive collars. The saturated theatrical finish remains
period-bright while low-sheen panels and mismatched inserts discourage a
formal fleet appearance.

## Invariants and prompt

Preserve the 76 × 28 × 18.5 m standard hull; five-pane bridge; six cabin
panes; armor bands; boarding/cargo/detention access; exactly two small and one
large closed hangar shutters; carried-craft clearances; three visible and three
hidden turret coordinates; three emitters per turret; Jump radiator and two
coil housings; scoop and six processors; four maneuver apertures; two power
grilles; landing seams; camera; crop; backdrop; and lighting.

```text
Generate the neutral Blackbeard standard-hull raider with the three-cell
internal hangar, six-station coverage map, and machinery coordinates above.
Derive the production plate without moving a coordinate. Fit only the three
visible triple-beam turrets, with exactly three optics apiece; keep the hidden
three occluded and all carried craft internal. Apply Rogue Tide aubergine,
vermilion, mustard, and blackened chrome. No extra mount, open shutter, exposed
craft, text, pirate insignia, weapon fire, or Jump glow.
```

The first production fit rendered four-emitter clusters. Focused edits rebuilt
all three visible inserts as exact triples, removed a fourth shoulder aperture,
and simplified both flank faces until nine visible beam emitters were clearly
countable. Artwork was generated for Cepheus Trader on 2026-08-20 with OpenAI
image generation in built-in mode, production derived from the approved
neutral anchor.
