# Raleigh Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production singleton family 081*

## Mechanical identity

- Record: `ship-81` Raleigh
- Path: Marque Marine Yards (P5)
- Hull: 500 displacement tons / approximately 7,000 m³; streamlined
- Dimensions: 72.0 m long, 25.0 m wide, and 15.5 m high
- Fit: TL11, eight armor points, radiation shielding, self-sealing hull, Jump
  E / Jump-2, maneuver and power E / thrust 2, and four weeks endurance
- Merchant support: six staterooms, armory, office, workshop, emergency low
  berth, one medical bed, repair drones, six fuel processors, and 198 tons cargo
- Weapons: three triple-beam turrets and two mixed beam/missile/sand triple
  turrets; no point-defense weapons
- Limitations: no hangar, carried craft, docking clamp, or fuel scoop

Raleigh is an independent armed-merchant family. It must not reuse the
Stevenson clipper, Hawkwood clipper-citadel, Marque escort brig, or Argonaut
boat. Its long thrust lines, protected boarding waist, bright collars, and
formal livery establish Marque Marine lineage without copying another chassis.

## Canonical architecture

The hull is a 72.0 × 25.0 × 15.5 m “cargo-sabre”: a long high six-pane command
prow, deep double-deck freight belly, narrowed protected boarding waist, two
thick integral cargo shoulders with functional edge ribs, slightly rising
dorsal datum, and compact squared low-thrust stern. Its handsome lines do not
hide the large filled cargo volume. It is a multi-deck spacecraft rather than
an aircraft, watercraft, flat fighter, tank arrangement, or pod cluster.

One enormous closed 15.5 × 5.5 m four-leaf cargo shutter runs along the lower
port freight belly. Three vertical divisions sit inside one continuous heavily
reinforced frame with handling rails and a low sill. It serves the 198-ton hold
and never opens in the catalog plate. There is no hangar or carried craft.

One shielded 2.4 m boarding airlock and gold/steel docking collar occupy the
narrow waist, with fold-flat handholds. The collar is not a turret. Exactly
three of six stateroom windows appear in the protected port command strip and
three are hidden starboard. One armory shutter, office/service panel, workshop
hatch, plain emergency-berth panel, plain medical hatch, and repair-drone slot
remain closed and unmarked.

## Weapon and coverage map

Five occupied triple-turret centers cover complementary arcs:

1. Visible dorsal-forward: triple beam turret with exactly three narrow optics.
2. Visible upper-aft port shoulder: the second triple beam turret with exactly
   three narrow optics.
3. Visible lower-mid port flank: mixed turret with one beam optic, one closed
   missile shutter, and one blunt four-port sandcaster.
4. Hidden upper starboard shoulder: the third triple beam turret.
5. Hidden ventral-aft starboard: the second mixed turret.

The standard plate exposes three of five weapons across dorsal and flank
surfaces. Hidden stations do not migrate to the crown for display. The two
beam-only cups use matching gold-chrome collars; the lower mixed cup keeps its
three faces visibly distinct. Ammunition remains internal.

There are no point-defense nodes. Never arm the boarding collar, processor,
coupling, sensor, cargo-frame fitting, service latch, landing door, or recovery
guide.

## Fuel, Jump, drive, and protection

Six processors form a bilateral bank with three round ribbed grilles visible
port and three hidden starboard, plus one manifold and coupling. Raleigh has no
recorded scoop.

The internal Jump-E drive reads through one segmented transverse radiator/
service band at the aft waist and two long shallow ribbed coil-service housings,
one visible and one hidden. There is no exterior Jump ring, hoop, portal, glow,
or tank. Two broad port maneuver apertures and two hidden starboard apertures
flank two separate power grilles at the modest thrust-two stern.

Eight armor points, radiation shielding, and self-sealing construction read
through three flowing protection bands, neutron-shadow plates around command
and engineering, reinforced opening frames, protected glazing, overlapping
plates, and visible dark gasket seams.

- Emerald `#13705B`: main pressure hull and engineering blocks
- Cream `#EFE0B5`: command, occupancy, cargo shutter, and selected radiation-
  shadow panels
- Burgundy `#8D2E3C`: boarding waist, cargo frame, and lower datum bands
- Gold-toned chrome: turret and docking collars
- Bright steel: functional edge ribs, cargo frame, and service interfaces
- Gunmetal/dark teal: glazing, recesses, grilles, apertures, and gasket seams

Keep the enamel maintained with practical cargo-handling and boarding wear.
The character is a commissioned armed merchant: dangerous and legitimate, but
still organized around revenue-producing freight volume.

Use a complete front-port three-quarter view about 12 degrees above with
restrained near-orthographic perspective, 3:2 landscape, warm upper-front-port
key, cool aft rim, charcoal stars, and faint teal grid. Render as original
late-1970s/early-1980s gouache-and-airbrush technical art. No text, logo,
watermark, open door, cargo, weapon fire, planet, modern CGI, or franchise
styling.
