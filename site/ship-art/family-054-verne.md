## Canonical family architecture

The Verne is 50.0 m long, 18.0 m wide, and 11.0 m high. Its streamlined
packet-liner hull has a flattened faceted spearhead prow, a deep rounded-
hexagonal pressure body, thick integral shoulder fairings, and a squared aft
engineering block. The tapered envelope and occupied fraction plausibly enclose
approximately 4,200 m³ without treating the entire bounding box as solid.

A low six-pane bridge brow crosses the prow. One 2.5 m port personnel airlock
sits beneath an oval docking recess. The visible port waist contains one shared
twelve-leaf commercial insert in two rows. Passenger, cargo, magazine, and
accommodation fits glaze or shutter those exact leaves; the insert outline and
leaf centers never move. A small low vehicle-garage shutter is common to all
fits. An arrow-shaped aft-port reservation becomes Nemo's full-hangar door and
remains a closed service panel elsewhere.

Three common 3-4 m dorsal turret socket centers run fore to aft. A separate
8 m semi-recessed channel accepts Nemo's particle-beam barbette. A 14 m lower-
port structural reservation accepts Stahlstadt's 51-ton missile bank. The
barbette and bay are distinct from the three turret sockets and remain visibly
blanked on fits that do not use them.

The Jump drive is internal. Every fit shows it through one segmented transverse
radiator/service band at the aft waist and two flush coil-service housings in
fixed metal collars. There is no exterior Jump ring or glow. Two broad stern
maneuver apertures flank one ribbed power grille. Robur enlarges only the
openings inside those fixed apertures for thrust 3. A flush ventral scoop grid,
four recessed landing-pad doors, and human-scale access seams complete the
shared hull.

### Invariants

All seven plates preserve exactly:

- the 50.0 × 18.0 × 11.0 m streamlined silhouette, prow, pressure body,
  shoulder fairings, and aft engineering block;
- the six-pane bridge brow, port airlock, docking recess, twelve-leaf insert,
  vehicle-garage shutter, and aft-port hangar boundary;
- three dorsal turret socket centers, one separate barbette channel, and one
  separate lower-port weapon-bay reservation;
- the transverse Jump-service band and two coil-service collars;
- stern drive block, fuel-scoop region, landing geometry, primary seams,
  camera, crop, perspective, backdrop, and lighting; and
- the absence of an external tank, arbitrary Jump ring, separate aircraft
  wing, exposed landing gear, or unrecorded weapon.

  21.7 tons cargo

- Weapons: one mixed triple turret, one triple beam-laser turret, one closed
  turret socket, and one separate 8 m particle-beam barbette
- Finish: emerald `#13705B`, cream `#EFE0B5`, burgundy `#8D2E3C`,
  gold-toned chrome, and bright steel edge ribs

### Robur — record 168

- Path 2, Venture Passage Works; later fast armed trader
- Commercial insert: four passenger leaves and eight cargo shutters;
  63.6 tons cargo
- Weapons: Fogg's three triple turrets plus exactly three 0.5 m point-defense
  laser nodes on the port prow, lower waist, and aft shoulder
- Later fit: paired bridge sensor cheeks, holographic controls, model-3/bis
  computer, repair drones, and enlarged openings inside the common thrust-3
  stern apertures
- Finish: Fogg's Venture palette with finer edge treatment for the single
  crystaliron layer

The mixed triple turret always contains three unlike apertures in one common
housing: one narrow beam-laser lens, one closed missile-rack shutter, and one
blunt sandcaster projector. Triple beam turrets use three matching recessed
optical apertures. The missile bank is architectural and its shutter count does
not stand for individual rounds.

## Production sequence

1. Approve the neutral 300-ton streamlined packet-liner anchor and twelve-leaf
   insert.
2. Produce Passepartout as the simplest cargo and single-turret reference.
3. Derive Hatteras and Aouda by changing only insert allocation and path finish.
4. Derive Fogg from the passenger reference by populating the other two turret
   sockets.
5. Derive Robur from Fogg; reject any glazing outside the shared insert and
   verify exactly three point-defense nodes.
6. Produce Stahlstadt by activating only the reserved lower weapon-bay zone.
7. Produce Nemo by activating only the reserved hangar and barbette zones;
   verify exactly two turrets plus the separate barbette.
8. Compare all seven masters at equal size before publication.
