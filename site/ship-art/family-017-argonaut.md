# Argonaut Family Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 017*

## Mechanical identity

- Family: `family-17`, Argonaut
- Members: `ship-17` Jason, `ship-19` Medea, `ship-178` Castor, and
  `ship-179` Pollux
- Hull: 30 displacement tons / approximately 420 m³
- Configuration: streamlined
- Technology level: 11
- Shared drives: `sJ` maneuver and `sJ` power / thrust 6
- Shared control: two-person cockpit
- Shared protection: two points of whole-hull crystaliron armor
- Shared electronics: basic military, model-2 computer, Evade/1, and Fire
  Control/1
- Shared weapon: one single turret with one beam laser
- Shared access: one pressure airlock
- Shared endurance: one week
- Shared limitations: no Jump drive and no sleeping accommodation

The four records are passenger, boarding, and troop fits of one aerodynamic
boat. The pressure body, cockpit, airlock aperture, turret ring, drive block,
landing geometry, and principal seams remain exact. Seating and cargo change
the glazing and aft service panel within reserved openings, not the hull.

## Canonical family anchor

The Argonaut is a long armored clipper-boat 21.0 m long, 7.6 m wide, and 4.1 m
high. A rounded chisel prow leads into a deep single-deck pressure cabin with an
equipment bilge, protected by thick swept shoulder fairings and a broad
rectangular aft drive block. The pressure body and shoulder roots occupy about
64 percent of the bounding envelope, plausibly enclosing approximately 420 m³.

A two-person four-pane cockpit sits high behind the prow. One 2.4 m port
pressure-airlock aperture lies amidships beneath a recessed oval docking
collar. A reserved six-bay viewport strip runs aft of the airlock; each fit may
glaze or shutter these exact bays. One reserved rectangular aft-port service
panel supports cargo access where the fit has meaningful hold volume. Neither
opening may move or enlarge.

A standardized circular hardpoint ring on the dorsal centerline behind the
cockpit carries the shared compact beam-laser turret. The stern has two
horizontal oval maneuver-field housings around one ribbed central power grille.
A small flush processor-service grille under the port shoulder is present on
the three fuel-processing fits and blanked on Pollux. Four recessed landing
pads and two carrier tie-down sockets remain flush with the streamlined body.

### Invariants

All four variants preserve exactly:

- the 21.0 × 7.6 × 4.1 m armored clipper silhouette, swept shoulder
  fairings, deep pressure cabin, and broad drive block;
- the two-person four-pane cockpit and canopy seam;
- the port airlock aperture and docking-collar recess;
- the six-bay viewport strip, aft service-panel boundary, and processor-grille
  boundary, whether open, glazed, shuttered, or blanked;
- the dorsal turret ring and one compact beam-laser turret;
- twin aft field housings, central power grille, landing pads, tie-down sockets,
  primary seams, camera, crop, perspective, backdrop, and lighting; and
- the absence of a second weapon, external fuel tank, exposed landing gear,
  Jump hardware, separate aircraft wings, or extra personnel doors.

### `ship-19` Medea

- Native path: 5, Marque Marine Yards

- Cargo: 2.45 tons

  cream occupancy band

### `ship-178` Castor

- Native path: 7, Admiralty Line Works
- Role: armed personnel boat for fourteen seated passengers
- Cargo: 5.45 tons
- Equipment: fuel processor
- Visible fit: use the same four glazed/two shuttered viewport allocation, full
  aft service door, and processor grille as Jason
- Livery: deep navy `#203B69`, warm white `#E8E5D4`, signal red `#C43C35`,
  bright aluminum datum lines and standardized component collars
- Cross-path rule: geometry and openings remain Jason-exact; only panel rhythm,
  collars, and finish express Admiralty manufacture

### `ship-179` Pollux

- Native path: 7, Admiralty Line Works
- Role: marine transport for twenty-five troops
- Cargo: 0.45 tons
- Equipment: no fuel processor
- Visible fit: shutter all six viewport bays behind warm-white troop-compartment
  panels; emphasize red handholds around the unchanged airlock; blank the aft
  cargo/service panel and processor grille inside their exact boundaries
- Livery: Castor's formal Admiralty palette with a wider warm-white occupancy
  band and signal-red assault/recovery marks
- Prohibited: extra airlock, boarding tube, cargo door, processor grille, second
  weapon, larger hull, or altered cockpit

## Production sequence

1. Approve the neutral 30-ton clipper-boat anchor.
2. Produce Jason as the simplest fitted Marque variant.
3. Produce Medea from the anchor and Jason, changing occupancy/access details.
4. Produce Castor from the anchor and Jason, changing only native-path finish.
5. Produce Pollux from the anchor and Castor, shuttering troop and service
   openings while preserving their boundaries.
6. Compare all four plates at equal size before publication.
