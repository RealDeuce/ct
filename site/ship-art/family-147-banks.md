# Banks Family Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 147*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/members: `family-147`; `ship-147` Banks, `ship-148` Solander,
  `ship-149` Parkinson, `ship-150` Tizard survey ship, and `ship-190` Tizard
  long-range scout
- Hull: 300 displacement tons / approximately 4,200 m³
- Dimensions: 58 m long, 20 m wide, and 12.5 m high
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11
- Shared protection: four armor points from one crystaliron layer
- Shared control: holographic bridge controls and bis Model/2 computer
- Shared endurance: two weeks of power-plant fuel
- Shared services: one fuel processor, medical bed, repair drones, office, and
  emergency low berth; no member has a fuel scoop
- Shared ammunition: 12 missiles and 20 sand canisters

The three Concord merchants use Jump-C, one Jump-2 fuel load, E/E maneuver and
power, basic civilian electronics, one mixed triple turret, two empty
hardpoints, and a four-ton vehicle hangar. Tizard survey keeps C/E/E but carries
two Jump-2 fuel loads, advanced sensors, a laboratory, probes, a 24-ton internal
Caduceus hangar, and three armed turrets. Tizard scout uses D/F/F, advanced
sensors, laboratory and probes, 59.5 tons of stores, three turrets, and a
30-ton external clamp carrying a 20-ton Caduceus.

## Shared canonical chassis

The family occupies a 58 × 20 × 12.5 m shallow streamlined pressure shell.
A rounded clipped prow, long straight-sided centerbody, strong lower keel, and
neatly faired compact stern make it an orderly modular transport rather than a
cylindrical launch or pointed warship. The bridge brow has exactly six panes;
flush cheek strips hold the baseline sensors. Two airlocks bracket the mission
zone. Landing-gear doors remain flush and closed.

The defining family feature is one approximately 25 × 6.5 m port module
frame. Its outer perimeter, four equal vertical stations, lower sill, depth,
attachment bolts, and relation to both airlocks never move. Conversion changes
only the material fitted inside those coordinates.

A small closed four-ton vehicle-hangar shutter sits aft of the main frame on
all three merchant fits. It is not one of the revenue-module cells and carries
no visible vehicle. Survey and scout fittings reuse its socket for their
recorded small-vehicle service requirements.

## Module reuse matrix

| Ship | Fixed-frame contents | External craft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Banks | Four broad ribbed freight shutters | None |
| Solander | One forward freight shutter; eight windows across three occupied cells | None |
| Parkinson | Twelve windows, three in each of four occupied cells | None |
| Tizard (`ship-150`) | One closed four-leaf internal-launch hangar shutter | None; Caduceus is inside |
| Tizard (`ship-190`) | Three freight shutters and one instrument/laboratory cell | One clamped Caduceus |

Banks's four freight cells express its 84.5-ton hold. Solander retains one cell
for freight and uses eight small dark-teal windows to mark its mixed service and
35.5-ton hold. Parkinson replaces the last freight cell, showing exactly twelve
windows and leaving only three tons of internal cargo. Window count is a visual
occupancy index, not a literal one-window-per-stateroom diagram.

The survey Tizard closes the same perimeter with four retracting shutter leaves
around an unobstructed approximately 21 × 7 × 5.5 m internal axis. It accepts
the canonical 17.5 × 6.4 × 4.6 m Caduceus (`ship-146`). The boat and every
part of it remain behind the closed shutter.

The scout Tizard has no internal launch hangar. Three cells become closed cargo
shutters; the aft cell carries two instrument collars and one antenna-grid
recess. Its 30-ton clamp holds one 20-ton Caduceus (`ship-189`) parallel to the
mothership, nose forward. The launch is about thirty percent of mothership
length and uses its canonical flattened-cylinder geometry, five-window port
rail, three-pane cockpit, airlock, cargo door, closed hardpoint, and paired aft
drive collars. It retains Venture sunflower, cobalt, orange, and chrome rather
than adopting the mothership's Civic finish.

## Turret map and fields of fire

Three fixed hardpoint coordinates cover complementary hemispheres:

- a visible forward dorsal/port-shoulder ring;
- a visible outward-canted aft port-flank/lower-shoulder ring; and
- a hidden ventral/starboard ring.

Banks, Solander, and Parkinson fit only the forward visible ring with one mixed
triple turret: one beam optic, one closed missile shutter, and one sandcaster
aperture. The visible flank ring is sealed by a smooth armored disk and the
hidden ring is likewise blank, matching the two unused fire-control stations.

Both Tizards activate all three rings. The visible dorsal cup is triple-beam;
the visible flank cup is mixed beam/missile/sand; the hidden ventral/starboard
cup is triple-beam. Thus the standard view shows two of three turrets and the
battery covers dorsal, port, ventral, starboard, forward, and aft approaches
without becoming a row of roof mounts. No family member has point-defense
nodes. Scientific ports, sensors, and docking machinery must remain barrel-free.

## Drives, fuel, and sensors

Banks, Solander, Parkinson, and survey Tizard share one non-glowing segmented
Jump-C radiator, two paired field-coil/service housings, four E maneuver
apertures, and two E power grilles. Survey Tizard's second Jump-2 fuel load is
internal and produces only paired service datum bands, never an external tank.

Scout Tizard deepens the coil details inside the same housings and enlarges the
four maneuver apertures and two power grilles within their fixed stern sockets
for D/F/F. The hull outline and radiator coordinate do not move. Every member
has one processor grille and a refuelling coupling but no scoop; never add a
ventral intake merely because the hull is streamlined.

The merchants retain flush commercial cheek strips. Both Tizards add circular
instrument collars, probe ports, a fine antenna grid, ceramic radome details,
and teal optical glass inside existing service sockets.

## Shipyard finishes

### Concord merchants

Banks, Solander, and Parkinson use warm ivory `#F1E3C2`, royal blue
`#2354A3`, vermilion `#E44B2D`, and polished aluminum/chrome. Long horizontal
bands, standardized door spacing, and bright rectangular collars emphasize
prosperous scheduled transport and rapid port turnaround.

### Civic Tizards

Both Tizards use warm white `#F4F0D8`, cyan `#25A9B8`, lime `#A8C83E`, and
clean chrome. Color concentrates around mission cells, sensors, airlocks, and
service frames to make public scientific purpose legible. The externally
clamped launch alone retains its Venture yellow/blue/orange finish.

restrained near-orthographic perspective, 3:2 landscape, warm upper-front-port
key, weak cool aft rim, charcoal sparse starfield, and faint teal plotting grid.
Render original late-1970s/early-1980s gouache-and-airbrush technical art with
bright opaque enamel and protected chrome. No planet, motion blur, modern gray
rendering, or recognizable franchise design.

## Production sequence and provenance

1. Approve the neutral shared chassis, module frame, hardpoint coverage map,
   machinery sockets, and inactive external-clamp foundation.
2. Produce Banks from the anchor.
3. Derive Solander directly from Banks and Parkinson directly from Solander so
   merchant geometry and finish remain identical.
4. Derive survey Tizard from Banks, then correct its dorsal and flank weapon fit
   before accepting the plate.
5. Derive scout Tizard from corrected survey Tizard, using the approved Venture
   Caduceus plate as the carried-craft reference.
