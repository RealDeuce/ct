## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-214`; `ship-214` Leavitt
- Shipyard path: Path 3, Outer Reach Cooperative
- Hull: 200 displacement tons / approximately 2,800 m³
- Dimensions: 43 m long, 20 m wide across the lifting surfaces, and 9 m high
- Configuration: streamlined lifting body; technology level 11; standard design
- Drives: Jump C / exact Jump-3, maneuver A / thrust 1, and power C
- Fuel: 132 tons; two Jump-3 loads plus four weeks of power-plant endurance
- Accommodation: captain, pilot, navigator, engineer, and turret gunner in
  three standard staterooms; six-person provision capacity
- Fuel handling: integral streamlined scoop and six tons of processors, able
  to refine one 60-ton jump load in twelve hours
- Trade capacity: five tons of internal cargo
- Battery: one single beam-laser turret; no ammunition or other weapons

Leavitt is the smallest rules-valid exact Jump-3 starship. Its exterior must
read as an unusually fuel-rich compact pathfinder rather than a roomy trader:
the broad lifting body is mostly tankage, while the narrow forward occupied
section and small hold remain visibly subordinate to the drives.

## Canonical lifting-body pathfinder

The 43 × 20 × 9 m hull has a sleek faceted crew-and-bridge nose, a broad
shallow centerbody, short swept lifting surfaces, and a visibly separate but
integrated cylindrical aft engineering section. A reinforced metal waist and
service collar join the two principal volumes. Small bridge panes, access
hatches, landing-gear seams, handholds, and maintenance panels establish the
five-person scale.

The single visible turret sits forward-port/dorsal and carries exactly one
narrow optical beam-laser barrel. The second hardpoint remains a flush circular
cover. Nothing else resembling a barrel, launcher, ordnance cell, point-defense
node, or ammunition port is present.

The streamlined scoop is a flush lower intake-grid region. Six compact
processor grille zones are distributed along the centerbody. Non-glowing Jump
field and radiator bands lead into the engineering collar. The distinct stern
contains the substantial C-rated plant and two restrained one-gravity maneuver
apertures; it never emits flame or a decorative glow.
