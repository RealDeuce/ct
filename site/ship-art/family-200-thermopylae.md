# Thermopylae Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 200*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-200`; `ship-200` Thermopylae
- Hull: 10 displacement tons / approximately 140 m³; 11.8 × 6.4 × 3.2 m
- Configuration: streamlined; TL9 standard design; no armor
- Performance: `sC` maneuver, `sL` power / thrust 6; one week; no Jump drive
- Control: one pilot in a one-person cockpit; Model/1 fib computer
- Armament: one fixed, non-pop-up single pulse laser; no ammunition
- Capacity: no passengers, cargo, accommodation, airlock, or external load

## Independent Admiralty interceptor

Thermopylae is a compact wingless axial dart with a chisel/needle prow,
three-pane single cockpit, short centerbody, chamfered belly, and broad aft
maneuver block. It is a spacecraft rather than an aircraft: no wing, fin,
tailplane, intake, or exposed landing strut. Military sensor cheeks, six closed
landing-pad doors, recovery hooks, tie-downs, and formal frames support carrier
operation.

The one weapon occupies a shallow dorsal/port-shoulder axial channel beside and
behind the cockpit. One short heavy ribbed pulse-laser emitter and insulated
collar point straight forward. It is fixed and non-pop-up—not a circular
trainable turret. Never add a second emitter, PD node, missile, bay, or cup.

Four large recessed maneuver apertures and one substantial power grille make
six-gravity performance the dominant aft feature. Fuel is internal for one
week. There is no Jump landmark, scoop, processor, tank, cargo door, passenger
window, or pressure airlock.

## Admiralty finish

Admiralty Line Works uses deep navy `#203B69`, warm white `#E8E5D4`, signal
red `#C43C35`, and bright aluminum. Navy covers centerbody and stern; white
identifies prow and cockpit; red marks the fixed weapon, recovery and drive
safety details; aluminum protects leading edges, datum lines and collars.

## Invariants

Preserve the 11.8 × 6.4 × 3.2 m wingless hull; three-pane cockpit; one fixed
pulse emitter and axial channel; sensors; four maneuver apertures; one power
grille; six landing doors; recovery hooks; tie-downs; seams; camera; crop;
backdrop; and lighting. Never make the weapon trainable or pop-up, add a second
weapon, cargo or accommodation, Jump/fuel hardware, wings, fins, flame, text,
logo, or watermark.
