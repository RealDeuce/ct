# Mercator Family Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 027*

## Mechanical identity

- Family: `family-27`, Mercator
- Members: `ship-27`, `ship-28`, and `ship-31`
- Shipyard path: Path 1, Concord Exchange Yards
- Hull: 100 displacement tons / approximately 1,400 m³
- Configuration: streamlined; technology levels 9 and 11
- Shared performance: Jump drive A / Jump-2; maneuver drive B / thrust 4; one
  jump and two weeks of power endurance
- Shared accommodation: four staterooms, one emergency low berth, and a
  four-person crew
- Shared protection: four armor points
- Shared weapon provision: one empty hardpoint and one unused fire-control
  station; no weapon mount is installed
- Shared role: light frontier trade, mail, exploration stores, and high-value
  freight

The three records are internal fits of one production trader. Their computers,
armor material, fuel-processing machinery, reserve missile stowage, cargo
allowance, and B/C power-plant fit do not require different doors, windows, or
outer volumes. The common engineering block provides the cooling margin and
flush intake/service grid used by every fit. Exact exterior reuse is required.

## Canonical family architecture

The Mercator is 32.5 m long, 12.8 m wide, and 7.8 m high with two occupied
decks. Unlike the broad wedge-shaped Poe courier, it uses three legible
commercial volumes under one streamlined skin: a rounded low command prow, a
tall rectangular central freight cassette inside bevelled aerodynamic
shoulders, and a compact aft engineering block with two neatly faired maneuver
pods. The tapered and stepped envelope plausibly encloses approximately
1,400 m³.

The command prow has a protected five-port bridge strip and one port personnel
airlock. Exactly four compact stateroom windows form an ivory occupancy band on
the upper port side. The central freight cassette carries one broad 7.5 × 3.6 m
closed two-leaf cargo shutter with a low sill and rectangular chrome collar.
One smaller emergency-berth service panel sits immediately forward of it. A
closed circular hardpoint cover lies flush on the dorsal centerline; it is an
empty standardized socket, not a turret or weapon housing.

The internal Jump drive reads through one transverse heat-rejection band at the
freight/engineering boundary and two low rectangular coil-service blisters,
each with a chrome access collar. The band and blisters are maintained service
hardware, not glowing rings. Two square aft maneuver-field apertures, a broad
central ribbed power grille sized for the family cooling margin, one flush lower
fuel-intake/service grid, six landing-pad doors, and ordered panel seams complete
the chassis.

  intake/service grid, landing-pad doors, and ordered seams; and
- camera, crop, perspective, backdrop, lighting, and exact production livery.

Do not add a turret, missile launcher, weapon aperture, projecting barrel,
external Jump ring, glowing drive effect, separate wing, exposed landing gear,
external tank, second cargo door, or visible missile. Ship 27's ammunition is
sealed reserve cargo for a later refit, not an installed launcher.
