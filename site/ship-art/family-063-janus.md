# Janus Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 063*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-63`; `ship-63` Janus
- Shipyard path: Path 4, Civic Survey Works
- Hull: 200 displacement tons / approximately 2,800 m³
- Dimensions: 51 m long, 16.5 m wide, and 10.5 m high
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11; non-standard design
- Protection: eleven whole points of crystaliron armor
- Drives: maneuver F and power F / thrust 6; no Jump drive
- Endurance: two weeks of power-plant fuel
- Control and complement: hardened holographic standard bridge, basic military
  electronics, Model/3 computer, and eight crew
- Accommodation and custody: two staterooms, two crew berthings, four heads,
  galley space for eight, two brig cells, and one emergency low berth
- Mission support: two fuel processors, repair drones, a 49.5-ton hold, and one
  standard 20-ton hangar carrying Caduceus (`ship-7`)
- Armament: one triple-beam turret, one mixed triple turret with two missile
  racks and one sandcaster, and three point-defense lasers
- Ammunition: 84 standard missiles and 40 sand canisters, all internal

Janus is a heavily armored in-system patrol and customs ship. Six-gravity
performance, hardened control spaces, distributed defenses, a protected
launch, brigs, and useful evidence stores support interception and boarding.
It cannot Jump and depends on a local base or carrier for strategic movement.

## Independent double-mask patrol spindle

The 51 × 16.5 × 10.5 m streamlined hull is a long “double-mask patrol
spindle”: blunt vertically clipped inspection prow, two thick occupied forward
cheeks flanking a deeply recessed command notch, a rounded-hexagonal pressure
body, broad integral patrol shoulders, pinched working waist, strong continuous
ventral keel, and compact oversized thrust-six stern. The paired prow masses
read as public-service inspection faces without becoming wings or separate
pods. The broken envelope plausibly contains approximately 2,800 m³.

Janus is independent of every other 200-ton patrol design. Never copy Warden's
short customs-shield hull or single upper-port turret; never copy Vidocq's
non-atmospheric standard hull, full hangar, five-pane prow, scoop, or turret
map; never copy Challenger's survey-compass chassis. Those ships may supply
Civic component vocabulary only.

Seven narrow bridge panes occupy the deep notch in asymmetric banks of four
and three around one broad armored center pillar. The unusual split face is a
Janus recognition feature. One of two stateroom windows is visible on the port
upper waist; the other is hidden starboard. Crew berthing and both brig cells
have no windows. One closed inspection airlock and one separate plain emergency
berth service panel sit forward. Neither carries a cross, badge, or text.

Two circular instrument collars, one low white ceramic radome, one fine flush
antenna grid, and small dark optical flats form the sensor suite. These calm
Civic instruments remain visually separate from weapons and point defense.

## Caduceus launch cell and cargo

One closed two-leaf standard-hangar shutter occupies the aft port shoulder. Its
clear boundary is approximately 7.2 m wide × 5.2 m high, with a reinforced
sill, protected recovery guides, and at least a 19 m internal launch axis. This
is a tight workmanlike bay sized from the canonical 17.5 × 6.4 × 4.6 m
Caduceus envelope. The Venture-finished craft remains fully internal and
invisible.

One separate closed 7 × 3.8 m two-leaf cargo/evidence shutter occupies the
mid-to-lower port waist. It serves the 49.5-ton hold and is visibly smaller
than the launch boundary. Never open either shutter, show the carried craft,
merge the apertures, or mistake the cargo door for a missile battery.

## Weapon and coverage map

The hull has exactly two turret hardpoint coordinates in complementary arcs:

1. mid lower-port flank at the shoulder/keel transition, visible and occupied
   by the mixed missile/missile/sandcaster triple turret; and
2. upper aft-starboard shoulder, hidden and occupied by the triple-beam turret.

The standard front-port view therefore exposes one of two turrets. The visible
low cup contains exactly two separate closed rectangular missile shutters and
one distinct blunt multi-port sandcaster projector. No missile is exposed. The
hidden three-beam cup covers the far dorsal, starboard, and aft approaches;
do not migrate it onto the visible crown merely to advertise the full battery.

Three point-defense coordinates extend coverage beyond the turret hemispheres:
forward upper-port cheek and lower-aft port keel are visible; upper-starboard
waist is hidden. The two visible nodes are tiny dark-teal optical lenses in
lime/chrome collars, each much smaller than a turret ring or instrument collar.
Never arm the radome, antenna grid, bridge optics, processor, coupling, docking
guide, or recovery fitting.

## Fuel, drive, and armor landmarks

One square ribbed port-belly fuel-processor grille and adjacent circular
refueling coupling are visible; the second processor is hidden starboard.
Janus has no recorded scoop. Four deep maneuver-field apertures form two stern
vertical pairs, with two visible port and two hidden starboard, while two
smaller separate power grilles serve the F/F thrust-six machinery.

There is no Jump drive. Never add a segmented Jump radiator, coil/service
housing, external ring, tank, luminous portal, or glow. The absence of those
landmarks and the dominant local-performance stern communicate the ship's
strategic dependence on bases.

Eleven armor points dominate the silhouette through three nested prow and
bridge shells, deep cheek plates, overlapping longitudinal flank belts,
armored hangar/cargo frames, and a thick segmented keel. All protection is
integrated into the streamlined pressure hull rather than attached as tanks,
wings, or decorative slabs.

## Civic Survey recognition finish

- Principal pressure body and nested armor shells: warm white `#F4F0D8`
- Command-notch faces, lower flank belt, hangar shutter, cargo accents, and
  engineering panels: saturated cyan `#25A9B8`
- Radomes, processors, recovery/status and defense collars: lime `#A8C83E`
- Bridge, instrument, armor, airlock, hangar, cargo, weapon, docking, and drive
  interfaces: clean bright chrome/aluminum
- Glazing, optical flats, recesses, slats and grilles: dark teal/gunmetal

Warden supplies only Civic finish and component vocabulary. Janus retains its
independent split command architecture, eleven-point armor, lower-flank mixed
turret, hidden beam turret, and two-door port working side.

The first neutral pass rendered only two broad bridge panes. A scoped edit
created the split bridge, but repeated attempts settled at a strong asymmetric
4+3 arrangement rather than the requested 3+3. Because pane count is not a
mechanical constraint and the seven-pane notch improves Janus recognition,
the accepted anchor and production fit intentionally preserve that exact 4+3
geometry.

emergency, sensor, hangar, cargo, processor, drive and armor coordinates; exact
