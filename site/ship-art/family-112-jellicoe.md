# Jellicoe Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production singleton family 112*

## Mechanical identity

- Record: `ship-112` Jellicoe; Admiralty Line (P7)
- Hull: 1,200 displacement tons / approximately 16,800 m³; standard,
  self-sealing, and radiation shielded
- Dimensions: 86.0 × 36.0 × 24.0 m
- Fit: TL11, armor eight, protected bridge, emergency power, improved
  magazine, Jump L / Jump-2, S/S thrust 4, three weeks endurance, advanced
  electronics, holographic controls, and dual Model 3 bis/fib computers
- Complement: thirty-one crew, eight staterooms, four crew berthings, armory,
  briefing room, two emergency berths, gym, two offices, workshop, two-bed
  medical bay, repair drones, and 51.8 tons cargo
- Weapons: four mixed beam/missile/sandcaster triple turrets, four meson bays,
  four particle-beam bays, and twelve point-defense lasers

Jellicoe is an independent compact battlecruiser, not a reduced Fisher or
Marathon. Its double-bevel shield prow, recessed six-pane command visor, four
huge rectangular battery sponsons, narrow armored waist, deep keel, and
hexagonal stern define a distinct bay-dominant Admiralty hull.

## Canonical architecture

The 86 × 36 × 24 m standard hull places four occupied upper/lower and
port/starboard sponsons around a strong central spine. The visible side shows
upper-forward, lower-forward, upper-aft, and lower-aft battery volumes. About
six occupied decks, human-scale access, and fine paneling preserve scale. The
hull is non-aerodynamic and has no hangar, carried craft, wings, tail, truss,
or detached nacelles.

A separate closed cargo shutter, docking collar, four of eight stateroom
windows, and one of two emergency-berth panels remain unarmed. Crew berthing is
windowless.

## Twelve-coordinate coverage map

Six hardpoints are visible and six hidden:

1. Upper-forward port sponson: one deep meson-gun bay.
2. Lower-forward port sponson: one long particle-beam bay.
3. Upper-aft port sponson: second long particle-beam bay.
4. Lower-aft port sponson: second deep meson-gun bay.
5. Forward upper-port ring: one mixed triple turret.
6. Aft lower-port ring: second mixed triple turret.

The hidden starboard side repeats two meson bays, two particle bays, and two
mixed turrets. Each mixed housing combines one cyan beam optic, one closed
missile shutter, and one perforated sandcaster projector. The widely separated
turrets overlap forward/upper and aft/lower/flank arcs; the four bays dominate
axial attack. Twelve PD coordinates split six visible/six hidden.

The lower-forward circular disk is an unarmed service face. All bays are
deeply rooted hull architecture and dwarf turrets. Cargo, docking, sensors,
Jump housings, and stern apertures are also unarmed.

## Machinery, armor, finish, and plate

Jellicoe has no scoop or processors. Internal Jump-L machinery uses a segmented
service band and four flush coil housings, never an external ring or glow.
Three visible/three hidden maneuver apertures and separate power grilles form
the S/S thrust-4 stern. Armor eight, radiation shielding, self-sealing skin,
protected bridge, emergency power, and improved magazines read through the
shield prow, thick sponson walls, redundant ribs, chrome load paths, and deep
recesses.

Admiralty finish uses navy `#203B69`, warm white `#E8E5D4`, signal red
`#C43C35`, bright aluminum/chrome, and gunmetal. Use the complete front-port
near-orthographic view, charcoal stars, faint teal grid, and original late-
1970s/early-1980s gouache-and-airbrush technical art. No text, logo, watermark,
open door, craft, weapon fire, planet, modern CGI, wing, tail, or Jump ring.
