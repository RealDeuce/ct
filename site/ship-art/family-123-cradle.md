# Cradle Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 123*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-123`, `ship-123` Cradle
- Shipyard path: Path 4, Civic Survey Works
- Hull: 350 displacement tons / approximately 4,900 m³
- Dimensions: 54.0 m long, 20.0 m wide, and 15.0 m high
- Configuration: standard; technology level 12
- Protection: two armor points and self-sealing hull
- Drives: Jump-D / Jump-2; maneuver D and power D / thrust 2
- Endurance: one Jump and three weeks of power-plant fuel
- Working fit: 148.75-ton cargo hold, scoop, four fuel processors, workshop,
  repair drones, and one 2,000-ton docking clamp
- Defense: three point-defense lasers; no offensive weapon and no ammunition

Cradle is a mobile support and ferry vessel. The empty external clamp is its
defining recognition feature and must communicate a capacity far greater than
the tender's own displacement.

## Canonical architecture

The parent is a calm symmetric support hull with a blunt faceted service prow,
protected bridge brow, long rectangular freight waist, full-length structural
spine, and squared engineering block. Its bounding envelope is about 30 percent
filled, leaving plausible tank, freight, machinery, and load-path volume.

Exactly three broad 6.5 × 4.0 m ribbed freight shutters line the visible port
waist in evenly spaced chrome frames. A smaller workshop/repair-drone hatch sits
aft. There is no passenger-window strip or weapon ring.

### Single 2,000-ton clamp assembly

Two heavy longitudinal dorsal rails, three deep cross-frames, four hinge
foundations, and one central power/feed socket tie the clamp into the entire
hull. The installed clamp is one paired-arm gantry yoke:

- exactly two massive articulated arms, one rooted along each rail through two
  hinges;
- approximately 8 m rise, 28 m open span, and 16 m useful reach;
- one telescoping transverse crosshead joining the arms as a single assembly;
- one approximately 5 m magnetic/mechanical contact jaw at each outer end;
- layered semicircular pads, folded gripping fingers, load cells, cable trunks,
  and bright replaceable wear surfaces; and
- empty, open, ferry-ready posture with no docked vessel in the plate.

The two contact jaws are parts of one clamp, not two independent clamps. The
assembly is heavy industrial load-bearing machinery, not a ring drive, wing,
crane decoration, or pair of manipulator toys.

### Other exterior systems

One segmented non-glowing Jump radiator and two flush coil-service housings
identify the internal Jump-D drive. A ventral scoop and four processor grilles
serve fuel operations. Two recessed maneuver-field apertures and one central
power grille represent the D/D machinery. Two repair-drone slots, redundant
sensor blisters, armored landing doors, towing/recovery beacons, and bright
datum rails support the tender role.

The port three-quarter view shows exactly two tiny point-defense laser lenses;
one node is hidden on the far/ventral side. They remain less than one quarter of
a turret-ring diameter. No turret or offensive weapon may appear.

- Main pressure hull: warm white `#F4F0D8`
- Freight and engineering panels: cyan `#25A9B8`
- Clamp arms, jaws, drone-service marks, and rescue datum lines: lime
  `#A8C83E`
- Rails, crosshead, door frames, radiator surrounds, jaw wear surfaces, and
  component collars: clean chrome
- Glazing: dark teal
- Wear: concentrated on contact pads, hinges, rails, and freight doors; precise
  public-service equipment rather than camouflage or generalized grime
