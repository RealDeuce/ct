# Drake Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 066*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-66`; `ship-66` Drake
- Shipyard path: Path 6, Rogue Tide Yards
- Hull: 300 displacement tons / approximately 4,200 m³
- Dimensions: 58 m long, 20 m wide, and 12.5 m high
- Configuration: streamlined; TL11; non-standard; four armor points
- Drives: Jump-C / Jump-2; maneuver F and power F / thrust 4; two weeks
- Crew: 23, including twelve marines; two staterooms, three crew berthings,
  eight low berths, emergency berth, armory, medical bed, and recreation
- Working fit: four processors, repair drones, breaching tube, 42.5 tons cargo,
  and a standard 30-ton hangar carrying Wayfarer Boarding (`ship-212`)
- Weapons: single particle-beam turret; triple beam/beam/sand turret; triple
  missile/missile/beam turret; 24 missiles and 20 sand canisters

Drake is a raider or commissioned privateer built for prize capture. Its
breaching machinery, marine complement, armed boat, hold, and coercive battery
matter more than sustained fleet combat.

## False-freighter cutlass chassis

The 58 × 20 × 12.5 m hull combines a low merchant-like command prow, long
blade pressure body, mismatched occupied shoulders, large aft-port launch block,
quieter starboard false-cargo shoulder, armored boarding keel, narrowed waist,
and thrust-four stern. The purposeful asymmetry is independent of Verne and
Stevenson geometry.

Nine panes form the low bridge brow. One of two stateroom windows is visible;
marine and crew berthing remain windowless. A reinforced port boarding airlock,
armory shutter, plain medical/berth panels, and repair-drone slot remain closed.

The lower-forward port prow carries one recessed, closed 3 m breaching-tube
collar with three telescoping sleeve seams, grapple pads, and load rails. It is
boarding machinery, never a weapon, engine, scoop, or Jump ring.

The closed aft-port standard-hangar shutter provides at least 9.4 × 4.8 m clear
area and a 20.5 m internal axis for the canonical 18.8 × 8.4 × 4.0 m Wayfarer.
The boat remains internal. A separate smaller 6 × 3.5 m lower-port shutter
serves the prize hold. Neither door may be opened in the catalog plate.

## Weapon and coverage map

Exactly three hardpoints cover complementary arcs:

1. visible forward upper-port shoulder: triple cup with exactly two short beam
   optics and one blunt four-port sandcaster;
2. visible aft upper-port shoulder: single particle-beam turret with one heavy
   insulated emitter and field collar; and
3. hidden forward upper-starboard shoulder: triple cup with two missile racks
   and one beam laser.

The view exposes two of three turrets; the far-side mixed cup and all ammunition
remain hidden. The modest upper-shoulder bias is acceptable for three mounts,
while their fore/aft and port/starboard allocation supplies distinct arcs. No
point defense or extra weapon may appear.

Two port processor grilles/couplings are visible and two starboard units hidden;
there is no scoop. Internal Jump-C appears through one offset segmented aft-
waist service band and a visible/hidden coil-blister pair, never an external
ring or glow. Two port maneuver apertures are visible and two starboard hidden,
with separate power grilles. Irregular overlapping belts and reinforced breach,
hangar, cargo, and prow frames express four armor points.

## Rogue Tide recognition finish

- Main blade and deceptive merchant panels: aubergine `#4D294F`
- Breach root, boarding, hangar threshold, weapons, thrust: hot vermilion
  `#D94B35`
- False-cargo panels, recovery and service details: mustard `#D4A62A`
- Armor, breach sleeves, doors, weapons, Jump and drive: blackened chrome
- Glazing, recesses and grilles: dark gunmetal/teal

Silver supplies only Path-6 finish vocabulary. Drake retains its independent
breach prow, asymmetric boat block, prize hold, and three-turret map.

## Reusable prompts and invariants

```text
Neutral anchor: create the independent 58 × 20 × 12.5 m streamlined Drake
false-freighter cutlass with low command prow, blade body, asymmetric occupied
shoulders, aft-port launch block, boarding keel, narrow waist, and thrust-four
stern. Add nine bridge panes, one visible/one hidden cabin position, boarding
airlock, armory and plain service panels. Install one closed recessed 3 m
telescoping breaching tube, one closed 9.4 × 4.8 m Wayfarer hangar with 20.5 m
axis, and one separate closed 6 × 3.5 m cargo shutter. Reserve three empty
hardpoints at visible forward-upper-port, visible aft-upper-port, and hidden
forward-upper-starboard positions. Show 2/2 processors, no scoop, internal
Jump-C band/blisters, 2/2 maneuver apertures and separate power grilles. Neutral
1970s/1980s gouache-and-airbrush front-port plate on stars/grid. No weapon, PD,
open door, craft, cargo, extra ring, text, logo, external Jump ring, or glow.

Drake fit: preserve every anchor coordinate. Apply Rogue Tide aubergine,
vermilion, mustard and blackened chrome. Fit the forward visible cup with two
beam optics plus one four-port sandcaster; fit the aft visible ring with one
single heavy particle emitter; keep the missile/missile/beam cup hidden
starboard. Keep the breaching tube stowed, Wayfarer internal, both shutters
closed, no scoop, Jump and stern landmarks fixed. No extra weapon, exposed
ammunition, PD, text, logo, fire, flame, or glow.
```

The first production pass left the aft ring closed and overpopulated the
forward cup. Two scoped edits installed the single particle emitter and replaced
only the forward head with its exact two-optic/one-projector fit.

Preserve the hull; bridge and service map; breach tube; hangar/cargo boundaries;
2-visible/1-hidden turret map; fuel, Jump, drive and armor landmarks; camera;
crop; backdrop; and lighting.
