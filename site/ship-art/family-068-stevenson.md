# Stevenson Family Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 068*

## Mechanical identity

- Members: `ship-68` Silver, `ship-69` Trelawney, `ship-72` Smollett,
  `ship-139` Trelawney, `ship-140` Silver, and `ship-141` Smollett
- Paths: Rogue Tide (P6), Venture Passage (P2), and Marque Marine (P5)
- Hull: 400 displacement tons / approximately 5,600 m³; streamlined
- Shared drives: Jump D / Jump-2 and maneuver/power H / thrust 4
- Shared protection: four armor points
- Shared support: five fuel processors, repair drones, one 30-ton full hangar,
  one 5- or 10-ton vehicle hangar, and a medical bed
- Early carried craft, records 68/69/72: 30-ton Jason
- Later carried craft, records 139/140/141: 30-ton Ariel

All six records require separate plates because their path finishes, weapons,
occupancy, auxiliary hangars, or generation details differ. Records 68 and 72
are mechanically exterior-equivalent and preserve exact geometry while changing
only Rogue versus Marque construction language.

## Canonical family architecture

The Stevenson is 58.0 m long, 20.0 m wide, and 12.0 m high. A long high-prowed
clipper hull combines a rounded armored command prow, narrow raised dorsal
spine, slightly waisted pressure body, thick integral blade-like shoulder
chines, and a broad low stern over two short under-keel fairings. The tapered
occupied envelope plausibly encloses approximately 5,600 m³.

An eight-pane protected bridge brow crosses the prow. A ten-bay port occupancy
strip may be glazed or shuttered without moving its boundaries. One crew hatch
and docking recess, one 5 m cargo/prize shutter, and one two-half auxiliary bay
frame remain fixed. Trelawney activates both auxiliary halves for 10 tons;
Silver and Smollett activate one half for 5 tons and blank the other.

The closed full-hangar door is 8.6 m wide by 5.4 m high. It clears both the
21.0 × 7.6 × 4.1 m Jason and the 19.0 × 7.8 × 4.8 m Ariel without changing the
parent hull. No carried craft appears outside the ship in a catalog plate.

Exactly four common circular turret socket centers run along the dorsal spine.
Two separate 8 m semi-recessed barbette channels lie at the shoulder/spine
junctions. A barbette is never placed on a turret ring and a turret is never
stretched into a barbette channel.

The internal Jump drive reads through two low longitudinal ribbed field-vane
service housings and one narrow segmented radiator at the stern root. Four
compact maneuver apertures surround two power grilles. A ventral scoop grid,
six recessed landing-pad doors, docking guides, and an armor edge band complete
the chassis. There is no external Jump ring or glow.

### Invariants

Preserve exactly the 58.0 × 20.0 × 12.0 m silhouette; bridge; ten occupancy
bays; crew, cargo, full-hangar, and auxiliary-bay boundaries; four turret socket
centers; two barbette-channel boundaries; Jump service housings; stern, scoop,
landing and docking geometry; armor band; primary seams; camera, crop,
perspective, backdrop, and lighting.

## Production fits

### `ship-68` Silver — early Rogue raider

- Four glazed crew/stateroom bays and six deceptive barracks shutters
- One active 5-ton auxiliary half; Jason in the closed full hangar
- Forward triple turret: two sandcasters and one beam laser
- Second triple turret: two missile racks and one beam laser
- Two aft turret covers closed; both particle-beam barbettes active
- Aubergine `#4D294F`, hot vermilion `#D94B35`, mustard `#D4A62A`,
  blackened chrome, false seams, replacement panels, and subdued sheen

### `ship-72` Smollett — early Marque escort raider

- Exact ship-68 equipment and opening geometry
- Emerald `#13705B`, cream `#EFE0B5`, burgundy `#8D2E3C`, gold-toned
  chrome, bright steel ribs, and boarding handholds

### `ship-69` Trelawney — early Venture passenger ship

- All ten occupancy bays glazed; both auxiliary halves active for 10 tons;
  Jason in the closed full hangar
- Four triple turrets: two beam-laser, one sandcaster, and one missile
- Both barbette channels inactive
- Sunflower `#F2C230`, cobalt `#174A8B`, signal orange `#E96D2F`, and
  bright protected chrome

### `ship-139` Trelawney — later Venture yacht

- Early Trelawney hull and two forward triple beam turrets retained
- Third ring: double beam-laser/missile turret; fourth: double beam-laser/
  sandcaster turret; both barbettes inactive
- Ten glazed bays with two warmer private/lounge panes; Ariel hangar
- Self-sealing seam gaskets, holographic-control sensor inset, and higher polish

### `ship-140` Silver — later Rogue boarding raider

- Six glazed occupancy bays and four boarding shutters; Ariel hangar
- Four turrets: two single particle-beam, one double beam/missile, and one
  double beam/sandcaster
- Both barbette channels closed and visibly blanked
- Rogue palette with self-sealing gaskets and brighter theatrical refit panels

### `ship-141` Smollett — later Marque light escort

- Four glazed bays and six escort/barracks shutters; Ariel hangar
- Two forward double turrets: beam/missile and beam/sandcaster
- Two aft turret covers closed; both particle-beam barbettes active
- Exactly three small point-defense laser nodes on port prow, lower waist, and
  aft shoulder; four weeks power endurance
- Marque palette with self-sealing gaskets and formal later-service finish

Turret apertures follow the global component bible: beam lasers use narrow
optics, missiles use closed shutters, sandcasters use blunt projectors, and
turret-scale particle beams use short heavy insulated emitters. The two long
barbettes are visibly larger, deeper installations.

## Production prompts

### Neutral anchor

```text
Create one neutral Stevenson anchor, a 400-displacement-ton streamlined Jump-2
clipper exactly 58 x 20 x 12 m. Use a rounded high command prow, narrow raised
dorsal spine, waisted pressure body, integral blade-like shoulder chines, broad
stern, and two short under-keel fairings. Put an eight-pane bridge, exact ten-
bay port occupancy strip, crew hatch and docking recess, 5 m cargo shutter,
closed 8.6 x 5.4 m full-hangar door, and a two-half auxiliary bay frame.
Reserve exactly four circular dorsal turret sockets and exactly two separate
elongated ribbed barbette channels. Add two aft Jump service vanes, one radiator
band, four maneuver apertures, two power grilles, scoop, six landing doors, and
armor edge band. Neutral aluminum, 1970s/1980s gouache-and-airbrush, complete
front-port three-quarter, 3:2, charcoal stars and teal grid. No paint, weapon,
open door, craft, tank, Jump ring, glow, wing, gear, text, or franchise styling.
```

### Shared edit rule

```text
Preserve exactly every anchor silhouette, opening, four turret centers, two
barbette channels, Jump and stern landmark, seam, camera, crop, backdrop, and
lighting coordinate. Change only stated glazing, auxiliary-bay half, installed
weapons, self-sealing or sensor details, path trim, and livery. Never combine
two turrets on one ring or place a turret weapon in a barbette channel.
```

### Variant sequence

```text
Silver 68: activate two forward mixed triple turrets and both particle-beam
barbettes; close two aft rings; use four glazed bays, one auxiliary half, and
Rogue colors.

Smollett 72: preserve Silver 68 equipment exactly; change only to Marque colors,
gold/steel collars, orderly panels, and boarding handholds.

Trelawney 69: glaze ten bays, activate both auxiliary halves, populate four
turret rings with two triple lasers, one triple sandcaster, and one shuttered
triple missile turret; close both barbette channels; use Venture colors.

Trelawney 139: retain two forward laser turrets; replace aft pair with double
beam/missile and beam/sand turrets; add two warmer private panes, self-sealing
gaskets, holographic inset, and polished later Venture finish.

Silver 140: close both barbette channels; populate four rings with two small
single-particle turrets and two mixed doubles; glaze six bays; add self-sealing
gaskets and later Rogue finish.

Smollett 141: retain both barbettes; use two forward mixed double turrets and
two aft covers; add exactly three tiny point-defense nodes, self-sealing gaskets,
and later Marque finish.
```

## Production sequence and provenance

The artwork was generated for Cepheus Trader on 2026-08-18 with OpenAI image
generation. Every fit referenced the corrected neutral anchor. Silver 68's
first pass was rejected for combining two turrets on one ring. Trelawney 69's
first pass was corrected to distinguish laser, sandcaster, and missile
apertures. Silver 140's first pass was rejected for leaving a weapon in a
barbette channel. The corrected masters satisfy the invariant socket map.
