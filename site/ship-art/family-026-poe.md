# Poe Family Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 026*

## Mechanical identity

- Family: `family-26`, Poe
- Members: `ship-26` Pym and `ship-33` Ligeia
- Hull: 100 displacement tons / approximately 1,400 m³
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11
- Shared protection: four points of crystaliron armor
- Shared Jump plant: drive A, Jump-2, one jump, two weeks of power endurance
- Shared combat fit: one triple turret with missile rack, sandcaster, and beam
  laser; twelve missiles and twenty sand canisters
- Shared control: Model/3 computer, holographic controls, basic military
  electronics, and a four-person crew
- Pym path: Path 2, Venture Passage Works
- Ligeia path: Path 6, Rogue Tide Yards

This is one hundred-ton courier hull in public dispatch and covert-service
fits. The outer pressure body, bridge, turret center, Jump-service landmarks,
main aperture grid, landing arrangement, and drive-block boundaries remain
fixed. Ligeia's greater thrust and specialized equipment alter fittings inside
those boundaries rather than stretching or replacing the family hull.

## Canonical family architecture

The Poe hull is 31.0 m long, 14.0 m wide, and 7.6 m high, with two occupied
decks. It is a broad clipped lozenge: a faceted command prow flows into a
full-width armored waist, then tapers around two integrated aft drive shoulders.
Rounded bevels and a shallow lifting belly make it atmospheric without turning
it into an aircraft. The tapered, partly hollowed envelope plausibly encloses
approximately 1,400 m³.

A protected five-port bridge blister sits high in the prow. One rectangular
port personnel airlock occupies the forward waist. Four compact stateroom-
window bays lie in a reserved upper-side strip; Pym glazes two and Ligeia
three, leaving unused boundaries blank. A single 8.0 × 3.4 m aft-port
service aperture establishes the cargo/hangar grid. One circular dorsal turret
socket lies over the center of mass. The turret contains three clearly
different, compact apertures: one shuttered missile cell, one sandcaster mouth,
and one dark beam-laser lens, with no long gun barrels.

The Jump drive is internal. Its shared external landmarks are two low
longitudinal service bulges on the upper engineering shoulders, each carrying
three narrow heat-rejection slats and one dark-chrome access collar. These are
maintenance and thermal structures, not glowing engines or rings. They stay
identical on both ships because both carry the same drive A and Jump-2 fit.

Two aft maneuver housings share one fixed outer outline. Pym fits one medium
rectangular field aperture inside each. Ligeia's drive B uses larger apertures
and paired auxiliary vector ports inside the same housings. A central ribbed
power grille, six flush landing-pad doors, lower intake-grid boundaries, major
armor seams, and bright-metal datum edges complete the invariant chassis.

### Chassis invariants

Both ships preserve exactly:

- the 31.0 × 14.0 × 7.6 m two-deck clipped-lozenge silhouette;
- faceted command prow, five-port bridge blister, and forward port airlock;
- four stateroom-window boundaries and the 8.0 × 3.4 m aft-port service
  aperture;
- dorsal turret socket and one three-aperture mixed turret;
- paired upper-shoulder Jump-service bulges, six slats, and two access collars;
- maneuver-housing outer boundaries, central power grille, six landing-pad
  doors, lower intake-grid boundaries, armor seams, camera, and lighting; and
- absence of an external Jump ring, exposed fuel tank, separate aircraft wing,
  second turret, projecting gun barrel, or additional hull volume.

## Variant deltas

### `ship-26` Pym

- Path 2, Venture Passage; dispatch courier; maneuver A / thrust 2
- Two staterooms, four-place galley, and 33 tons cargo; no fuel processor or
  carried boat
- Glaze exactly two stateroom-window bays and leave two reserved bays
  as flush blank panels
- Use the full shared aft-port aperture as one large closed freight shutter with
  a protected low sill
- Fit medium maneuver apertures, one per fixed aft housing; leave lower
  processor-intake boundaries closed
- Finish: sunflower `#F2C230`, cobalt `#174A8B`, signal orange `#E96D2F`, and
  bright chrome; prosperous but frontier-protected

### `ship-33` Ligeia

- Path 6, Rogue Tide; covert courier; maneuver B / thrust 4; stealth hull
- Three staterooms, fuel processing, one six-ton hangar, and 14 tons cargo
- Glaze exactly three stateroom-window bays behind dark flush covers;
  leave the fourth boundary blank
- Divide only the shared aft service aperture into a 5.0 m closed cargo shutter
  and a 3.0 m ribbed hangar shutter; do not add another bay or enlarge the hull
- Open two flush lower fuel-processing intake grids
- Enlarge the two maneuver apertures and add two auxiliary vector ports inside
  each unchanged aft housing
- Retain the shared mixed turret and add exactly one small flush point-defense
  laser node on the dorsal aft quarter, with one dark lens and no barrel
- Apply low-reflectivity stealth panels without modern monochrome styling:
  aubergine `#4D294F`, hot vermilion `#D94B35`, mustard `#D4A62A`, and blackened
  chrome; use false seams and mismatched replaceable panels within the invariant
  structural grid
