# Homeric Family Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 080*

## Mechanical identity

- Family: `family-80`, Homeric
- Members: `ship-80`, `ship-82`, `ship-83`, `ship-86`, `ship-88`, and
  `ship-152` through `ship-157`
- Paths: Concord Exchange (P1), Outer Reach (P3), Marque Marine (P5), and
  Tempest Arsenal (P9)
- Hull: 800 displacement tons / approximately 11,200 m³; standard
- Dimensions: 72.0 m long, 24.0 m wide, and 18.0 m high
- Common fit: TL11, self-sealing, Jump drive J / Jump-2, fuel scoop, no armor
- Ordinary drive fit: maneuver J and power J / thrust 2
- Cyclops drive fit: maneuver L and power L / thrust 3

The source-derived descriptions for records 80 and 86 formerly called the
parent hull streamlined although their construction records specify the
`standard` configuration. The normalized construction is authoritative. The
descriptions were corrected and the family is depicted as a blunt,
non-aerodynamic standard hull.

The eleven records require ten plates. Records 80 and 152 have the same visible
module, armament, clamp, and craft fit, so they use one exact Ithaca plate.
Their electronics, computer options, fuel processors, and internal cargo
allocation do not change the exterior.

## Canonical architecture

The Homeric is one integrated rectangular pressure hull, not a distributed
frame. A blunt stepped command block fills the forward quarter. A long central
dorsal spine and twin protected load rails lead to a broad aft engineering
block. The full bounding box encloses approximately 11,200 m³ once the inset
waist, service channels, and corner cuts are allowed for.

Four rectangular module faces are visible on the port side and four matching
faces lie on the hidden starboard side. The forward pair is always the
docking/service interface. The three aft pairs are the six convertible
positions used for freight, occupancy, cold-sleep, extra fuel, or missile-bank
fits. The eight frames, centers, structural rails, and door outlines never
move. Module contents may change; module geometry may not.

Two common dorsal turret centers serve the ordinary mixed triple mounts. Six
small circular weapon inserts sit at the upper edges of the six convertible
module positions. Two separate longitudinal armored channels in the dorsal
spine accept particle-beam barbettes. The ordinary fits close all module-edge
inserts and both barbette channels. Armed Odysseus closes the common mixed-
turret centers, activates the six module-edge inserts, and activates both
barbette channels.

The internal Jump drive has no exterior ring and emits no decorative glow. Its
recognition landmarks are a segmented radiator/service strip, two flush coil-
service blisters, and repeatable access panels around the aft spine. The
ventral field grid doubles as the standard hull's fuel-scoop intake. Thrust-3
Cyclops fits enlarge only the dark openings inside the inherited stern drive
boundaries.

The port forward service root accepts either a 90-ton-rated clamp for the
23.5 m Albatross or a compact 30-ton clamp for the 21.0 m Jason and 18.8 m
Wayfarer Cargo. The craft is visibly outside the pressure hull with a readable
mechanical connection. No Homeric record has a hangar.

### Invariants

Preserve exactly:

- the 72.0 × 24.0 × 18.0 m integrated standard-hull silhouette;
- stepped command block, central spine, protected rails, and aft block;
- four visible plus four hidden module frames, with the forward pair reserved
  for service and the aft three pairs reserved for six convertible modules;
- two common turret centers, six module-edge inserts, and two independent
  barbette channels;
- radiator, two coil-service blisters, scoop grid, stern boundaries, landing
  doors, main seams, camera, crop, backdrop, and lighting; and
- the absence of a smooth aerodynamic nose, distributed truss voids, exterior
  Jump ring, free-standing fuel tank, unrecorded hangar, or unrecorded weapon.

## Production fits

### Ithaca — records 80 and 152

All six aft module faces are closed cargo shutters. Two mixed triple turrets
occupy the common dorsal centers. One 40-ton Albatross is secured to the Clamp
90 root. Concord finish: warm ivory, royal blue, vermilion, and polished chrome.

### Ithaca — record 86

Retain the same cargo shutters and two mixed triple turrets. Remove the clamp
and craft and close the forward service root with inherited hull plating.

### Odysseus — record 82

Retain Ithaca's cargo, weapon, clamp, and Albatross geometry exactly. Apply the
Marque palette: deep emerald, warm cream, burgundy, gold-toned chrome, and
bright protected metal rails.

### Scheria — record 83

Fit the six convertible positions with rugged colonial accommodation:
disciplined small window strips, life-support grilles, baggage doors, and
embarkation hatches. Retain two mixed triple turrets and carry one Jason on a
30-ton clamp. Use the Concord palette.

### Calypso — record 153

Use one visible cargo shutter and two visible protected fuel-service faces;
their hidden mates complete the extended-range allocation. Fuel faces use
ribbed plates, inspection disks, and service couplings inside the inherited
frames, never detachable tanks. Retain two mixed triple turrets and carry one
Wayfarer Cargo. Outer Reach finish: avocado, ochre, brick red, and brushed or
patch-polished aluminum.

### Nausithous — record 154

Glaze the six convertible positions with regular modest passenger-window bands
and retain life-support grilles and embarkation doors. Retain two mixed triple
turrets and carry one Wayfarer Cargo. Use a polished Concord finish.

### Scheria — record 155

Treat the six convertible positions as sealed low-berth modules with insulated
access plates, coolant couplings, cryogenic-service grilles, and status lights;
use few or no passenger windows. Retain two mixed triple turrets and one
Wayfarer Cargo. Use a hard-service Concord finish.

### Cyclops — records 88 and 157

Keep the forward module pair as yellow-and-blue docking service. Convert the
three visible aft faces into deep violet armored missile-bank shutters; the
three hidden partners complete exactly six banks. The shutters represent banks,
not individual missiles. Retain two mixed triple defensive turrets and the
thrust-3 stern apertures. Record 88 carries Jason; record 157 carries Wayfarer
Cargo. Tempest finish: safety yellow, ultramarine, vivid violet, orange-red
hazards, black edging, and bright chrome.

### Odysseus — record 156

Keep six cargo shutters. Activate one double turret at each of the six
convertible module-edge inserts: two beam pairs, two missile pairs, and two
sandcaster pairs. The front-port plate shows one pair of each; the mirrored
starboard fits are occluded. Close the forward service insert. Activate both
longitudinal dorsal channels as separate fixed particle-beam barbettes and do
not retain the ordinary mixed turrets. Carry one Wayfarer Cargo. Use the Marque
palette.

Use a complete front-port three-quarter view from approximately 12 degrees
above, restrained near-orthographic perspective, 3:2 landscape. Light with a
warm upper-front-port key and weak cool aft rim against charcoal stars and a
faint teal plotting grid. Render as original late-1970s/early-1980s gouache,
airbrush, and painted-miniature technical art with saturated enamel and
selective chrome. No text, logo, watermark, weapon fire, planet, motion blur,
modern gray product rendering, or recognizable franchise styling.

## Production sequence and review notes

1. Approve the neutral standard-hull anchor and immutable eight-frame grid.
2. Produce the shared external Ithaca, then derive record 86 by removing only
   the clamp and craft; derive record 82 by changing only path finish.
3. Produce fuel, colonial, passenger, and low-berth module treatments without
   changing any frame center or hull volume.
4. Produce one six-bank Cyclops, then change only its carried craft for the
   later record.
5. Produce armed Odysseus last, using the established module inserts and
   separate barbette channels rather than inventing hardpoints.

Review corrections removed surplus turret barrels and mounts from Nausithous,
low-berth Scheria, and Cyclops; restored Cyclops' forward service face after an
early pass treated all four visible faces as missile banks; and closed armed
Odysseus' forward service insert after an early pass armed all four visible
faces. The approved armed plate retains two fore-and-aft dorsal barbette channel
housings rather than the later rejected edit that collapsed them into one.
