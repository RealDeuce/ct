## Canonical architecture

The Nightingale is a calm symmetric non-aerodynamic hospital citadel with an
eight-pane command block, two long bands of small ward windows, broad shoulders,
and a square engineering block. Five visible hardpoint centers have five
mirrored partners: three visible dorsal rings and their mirrors carry the six
double sandcasters; two visible and two mirrored rings remain closed. Five
visible and five hidden tiny nodes provide ten-point point-defense coverage.

One closed 9.5 × 5.8 m aft-port hangar shutter and 28 m internal apron serve
the two 26 m medical cutters and 12.5 m launch. The craft are internal. Two
stretcher airlocks, docking collars, an emergency receiving door, instrument
collars, ceramic radomes, and teal ward glass establish clinical scale.

The internal Jump drive appears only as one segmented radiator and two flush
coil-service housings—never a ring or glow. Four maneuver apertures and two
power grilles occupy fixed stern boundaries. Record 93 uses smaller L/L inserts;
record 160 enlarges only the dark openings for P/P.

Preserve the dimensions, hull, bridge, windows, five visible hardpoints, ten
point-defense centers, hangar, medical access, Jump landmarks, stern boundaries,
camera, and lighting. Never add an external craft, second hangar, offensive
weapon, tank, truss, wing, tail, Jump ring, or real-world medical emblem.
