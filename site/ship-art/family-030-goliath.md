# Goliath Family Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 030*

## Mechanical identity

- Family: `family-30`, Goliath
- Members: `ship-30` and `ship-32`
- Shipyard path: Path 7, Admiralty Line Works
- Hull: 90 displacement tons / approximately 1,260 m³
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11
- Shared protection: eight points of crystaliron armor
- Shared performance: `sP` maneuver and power / thrust 6; one-week endurance;
  no Jump drive
- Shared control: four-person control cabin, Model/2 computer, basic military
  electronics, Evade/1, and Fire Control/1
- Shared troop fit: fifty acceleration seats, two compact staterooms, two
  pressure airlocks, and 17.6 tons cargo
- Shared armament: one single turret with one beam laser

The two source records are mechanically identical. They represent the same
assault lander without a refit, block, or shipyard difference and therefore use
one exact exterior plate.

## Canonical family architecture

The Goliath is 30.0 m long, 14.5 m wide, and 7.0 m high with two occupied decks.
It is a broad armored lifting body with a blunt chisel prow, deep troop-bay
waist, shallow landing belly, and two strong aft maneuver shoulders. Its tapered
and bevelled envelope plausibly encloses approximately 1,260 m³. It must read as
a large landing craft rather than a Jump-capable trader or narrow aerospace
transport.

A protected five-port four-person control cabin occupies the upper prow. Two
small crew-stateroom windows sit behind it. The fifty-seat troop bay has
no windows. Exactly two rectangular pressure-airlock doors lie on the visible
port flank, one at each end of the troop bay, each recessed behind an armored
red surround and bordered by bright steel handholds. Between them, one large
closed 6.5 × 3.2 m stores/vehicle ramp serves the 17.6-ton hold; it has a low
reinforced sill and overlapping armored shutter, not a third personnel airlock.

One circular dorsal turret socket lies over the forward half of the troop bay.
It carries a compact single beam-laser turret with one dark optical aperture and
no projecting barrel. Two large hexagonal aft maneuver-field apertures, one
central ribbed power grille, six armored landing-pad doors, paired recovery
hooks, formal datum lines, and overlapping leading-edge armor complete the
chassis. There is no Jump-drive service band, coil blister, field vane, or ring.

### Invariants

Both catalog records preserve exactly:

- the 30.0 × 14.5 × 7.0 m broad armored lifting-body silhouette;
- chisel prow, five-port control cabin, and two crew windows;
- windowless troop bay, exactly two pressure-airlock doors and handhold sets;
- one central closed stores/vehicle ramp, low sill, and armored shutter;
- one dorsal single beam-laser turret with one lens and no barrel;
- paired aft maneuver shoulders and hexagonal apertures, central power grille,
  six landing-pad doors, two recovery hooks, armor seams, and datum lines; and
- camera, crop, perspective, backdrop, lighting, and Admiralty livery.

Do not add a third airlock, passenger window strip, second cargo ramp, second
turret, missile cell, gun barrel, exposed troop, external vehicle, Jump hardware,
external tank, separate aircraft wing, or exposed landing gear.
