## Canonical architecture

The Vauban is a massive coherent atmospheric spear/lozenge, not a scaled-up
fighter. A blunt faceted command prow and recessed eight-pane bridge lead into
wide battery shoulders, a long armored citadel/spine, tapered protected flanks,
a shallow lifting belly, and a deep faired engineering block. Eight-point armor
produces thick leading shoulders, overlapping edge layers, and deeply recessed
bridge, drives, bays, and magazines.

Ten capital bays form five visible/hidden pairs. The standard port view shows
two deep meson-gun apertures, two long particle-beam channels, and one enormous
100-ton torpedo shutter; their five mirrors account for four meson, four
particle, and two torpedo bays. Bay architecture remains materially larger than
turrets and shapes the primary hull.

Fifteen triple turrets follow the camera visibility rule rather than appearing
all at once. Eight are visible: three forward beam triples, three amidships
missile triples, and two aft sandcaster triples. Seven hidden far/ventral mounts
complete two beam, two missile, and three sand triples, for five of each type.
Thirteen tiny visible PD lenses and twelve hidden partners total twenty-five.

A common 12 × 7 m aft-port hangar outer frame has an approximately 30 m
internal axis. Citadel uses a broad two-lane 100-ton door for its 26 m Proteus
Cargo and 17.5 m Caduceus. Ravelin fits a central armored mullion and two narrow
inner shutters for the 18.6 m Grapnel and 17.5 m Caduceus in a tighter 50-ton
bay. Every craft remains internal.
