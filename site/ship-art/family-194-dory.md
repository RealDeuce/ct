# Dory Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 194*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-194`; `ship-194` Dory
- Hull: 20 displacement tons / approximately 280 m³
- Dimensions: 14.5 m long, 7.5 m wide, and 4.5 m high
- Configuration: standard; technology level 9; standard design
- Protection: no armor
- Performance: `sA` maneuver and power / thrust 1; one-week endurance
- Control: one pilot in a two-person control cabin; Model/1 computer
- Payload: 10.9 tons internal cargo; no external load
- Armament: none; one unused hardpoint/fire-control station
- Limitations: no Jump drive, scoop, processor, passenger cabin, or carried
  craft

## Independent Concord cargo scow

Dory is a 14.5 × 7.5 × 4.5 m non-streamlined utility launch. A blunt low
hexagonal control cab, huge rectangular cargo cassette, shallow structural
belly, and short aft drive block form a simple port-service scow rather than an
aerodynamic boat. Two broad forward panes and one narrow port corner pane serve
the two-place control cabin. One small closed hatch gives cab access.

The central cassette holds 10.9 tons / approximately 153 m³ of payload, more
than half the craft's displacement. One broad closed two-leaf port shutter,
about 7.5 × 2.8 m, occupies most of its visible face. Its rectangular collar,
six flush landing-pad door seams, commercial sensor strips, and clean service
spacing support ship-to-shore lighterage. No cargo is exposed.

## Empty hardpoint

One 3–4 m circular mounting ring lies on the dorsal centerline behind the cab.
It remains under a smooth flush metal cover because the one fire-control
station is unused. Never add a turret cup, emitter, missile shutter, sandcaster,
PD node, or decorative barrel. With only one hardpoint, a distributed coverage
map does not apply; the sealed ring coordinate nevertheless remains invariant.

## Drives and fuel recognition

Two small recessed aft field collars and one compact ribbed power grille identify
the `sA`/`sA` one-gravity stern. Only 0.4 tons of internal fuel supports one
week. Dory has no Jump landmark, fuel scoop, processor grille, external tank,
or flame. Landing-pad doors remain flush and closed.

## Concord finish

Concord Exchange uses warm ivory `#F1E3C2`, royal blue `#2354A3`, vermilion
`#E44B2D`, and polished aluminum/chrome. Ivory dominates; a blue cargo/service
band crosses the broad shutter; vermilion marks the cab brow, shutter collar,
hatch, belly edge, and restrained drive details; chrome protects corners,
collars, the hardpoint cover, and stern. Bright clean period color makes a
cheap utility craft look maintained rather than militarized.

## Invariants

Preserve the 14.5 × 7.5 × 4.5 m hull; three-pane two-place cab; closed access
hatch; exact cargo cassette, two-leaf shutter, and collar; one covered dorsal
hardpoint; commercial sensors; two field collars; one power grille; six landing
doors; belly; principal seams; camera; crop; backdrop; and lighting.

Never install a weapon, open the cargo door or cab hatch, expose payload, add a
windowed passenger module, scoop, processor, Jump hardware, external tank,
extra pod, craft, wing, fin, glow, flame, text, logo, or watermark.
