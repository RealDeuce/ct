# Marque Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production singleton family 074*

## Mechanical identity

- Record: `ship-74` Marque
- Path: Marque Marine Yards (P5)
- Hull: 400 displacement tons / approximately 5,600 m³; streamlined
- Dimensions: 61.0 m long, 21.0 m wide, and 13.5 m high
- Fit: TL12, four armor points, Jump D / Jump-2, maneuver and power H /
  thrust 4, three weeks endurance, advanced electronics, and Model 3 bis/fib
- Weapons: one particle-beam barbette, one triple beam-laser turret, two
  missile/missile/sand triple turrets, and four point-defense gatling lasers
- Mission spaces: six marines, armory, briefing room, office, two workshops,
  two-bed medical bay, ATV and air-raft garages, 16 tons cargo, and one 30-ton
  full hangar containing Grapnel

Marque is a separate escort-brig family. It must not reuse the 58 m Stevenson
clipper or 64 m Hawkwood clipper-citadel, though all three share Marque Marine's
finish, protected boarding vocabulary, and bright-metal component language.

## Canonical architecture

The hull is a 61.0 × 21.0 × 13.5 m boarding-pennant clipper. A long faceted
six-pane command prow rises over a deep armored keel. Swept-back integral
shoulder rails protect a narrow recessed boarding waist; a broad port launch
shoulder balances a mostly occluded starboard troop shoulder. A short squared
engineering stern completes the multi-deck pressure body.

One closed 10.6 × 5.8 m two-leaf full-hangar shutter opens from the port launch
shoulder into an approximately 20 m internal craft axis. It clears the approved
18.6 × 9.4 × 4.8 m Grapnel without exposing the boat in the catalog plate. A
4.5 × 3.2 m ATV garage, 3.2 × 2.2 m air-raft garage, and 4 m cargo shutter are
three separate lower-waist openings. One shielded boarding airlock has a
bright-metal docking collar and fold-flat handholds. Exactly three of five
stateroom windows appear on the port side; the remaining two are occluded.
Marine berthing and work spaces remain windowless.

The four hardpoint installations deliberately cover different arcs rather than
forming a dorsal row:

1. One visible lower-forward port-cheek particle-beam barbette occupies a 6.5 m
   insulated channel.
2. One visible dorsal-aft triple-beam turret has exactly three narrow emitters.
3. One starboard-forward missile/missile/sand triple turret is occluded.
4. One ventral-aft missile/missile/sand triple turret is occluded.

This exposes two of four weapon stations in the standard front-port view while
preserving dorsal, ventral, port, and starboard overlapping fields. Four tiny
point-defense centers cover port prow, dorsal waist, starboard prow, and
ventral stern; the first two are visible and the latter two are occluded.

Four processors use two visible port grilles and two occluded starboard grilles.
The internal Jump drive reads only through two shallow ribbed aft-shoulder coil
service covers and one segmented stern-root radiator/service band. There is no
external ring, glow, or fuel scoop. Four maneuver apertures, armor edge bands,
four landing-pad doors, a repair-drone hatch, and compact sensor fittings
complete the exterior.

### Invariants

Preserve the dimensions, silhouette, six-pane bridge, three visible stateroom
windows, hangar and three smaller door boundaries, airlock, four-arc hardpoint
map, four point-defense centers, processor grilles, Jump-service landmarks,
stern, landing doors, seams, camera, crop, backdrop, and lighting. Never add a
fifth hardpoint, extra visible weapon, second hangar, visible carried craft,

## Finish and catalog plate

Marque Marine uses emerald `#13705B`, cream `#EFE0B5`, burgundy `#8D2E3C`,
gold-toned chrome, and bright steel. Keep the enamel maintained with restrained
boarding and launch-bay wear. The character is a handsome commissioned armed
merchant: dangerous, visibly legitimate, and not a gray fleet warship.

Use a complete front-port three-quarter view about 12 degrees above, restrained
near-orthographic perspective, 3:2 landscape, warm upper-front-port key, cool
aft rim, charcoal stars, and a faint teal grid. Render as original late-1970s/
early-1980s gouache-and-airbrush technical art. No text, logo, watermark,
weapon fire, planet, modern CGI, or recognizable franchise styling.

## Production sequence and provenance

Generated for Cepheus Trader on 2026-08-20 with OpenAI image generation in
built-in mode. The first neutral pass had four visible occupancy panes; a
single-object correction removed the aft pane. A second single-object
correction reduced an oversized dorsal cover to an ordinary triple-turret
socket. The production plate derives from that corrected anchor and uses the
approved Grapnel master only as a Marque Marine finish reference.
