# Themistocles Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production singleton family 110*

## Mechanical identity

- Record: `ship-110` Themistocles; Admiralty Line (P7)
- Hull: 1,800 displacement tons / approximately 25,200 m³; standard;
  104 × 38 × 27 m
- Fit: TL11, armor ten, reinforced structure, protected bridge/drives,
  emergency power, improved magazine, Jump P / Jump-2, T/T thrust 4, four
  weeks endurance, command/hardened holographic bridge, two Model 3 bis/fib
  computers, basic-military electronics, 88 crew, and 2 tons cargo
- Craft: two Wayfarer Cargo boats and one Caduceus launch in an internal
  80-ton full hangar
- Weapons: six particle bays, two railgun bays, four triple-beam, two
  triple-missile, four triple-sandcaster turrets, and eighteen PD lasers

This independent command cruiser is an armored trireme: blunt command prow,
tall eight-pane command ziggurat, three stepped occupied weapon galleries,
deep keel, broad mission citadel, and squared engineering stern. It is not a
stretched Duncan, Salamis, Togo, or Yi.

## Eighteen-coordinate coverage map

Nine hardpoints are visible and nine hidden. Visible bays are particle channels
on the upper-forward, midship-flank, and lower-aft port galleries plus one
extra-long parallel-rail recess on the lower-forward keel. The hidden side
repeats three particle and one railgun bay.

Five visible turrets occupy forward upper crown and forward/mid flank beam
stations, a midship lower-flank missile station, and aft upper/lower sandcaster
stations. Matching hidden stations complete the ten turrets. This map covers
forward, aft, upper, lower, and flank arcs rather than forming a dorsal row.
Eighteen PD coordinates split nine visible/nine hidden. The forward flush disk
and central crown coil-service disk are explicitly unarmed.

The four bays remain hull architecture, much larger than turrets. One closed
15 × 7 m shutter serves three internal craft berths. Four visible banks of
eleven closed escape-pod covers mirror four hidden banks. Six of twelve
stateroom windows show; crew berthing is windowless.

Eight processors split four visible/four hidden beside a protected scoop.
Internal Jump-P machinery uses a service band and six flush coil housings, no
ring or glow. Three visible/three hidden maneuver apertures and separate power
grilles form the T/T stern. Admiralty finish is navy `#203B69`, warm white
`#E8E5D4`, red `#C43C35`, chrome/aluminum, and gunmetal in a complete
front-port late-1970s/early-1980s gouache-and-airbrush technical plate.
