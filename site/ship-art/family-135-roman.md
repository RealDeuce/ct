# Roman Family Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 135*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/members: `family-135`; `ship-135` Scipio, `ship-136` Onager,
  `ship-137` Corvus, and `ship-138` Vesuvius
- Hull: 1,000 displacement tons / approximately 14,000 m³
- Dimensions: 82 m long, 30 m wide, and 19 m high
- Configuration: standard; technology level 11
- Protection: five crystaliron armor points, radiation shielding, self-sealing
  hull, and armored bulkheads around bridge, drives, and magazines
- Shared drives: Jump-K / Jump-2, maneuver S and power S / thrust 5
- Shared endurance: one Jump and four weeks of power-plant fuel
- Shared control: advanced electronics, hardened holographic bridge, two
  fib/bis computers, emergency power, and improved magazine
- Shared support: 104 personnel, scoop, four processors, repair and PD drones,
  two workshops, four-bed medical bay, and one 30-ton full hangar
- Carried craft: one internal 18.8 × 8.4 × 4.0 m Wayfarer Armed (`ship-134`)
- Shared defense: four triple-beam turrets, two mixed missile/missile/sand
  triple turrets, ten laser point-defense nodes, 80 sand canisters, and at
  least 96 turret missiles

The four variants differ only in their 51-ton main-battery bay fit and native
yard finish. All hull, armor, drive, turret, point-defense, hangar, and support
coordinates remain common.

## Canonical chassis

The Roman is a long axial standard-hull destroyer, deliberately distinct from
the shorter Duncan citadel. A narrow blunt armored command prow leads into a
raised centerline spine, two stepped weapon shoulders, a deep crew/mission
block, slightly pinched service waist, and separated square engineering
battery. Repeated transverse frames and strong longitudinal load paths make the
one chassis recognizable under three different shipyard finishes.

A protected bridge brow and paired flush advanced-sensor faces sit inside the
command armor. Five-point armor produces coherent overlapping edge layers,
thick shoulders, protected recesses, and armored datum ribs rather than random
tank plates. The radiation seam band and self-sealing access pattern remain
fixed.

## Capital-bay map

Four 51-ton bay volumes occupy two mirrored shoulder pairs. The port three-
quarter plate exposes exactly two large sockets; their starboard mirrors are
hidden. Each approximately 10 × 4.5 m aperture is multi-deck hull architecture,
far larger and deeper than a turret.

- **Socket A, forward port:** the family-variable bay.
- **Socket B, midship port:** one particle-beam bay on every member.
- **Hidden A/B:** exact starboard mirrors of the visible pair.

Variant allocations are therefore:

| Ship | Visible Socket A | Visible Socket B | Hidden mirrors | Full battery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scipio | Meson gun | Particle beam | One of each | 2 meson + 2 particle |
| Onager | Six-cell torpedo bay | Particle beam | One of each | 2 torpedo + 2 particle |
| Corvus | Particle beam | Particle beam | Two particle | 4 particle |
| Vesuvius | 24-cell missile bank | Particle beam | One of each | 2 missile + 2 particle |

The meson aperture uses a dark non-glowing focusing throat and layered field
vanes. A particle bay uses one deeply recessed insulated axial emitter with
ceramic collars and heat shielding. The torpedo bay has exactly six large
closed shutters in two rows of three. The missile bank has exactly twenty-four
smaller closed shutters in four rows of six. Neither launch fit exposes live
ordnance.

## Turret and point-defense coverage

Six one-ton turret rings are fixed. Exactly three are visible:

- a forward dorsal triple-beam turret;
- an outward-canted port-shoulder mixed triple turret, with two closed missile
  shutters and one blunt sandcaster aperture; and
- an aft dorsal triple-beam turret.

The hidden half is a starboard-shoulder mixed turret plus two beam triples on
far/ventral arcs. Together with the four mostly axial weapon bays, this layout
provides overlapping flank, dorsal, ventral, forward, and aft coverage instead
of stacking the whole battery on top. Preserve the coverage map on every fit.

Ten 0.4–0.8 m laser point-defense nodes occupy five mirrored pairs. The plate
shows exactly five tiny blue-white lenses; the five far/ventral partners are
hidden. They remain much smaller than turret rings.

## Hangar and machinery

One closed 10 × 5.2 m aft-port full-hangar shutter and approximately 23 m
internal axis accept the canonical Wayfarer Armed. The 18.8 m boat remains
inside and does not appear beside the destroyer.

One segmented non-glowing Jump radiator and two flush rectangular field-coil
housings identify Jump-K. A protected ventral scoop and four processor grilles
serve the fuel system. Four recessed maneuver-field apertures and two ribbed
power grilles fit inside the fixed square S/S engineering battery. Emergency-
power access, drone slots, landing doors, and handholds remain common.

## Variant finishes

### Scipio — Admiralty Line Works (P7)

Deep navy `#203B69`, warm white `#E8E5D4`, signal red `#C43C35`, and bright
aluminum. Formal axial color blocks emphasize the meson/particle battle fit and
mass-produced fleet authority.

### Onager and Vesuvius — Tempest Arsenal (P9)

Ultramarine `#3046A6`, violet `#6B3F91`, warning yellow `#F2C84B`, and bright
chrome. Warning yellow concentrates at launch shutters, mixed turrets, and
magazine boundaries. The saturated finish is controlled rather than neon.

### Corvus — Redoubt Shipbuilding (P8)

Fire red `#B83B2E`, orange `#E9782E`, bone `#D8CCA9`, heavy stainless, and
restrained gunmetal. The unchanged chassis reads as a compact protected direct-
fire battery through deep recess shadow and stainless bay collars.

## Invariants

Preserve the 82 × 30 × 19 m hull, command prow, spine, shoulder, mission,
waist and engineering volumes; bridge and sensors; armor/radiation boundaries;
four bay socket coordinates; six turret coordinates and coverage arcs; ten PD
coordinates; hangar outer frame; Jump, scoop, processor, maneuver, power,
emergency, drone and landing stations; principal seams; camera; crop; backdrop;
and lighting.

Never move all turrets to the crown, expose more than three turrets or two bays
in the standard view, show the Wayfarer outside, open the hangar, expose a
missile or torpedo, add a barbette, wing, fin, external tank, glowing Jump ring,
flame, text, logo, or watermark.

## Catalog plate and reusable prompts

Use a complete front-port three-quarter view about 12 degrees above centerline,
restrained near-orthographic perspective, 3:2 landscape, warm upper-front-port
key, weak cool aft rim, charcoal sparse starfield, and faint teal plotting grid.
Render original late-1970s/early-1980s gouache-and-airbrush technical art with
bright opaque enamel and metal highlights. No planet, motion blur, modern gray
rendering, or recognizable franchise design.

```text
external craft, open door, paint, text, or glow.

Scipio fit: preserve every anchor coordinate. Fit visible Socket A as meson and
Socket B as particle, with mirrored hidden partners. Fit three visible turrets:
forward/aft beam triples and one canted port-shoulder mixed triple; activate
five tiny PD lenses. Close the hangar and apply Admiralty navy, white, red, and
aluminum. Remove any accidental unringed weapon housings.

Onager fit: edit Scipio only inside Socket A and path finish. Replace meson with
six large closed torpedo shutters, preserve Socket B particle channel and every
turret/PD/hangar/machinery station, and apply Tempest ultramarine, violet,
warning yellow, and chrome.

Corvus fit: edit Scipio only inside Socket A and path finish. Replace meson with
a second matched particle channel, preserve all common stations, and apply

Preserve the Tempest finish and every other feature.
```
