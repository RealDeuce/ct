# Bulwark Family Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 121*

- Parent dimensions: 62.0 m long, 24.0 m wide, and 16.0 m high

## Canonical parent architecture

The Bulwark is a compact axial escort with a blunt faceted hardened command
prow, deeply recessed bridge brow, broad protected shoulders, short dense waist,
and squared high-thrust engineering block. Its 62 × 24 × 16 m envelope is
approximately 29 percent filled, leaving plausible internal volume for drives,
fuel, hangar clearances, armor, and the conversion battery.

A 14 × 7 m forward-center reserve battery block is primary structure. Curtain
keeps its upper cover closed around an internal/hidden second barbette station.
Keep opens the same block into a multi-deck particle-beam channel without
changing the outer silhouette. One separate 7 × 4 m dorsal barbette socket lies
aft of the block and remains at the same coordinate on both plates.

One closed 9.6 × 4.8 m standard-hangar shutter on the visible port flank gives
tight clearance to the canonical 18.8 × 8.4 × 4.0 m Wayfarer Boarding boat. The
craft remains inside. One small shared cargo shutter lies forward of the hangar.
An aft-waist modular panel carries Curtain's Jump-service landmarks or Keep's
additional cargo access.

Four large recessed maneuver apertures and two ribbed power grilles communicate
the Q/Q drive fit. A ventral scoop, eight processor grilles, landing-pad doors,
redundant sensor cheeks, bright datum rails, and accessible seams are invariant.

## Shared weapon map

The port three-quarter plate shows exactly two of three turrets: one beam triple
and the one mixed triple. The second beam triple is hidden on the far/ventral
side. Exactly three tiny PD lenses are visible and two are hidden. A PD fixture
remains less than one quarter of a turret-ring diameter.

The beam turret has three narrow optical apertures. The mixed turret has two
closed gridded missile shutters and one short blunt multi-port sandcaster. No
missile is exposed.

Curtain shows one particle-beam barbette in the shared aft socket; its second
barbette is hidden, so one of two is visible. Keep retains that one visible
barbette but has no hidden second barbette. Its forward particle bay is an
architectural multi-deck channel rather than another turret or barbette.

## Variant deltas

### `ship-121` Curtain

- One armor layer / four points: one continuous protective edge band and
  recessed guards, without deep battleship plating
- Closed forward reserve battery block; one visible and one hidden particle
  barbette
- Active segmented non-glowing Jump radiator and two flush coil housings in the
  aft-waist panel for Jump-G
- Four-ton hold behind the shared small shutter
- Admiralty deep navy `#203B69`, warm white `#E8E5D4`, signal red `#C43C35`,
  and bright aluminum

### `ship-122` Keep

- Three armor layers / twelve points: two added nested protective bands and
  deeper component recesses inside the same outer outline
- Forward reserve block opened into one 50-ton particle-beam bay: long recessed
  channel, overlapping shutters, broad dark emitter aperture, insulated rails,
  deep load paths, and orange heat shielding
- One particle barbette only, visible in the inherited aft socket
- No Jump drive: radiator and both coil landmarks are replaced by flush armored
  cargo-service plates; additional closed access represents the 50.9-ton hold
- Redoubt fire red `#B83B2E`, orange `#E9782E`, bone `#D8CCA9`, heavy
  stainless, and restrained gunmetal/chrome

## Invariants

Preserve the 62 × 24 × 16 m silhouette, bridge and command prow, reserve-block
boundary, aft barbette center, two visible and one hidden turret centers, three
visible/two hidden PD positions, hangar and small cargo shutter, modular aft-
waist panel, scoop/processors, engineering block, landing and sensor geometry,
main seams, camera, crop, backdrop, and lighting.

Never add a third visible turret, second visible barbette, second bay, external
Wayfarer, open hangar, visible missile, glowing Jump ring, wing, tail, mast, or
unrelated hull redesign. Keep has no external Jump landmark of any kind.

2. Derive Curtain with the closed block, Jump landmarks, and Admiralty fit.
3. Derive Keep from both references; change only bay, Jump/cargo, armor, and
