# Midway Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production singleton family 115*

## Mechanical identity

- Record: `ship-115` Midway; Admiralty Line (P7)
- Carrier hull: 2,000 displacement tons / approximately 28,000 m³; close
  structure; 126 × 58 × 38 m before carried craft
- Loaded combination: 3,800 tons with six externally clamped 300-ton Monitor
  gunboats; the Y/Y/Y drives and Jump-2 fuel are sized against that load
- Fit: TL10, armor two, radiation shielded, self-sealing, basic military
  electronics, command/hardened holographic bridge, three fib Model/2 computers
- Performance: Jump-2, thrust 2 loaded, two weeks endurance
- Complement: 151 crew, eighteen staterooms, nineteen crew berthings, nineteen
  heads, extensive galley/recreation space, medical and operating facilities,
  workshops, armories, offices, briefing rooms, brig, gyms, emergency berths,
  repair drones, and 188.75 tons cargo
- Craft: six Monitors on 300-ton external clamps; two Archimedes work pods and
  two Wayfarer Cargo boats inside one closed 80-ton hangar
- Battery: five triple-railgun, five triple-beam, five triple-missile, and five
  triple-sandcaster turrets; ten minigun and ten laser PD nodes
- Ammunition: 450 railgun rounds, 180 missiles, and 300 sand canisters

Midway is a compact “fleet hive”: a blunt command prow, buried command citadel,
stepped service spine, deep hangar/workshop belly, adjacent armored machinery
blocks, and twin stern drive masses all feed one dense close-structure load
path. It is not a smooth liner, a stretched Hannibal, or an open truss carrier.

## External Monitor flotilla

The carrier's six Monitors are a second hull mass, not decorative pods. Each is
44 × 22 × 15 m and retains family 070's vertical shield prow, buried brow,
offset particle-bay shoulder, rectangular radiation-shielded citadel, and short
stern. Three complete Redoubt-red craft occupy visible high-port, mid-port, and
low-port clamp cradles, parallel to Midway and separated from it by capture
collars, umbilicals, and dark release gaps. Three matching starboard craft are
fully occluded. Their combined 1,800 tons explains Midway's loaded proportions.

The visible gunboats retain Redoubt fire red `#B83B2E`, orange `#E9782E`, bone
`#D8CCA9`, stainless, and gunmetal. Midway remains Admiralty navy `#203B69`,
warm white `#E8E5D4`, signal red `#C43C35`, chrome/aluminum, and gunmetal, so
carrier and flotilla remain visually independent. The Archimedes and Wayfarer
craft are internal behind two closed lower-belly shutters.

## Battery, service, and coverage

The front-port plate exposes about half the twenty carrier turrets. Full-size
triple mounts occupy separated forward-dorsal, cheek, mid-dorsal, port-flank,
lower-edge, aft-quarter, and ventral positions. Rail, beam, closed missile, and
sandcaster faces are visually distinct. Far-side stations complete five of
each type. The arrangement favors overlapping arcs and release-lane protection,
not a single dorsal battery row.

Ten of twenty tiny PD nodes are visible: five short minigun clusters and five
cyan optical heads spread around the prow, shoulders, flank, belly, clamp
approaches, and stern. They remain much smaller than turret rings. Clamp
fittings, processor doors, sensors, glazing, hangar shutters, and service
grilles are unarmed.

Fifteen processor access/cooling fields repeat along the service spine. Jump-Y
machinery remains internal; only broad integrated aft radiator/service bands
and recessed coil-service housings identify it externally. There is no Jump
ring, portal, halo, glow, or external tank. Four visible maneuver apertures and
four hidden starboard apertures serve the loaded thrust-two plant.

## Production and provenance

Generated 2026-08-21 with OpenAI image generation in built-in mode. The first
anchor established the close-structure carrier but underscaled its gunboats.
A scoped anchor correction replaced them with three substantial Monitor-derived
casemates. Production fitted Admiralty colors and the distributed battery; one
color-only correction restored Redoubt red across the three detached craft.
The prompts remain here for reuse.
