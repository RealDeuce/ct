# Leviathan Family Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 126*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/members: `family-126`; `ship-126` Leviathan and `ship-127` Behemoth
- Shipyard path: Path 1, Concord Exchange Yards
- Hull: 1,900 displacement tons / approximately 26,600 m³
- Dimensions: 112 m long, 36 m wide, and 23 m high
- Configuration: distributed; technology level 11
- Protection: one point of titanium-steel armor, radiation shielding, and
  self-sealing construction
- Common drive: maneuver K / thrust 1; four-week endurance and emergency power
- Common working fit: ten fuel processors, repair drones, workshop, 40 tons of
  external craft, and two 30-ton docking clamps
- Common defense: six double beam-laser turrets, two mixed missile/sandcaster
  double turrets, 24 missiles, and 40 sandcaster canisters
- Carried craft: one 8.4 m Archimedes work pod and one 19.5 m Nausicaa ship's
  boat, both externally clamped

Leviathan installs Jump-Q and power-Q machinery for Jump-2 and retains 1,076.9
tons of cargo. Behemoth removes the Jump installation, uses power K, and fills
the recovered volume with 1,581.4 tons of cargo for orbital and in-system work.

## Shared canonical frame

The family uses a long, low open box-truss structure rather than an enclosing
shell. A blunt forward command pod joins paired longitudinal keels, regular
crossmembers, and standardized rectangular pressure-freight modules. Broad
ribbed shutters, chrome module collars, deep service gaps, visible load paths,
catwalks, handrails, and cable runs keep the distributed construction legible.
A compact common maneuver/power pod terminates the aft frame.

One large mid-aft strategic bay has fixed boundaries and common interfaces. It
accepts either the Leviathan Jump installation or the Behemoth freight pair;
the surrounding keels, crossmembers, module positions, access routes, and aft
pod do not move between variants.

Exactly two 30-ton clamp saddles sit on the visible lower port rail. The forward
saddle carries Archimedes and the aft saddle carries Nausicaa. Preserve their
published shapes and 8.4-to-19.5 m scale relationship; the clamp jaws must
visibly meet each craft rather than leaving it free-flying. There is no hangar.

Eight one-ton double-turret rings are shared. The standard port three-quarter
plate exposes exactly four: three paired-beam cups and one mixed cup with one
closed missile shutter and one blunt sandcaster aperture. The remaining four
mounts are hidden on the far and ventral sides. No point-defense nodes are fit.

Ten modest processor grilles serve the common fuel plant. A distributed hull
cannot mount a fuel scoop, so no scoop or intake appears on either variant.

## Variant recognition

### Leviathan — interstellar bulk carrier

The strategic bay contains one long segmented, non-glowing radiator folded
along the inner upper frame, two stout chrome-collared field-coil housings,
armored cable trunks, and compact fuel/service tanks. These components identify
the Jump-Q installation without becoming a glowing ring or another engine.
The aft Q-rated power grille is the larger of the two family fits.

### Behemoth — in-system bulk hauler

Every Jump-specific component is absent. Two enormous standardized freight
pressure modules occupy the same bay sockets, one visible and one partly hidden,
adding roughly 500 displacement tons of enclosed cargo. Their shutters, collars,
finish, and wear match the common modules. A smaller K-rated power grille fits
inside the unchanged aft-pod boundary. Open truss gaps remain visible around the
additional modules.

## Concord finish

- Command pod and pressure-freight modules: warm ivory `#F1E3C2`
- Truss recesses and lower structural rails: royal blue `#2354A3`
- Access panels, clamp machinery, and safety marks: vermilion `#E44B2D`
- Module collars, turret rings, load-path edges, clamp jaws, and radiator
  frames: polished aluminum/chrome
- Glazing: dark bronze
- Character: bright scheduled-route commercial enamel with practical wear at
  freight sills and clamp contacts, never military camouflage

## Invariants

Preserve the 112 × 36 × 23 m frame, command pod, common freight modules,
keels, crossmembers, strategic-bay boundary, aft pod, two clamp coordinates,
two carried craft and their scale, eight turret coordinates, processor grilles,
airlock, access routes, principal seams, camera, crop, backdrop, and lighting.

Never add a smooth enclosing hull, hangar, scoop, free-flying auxiliary craft,
third clamp, extra turret, point defense, wing, fin, flame, or glowing Jump
ring. Variant edits may alter only the strategic-bay equipment and the power
grille inside its common boundary.

## Catalog plate

Use a complete front-port three-quarter view about 12 degrees above centerline,
restrained near-orthographic perspective, 3:2 landscape, warm upper-front-port
key, weak cool aft rim, charcoal sparse starfield, and faint teal plotting grid.
Render original late-1970s/early-1980s gouache-and-airbrush technical art with

weapon fire, motion blur, modern gray rendering, or franchise resemblance.

## Reusable production prompts

```text
Neutral anchor: create the unpainted 112 × 36 × 23 m, 1,900-ton distributed
freight frame with blunt command pod, paired box-truss keels, crossmembers,
common rectangular pressure-freight modules and ribbed shutters, one vacant
mid-aft strategic bay, compact aft pod, two empty visible 30-ton clamp saddles,
four visible and four hidden turret rings, ten processor grilles, airlock,
catwalks, and handrails. Keep the open load paths obvious. No weapon, craft,
Jump equipment, scoop, enclosing shell, or paint.

Leviathan fit: preserve every anchor coordinate. Install a segmented non-glowing
Jump radiator, two field-coil housings, cable trunks, service tanks, and the
larger Q power grille in the strategic bay. Fit four visible defensive turret
cups: three paired-beam and one mixed missile/sand. Clamp the canonical 8.4 m
Archimedes forward and 19.5 m Nausicaa aft. Apply Concord ivory, blue,
vermilion, and chrome. No extra weapon, craft, cargo module, scoop, or redesign.

Behemoth fit: edit the Leviathan plate without moving any shared feature. Remove
all Jump-specific hardware and replace it with a matched pair of approximately
500-ton combined pressure-freight modules in the same strategic-bay sockets.
Reduce only the power grille from Q to K. Preserve both clamped craft, all eight
turret coordinates, common freight modules, livery, camera, and lighting.
```

   two empty clamp saddles.
