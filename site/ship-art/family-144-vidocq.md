# Vidocq Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 144*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-144`; `ship-144` Vidocq
- Hull: 200 displacement tons / approximately 2,800 m³
- Dimensions: 46 m long, 18 m wide, and 11 m high
- Configuration: standard; technology level 12
- Protection: four armor points from one crystaliron layer, self-sealing shell,
  and armored bulkheads around the bridge and drives
- Drives: no Jump drive; maneuver F and power F / thrust 6
- Endurance: two weeks of power-plant fuel
- Control: basic military electronics, hardened holographic bridge, fib/bis
  Model/3 computer, emergency power, and improved magazine
- Mission spaces: 32.1-ton evidence-and-supply hold, two brigs, boarding work
  rooms, workshop, repair drones, and one-bed medical bay for fourteen crew
- Boat facilities: one 20-ton full hangar carrying one Caduceus (`ship-145`)
- Armament: one twin-plasma triple-turret cup, one mixed beam/missile/sand
  triple turret, two point-defense gatling-laser nodes, 24 missiles, and 20
  sand canisters
- Fuel handling: one scoop and one processor

## Canonical chassis

Vidocq is a compact, symmetric non-atmospheric patrol cutter with a blunt
protected command prow, shallow central waist, strong lower keel, broad port
launch-bay shoulder, and an oversized six-gravity engineering block. Its
46 × 18 × 11 m envelope keeps the approximately 2,800 m³ pressure volume
credible without making the vessel resemble an aircraft. It has no wings,
tail, lifting-body nose, landing gear, or Jump-drive structure.

The hardened bridge sits behind five narrow panes. Paired circular instrument
collars, a white ceramic radome, a fine antenna grid, and teal optical sockets
form the basic-military sensor suite; none is a weapon. Two forward waist
airlocks flank an inspection-and-grappling service station. The smaller closed
6 × 3.5 m evidence shutter remains clearly separate from the hangar.

## Caduceus hangar

The closed two-leaf port shutter protects a full hangar sized for the canonical
17.5 × 6.4 × 4.6 m Caduceus fast launch. Preserve an approximately 20 m
internal launch axis, at least 7.5 m clear width, at least 5.5 m clear height,
the heavy outer frame, central leaf seam, protected guide rails, and clear
departure corridor. The 20-ton craft remains inside and never appears beside
the cutter in the catalog plate.

The hangar is a boat facility, not a cargo door, missile bank, or broadside
weapon. Its large uninterrupted shutter and safety threshold must remain
legible under every finish.

## Weapon map and coverage

Two one-ton triple-turret rings occupy complementary hemispheres:

- the visible forward dorsal/port-shoulder cup carries exactly two short,
  heat-shielded plasma emitters and one armored blanking plate; it covers
  forward, port, dorsal, and much of the aft arc;
- the hidden ventral/aft cup carries one beam laser, one closed missile rack,
  and one sandcaster; it covers ventral, starboard, and stern approaches.

This division avoids redundant crown-only coverage even though the cutter has
only two hardpoints. The standard plate shows only the plasma cup. It must not
invent a second visible turret, turn a sensor collar into a gun, or expose a
missile.

Two point-defense nodes form one dorsal/ventral pair. The plate shows one tiny
aft-dorsal multi-lens node; its underside partner is occluded. Both are much
smaller than the turret cup and sensor collars.

## Drives and external services

Four deeply recessed maneuver apertures and two dark ribbed power grilles make
the F/F six-gravity stern visibly stronger than a merchant drive block. One
protected lower-keel scoop and one compact processor grille support local
patrol operations. Emergency-power access and repair-drone slots remain small
service fittings.

Vidocq is confined to its home system. Never add a segmented Jump radiator,
field-coil housing, Jump ring, or glow. Its strategic dependence on a base is
part of the silhouette as surely as its large engines are.

## Civic Survey finish

Civic Survey Works uses warm white `#F4F0D8`, saturated cyan `#25A9B8`, lime
`#A8C83E`, and protected clean chrome. The scheme declares accountable civil
authority and makes the cutter easy to identify during inspection or rescue.
Chrome concentrates around instrument collars, airlocks, the hangar frame, and
service interfaces. Restrained threshold markings may identify boarding and
launch hazards, but naval camouflage and gray default rendering are forbidden.

## Invariants

Preserve the 46 × 18 × 11 m standard hull; five-pane prow; sensor collars,
radome, grid, and optical sockets; waist and keel; two airlocks and boarding
station; evidence shutter; 20-ton hangar frame and clearance; dorsal/ventral
turret coordinates; paired PD coordinates; scoop and processor; four maneuver
apertures; two power grilles; principal seams; camera; crop; backdrop; and
lighting.

Never move both turrets to the dorsal surface, expose the ventral mixed turret,
show more than one PD node, confuse a sensor with a gun, open the hangar, show
the Caduceus externally, add a Jump component, add a wing, fin, landing gear,
external tank, flame, text, logo, or watermark.

## Catalog plate and reusable prompts

Use a complete front-port three-quarter view about 12 degrees above centerline,
restrained near-orthographic perspective, 3:2 landscape, warm upper-front-port
key, weak cool aft rim, charcoal sparse starfield, and faint teal plotting grid.
Render original late-1970s/early-1980s gouache-and-airbrush technical art with
bright opaque enamel and protected chrome. No planet, motion blur, modern gray
rendering, or recognizable franchise design.

```text
Neutral anchor: create the unpainted 46 × 18 × 11 m, 200-ton Vidocq standard
system cutter with blunt protected prow, five-pane bridge, calm symmetric sensor
suite, shallow waist, strong keel, two boarding airlocks and inspection station,
separate evidence shutter, one closed 20 × 7.5 × 5.5 m Caduceus full-hangar
shutter, exactly one visible dorsal/port and one hidden ventral/aft turret ring,
one visible/one hidden tiny PD socket, one scoop, one processor, four maneuver
apertures, and two power grilles. No installed weapon, Jump hardware, external
craft, open door, paint, text, or glow.

Vidocq fit: preserve every anchor coordinate. Fit the visible turret with exactly
two short plasma emitters and one blanked position; activate one tiny aft-dorsal
PD node. Leave the mixed turret on the hidden ventral/aft surface. Keep every
sensor barrel-free, both shutters closed, and the Caduceus inside. Apply Civic
Survey warm white, cyan, lime, and chrome. Do not add another visible weapon or
any Jump-drive component.
```

## Production sequence and provenance

1. Generate and approve the neutral cutter with fixed sensor, access, hangar,
   evidence, weapon, PD, fuel, and drive coordinates.
2. Derive the production plate from the anchor, fitting only the visible plasma
   cup and one PD node while preserving the hidden complementary arcs.
3. Check the finished plate against the canonical Caduceus dimensions and
   confirm that no boat, Jump hardware, or accidental sensor-weapon appears.
