# Grapnel Visual Manifest

## Mechanical identity

- Record: `ship-211` Grapnel
- Path: Marque Marine Yards (P5)

- Dimensions: 18.6 m long, 9.4 m wide, and 4.8 m high
- Fit: TL11, four armor points, two-person control cabin, fourteen acceleration

- Limitations: no Jump drive, fuel scoop, sleeping accommodation, or second

Grapnel is a separate broad landing/boarding-boat family. It must not reuse the
long Argonaut clipper, flattened-cylinder Caduceus, or Wayfarer utility hull.
Its approved dimensions set one half of Ravelin's 50-ton hangar load.

## Canonical architecture

The pressure body is a broad flattened hexagon with a clipped faceted command
prow, flared protective shoulder rails, deep boarding waist, chamfered belly,
and squared twin-field stern. One occupied deck and an equipment bilge
plausibly enclose approximately 420 m³.

A protected four-pane control brow serves two operators; three panes are
normally readable from the port view and the far pane is partly hidden. Seven
small dark-bronze window bays form one straight port strip for seven paired
seat rows. Exactly one 2.4 m airlock follows the strip, surrounded by fold-flat
boarding handholds. A separate flush burgundy dorsal transfer leaf serves the
2.5-ton cargo hold. One small lower-port grille serves the processor.

One low dorsal axial channel carries one fixed forward beam laser with a narrow
cobalt optic and gold-toned chrome collar. It has no circular ring and must
never become a trainable turret. Two aft field collars around one power grille,
four flush landing-pad doors, two recovery tie-downs, and protected four-point
armor edges complete the craft.
