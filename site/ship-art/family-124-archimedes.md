## Mechanical identity

- Record: `ship-124` Archimedes
- Path: Outer Reach Cooperative (P3)
- Hull: 10 displacement tons / approximately 140 m³; standard
- Dimensions: 8.4 m long, 5.2 m wide, and 3.8 m high
- Fit: TL11, radiation shielding, two-person cockpit, four acceleration seats,
  one airlock, 1.9 tons cargo, sA maneuver and power / thrust 2, one week
  endurance, and no weapon or Jump drive

Archimedes is produced before the Corbett family because both destroyer records
carry one inside their common 70-ton hangar. Its approved dimensions and shape
set the parent hangar's third craft position.

## Canonical architecture

Archimedes is a squat rectangular vacuum work pod, not a streamlined launch.
The blunt forward cockpit has four protected panes for two operators. Two small
port cabin windows reveal the four-seat occupied bay. One human-scale pressure
airlock and one small two-leaf cargo hatch serve the 1.9-ton hold.

Layered shield panels, accessible fasteners, maintenance sockets, and fold-flat
handholds make the radiation-protected shell readable without implying armor.
One dorsal hardpoint disk remains fully closed and unarmed. Four recessed field-
thruster collars surround the ribbed aft power grille. There is no scoop,
external tank, Jump hardware, wing, tail, or exposed landing skid.

Preserve the 8.4 × 5.2 × 3.8 m box, four cockpit panes, two cabin windows,
airlock, cargo hatch, closed hardpoint, four thruster collars, power grille,
landing-pad doors, seams, camera, crop, backdrop, and lighting.
