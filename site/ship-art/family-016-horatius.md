# Horatius Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 016*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-16`; `ship-16` Horatius
- Shipyard path: Path 7, Admiralty Line Works
- Hull: 10 displacement tons / approximately 140 m³
- Dimensions: 11.4 m long, 4.3 m wide, and 4.4 m high
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11; non-standard design
- Protection: unarmored
- Drives: maneuver sC and power sG / thrust 6; no Jump drive
- Endurance: one week of power-plant fuel
- Control: one pilot in a one-person cockpit; basic-military electronics,
  Model/2 computer, Evade/1, and Fire Control/1
- Capacity: no passengers, pressure airlock, or cargo
- Armament: one trainable single beam-laser turret; no ammunition

Horatius is an ammunition-independent laser interceptor for short carrier
sorties. Speed, numbers, coordinated recovery, and its energy weapon substitute
for armor and endurance; the craft has no independent strategic capability.

## Canonical tower shield

The 11.4 × 4.3 × 4.4 m streamlined hull is a narrow, tall “tower-shield”
lifting blade: short blunt faceted upper prow, deep continuous ventral keel,
pinched waist, high-backed centerbody, and compact vertical engineering stern.
Short thick lower chines remain part of the pressure envelope rather than
becoming wings. The deep filled hull plausibly encloses approximately 140 m³.

Horatius is mechanically identical to Apollo (`ship-14`), but family identity
outranks equal equipment. It must never inherit Apollo's long horizontal
spindle, five-pane visor, forward lower-port hardpoint, four-aperture cruciform
stern, or longitudinal panel layout. It also remains distinct from the wedge,
arrowhead, dart, and lenticular fighter families already issued.

One uninterrupted dark wraparound visor forms the forward command cap. It is a
single pane with no subdivisions, and its canopy seam is the only personnel
access. The small lower-forward port rectangle is a flush barrel-free military
sensor flat. There is no airlock, side door, passenger window, cargo shutter,
armor band, or layered plate. Landing seams, tie-downs, and recovery fittings
remain human-scale.

## Aft-flank beam gimbal

The only 3–4 m hardpoint ring occupies the mid-to-aft upper port flank, visibly
on the side rather than the dorsal crown or forward chin. Its compact low cup is
canted outward and upward and carries exactly one short narrow cyan optical
beam emitter in one recessed aperture.

The position complements a fast interceptor's maneuvering by covering port,
dorsal, aft, and portions of the forward arc. One hardpoint still leaves
ventral-forward and far-starboard blind regions. Do not add another cup, paired
optic, point-defense lens, missile shutter, or gun-like sensor. Three small
signal-red-rimmed circles along the deep keel are solid recovery sockets aligned
to the landing datum; they are far smaller than the turret and have no lens,
barrel, or weapon function.

## Drives and Admiralty recognition

Two tall D-shaped maneuver-field apertures flank one distinct large vertical
ribbed power-service grille at the compact stern. Hidden secondary field
surfaces remain on the far side. The oversized grille expresses the sG plant
that powers both six-gravity performance and the beam weapon. There is no scoop,
processor, Jump hardware, external tank, flame, or glow.

Admiralty Line Works uses deep navy `#203B69`, warm white `#E8E5D4`, signal red
`#C43C35`, and bright aluminum/chrome. Navy covers the deep keel, engineering
block, and selected high-backed panels; white defines the command prow, visor
surround, and formal mid-flank recognition path; red marks the turret collar,
recovery sockets, and drive-safety details; aluminum protects the structural
chines, visor, hardpoint, sensor, landing, recovery, and machinery interfaces.
The bright trim is structure and datum marking, not armor.

## Invariants and production prompts

Preserve the 11.4 × 4.3 × 4.4 m deep narrow silhouette; one-piece visor and
canopy seam; lower-forward sensor flat; aft upper-port ring and one-optic cup;
two tall stern apertures and vertical power grille; solid recovery sockets;
landing seams; absence of armor and doors; camera; crop; backdrop; and lighting.
Keep the pilot internal.

```text
Neutral anchor: create the unpainted 11.4 × 4.3 × 4.4 m, 10-ton streamlined
Horatius tower-shield fighter with blunt command prow, one uninterrupted visor,
deep ventral keel, pinched waist, high centerbody, compact vertical stern, one
sealed aft upper-port flank turret ring, one flush lower-forward sensor flat,
two tall D-shaped maneuver apertures, one vertical power grille, landing seams,
recovery sockets, and tie-downs. No installed weapon, armor, airlock, side door,
cargo shutter, wing, fin, Jump/fuel gear, text, or glow.

Horatius fit: preserve every anchor coordinate. Replace only the aft-flank
ring's blanking disk with one low trainable cup carrying exactly one short cyan
beam optic. Apply Admiralty navy, warm white, red, and aluminum without adding
armor. Keep the keel circles solid recovery sockets. No second optic or mount,
dorsal/forward weapon, point defense, text, insignia, weapon fire, or drive glow.
```

## Production sequence and provenance

1. Generate and approve the neutral tower-shield hull with fixed visor, sensor,
   aft-flank hardpoint, keel recovery, drive, and power coordinates.
2. Derive the production fit from that anchor, using Apollo only as an
   Admiralty finish reference and fitting one single-optic gimbal.
3. Review Apollo and Horatius together to prove independent geometry, then
   verify exactly one weapon, absent armor/access/cargo, and clear sG machinery.
