# Polo Family Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 038*

## Mechanical identity

- Family: `family-38`, Polo
- Members: `ship-38` Marco Polo, `ship-39` Niccolò Polo, and `ship-40` Maffeo Polo
- Native paths: Path 3 Outer Reach for Marco; Path 1 Concord Exchange for
  Niccolò and Maffeo
- Hull: 200 displacement tons / approximately 2,800 m³
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11; no armor
- Shared performance: Jump drive B / Jump-2, maneuver and power drive C /
  thrust 3, one jump, and two weeks of power endurance
- Shared systems: basic civilian electronics, Model/2 computer, fuel processor,
  repair drones, autodoc, and one emergency low berth
- Shared armament: one double turret fitted with one beam laser and one capped
  partner aperture; one additional hardpoint remains closed and empty

All three records are internal fits of the same trader chassis. Marco carries
four crew staterooms and 88.7 tons freight. Niccolò turns a twenty-ton forward
hold into fifteen steerage places, two heads, and a self-service common/galley,
leaving 68.7 tons freight. Maffeo installs four additional passenger staterooms
and a larger common/galley, leaving 67.7 tons. The forward insert changes doors,
windows, and ventilation within one fixed aperture; the main hold and outer
hull do not move.

## Canonical family architecture

The Polo is 44.0 m long, 17.0 m wide, and 10.0 m high with three useful deck
levels. It is a broad commercial lozenge: a rounded low command prow, a deep
rectangular cargo body enclosed by swept aerodynamic shoulders, and a compact
aft engineering wedge. The filled, chamfered envelope plausibly encloses
approximately 2,800 m³. It is larger and broader than the 100-ton Mercator but
retains an obvious loading route and surface-port utility.

The command prow has a protected seven-port bridge brow and one recessed port
crew-access hatch. Exactly four crew-stateroom windows form a short upper strip
behind the brow. Below and aft of that strip is one fixed 8.0 × 3.2 m forward
conversion aperture. Its perimeter and sill are family invariants; its neutral
anchor grid has five vertical insert stations. Marco fills those stations with
a closed five-leaf folding freight shutter.
Niccolò fills the five bays with protected steerage windows and two low sanitary
vent grilles. Maffeo replaces the internal grid with one continuous six-pane
occupancy insert: four cabin windows and two wider common-room windows.

The main cargo body carries one broad 10.0 × 4.8 m closed two-leaf freight
door with a low loading sill, unchanged in every fit. A small repair-drone
shutter lies immediately aft. On the dorsal centerline, one common 3.5 m
double-turret ring carries a compact housing with one narrow beam-laser optic
and one visibly capped partner aperture. A second circular hardpoint cover lies
flush aft and remains closed.

The internal Jump drive reads through one segmented transverse heat-rejection
band at the cargo/engineering boundary and two low oval coil-service bulges
with partial bright-metal collars. They are maintained service landmarks, not
glowing rings. One broad flush ventral fuel-scoop grid, two rectangular aft
maneuver apertures, one central power grille, six landing-pad doors, paired
docking guides, and the main load seams complete the chassis.

### Invariants

All variants preserve exactly:

- the 44.0 × 17.0 × 10.0 m broad three-volume streamlined silhouette;
- command prow, seven bridge ports, crew-access hatch, and four crew windows;
- fixed forward conversion-aperture perimeter and sill;
- main two-leaf freight door, loading sill, and repair-drone shutter;
- double-turret ring and housing with one beam optic and one capped aperture;
- closed second hardpoint, Jump heat band, coil-service bulges and collars,
  fuel-scoop grid, aft drive apertures, power grille, landing-pad doors,
  docking guides, and load seams; and
- camera, crop, perspective, backdrop, and lighting.

Do not add a second weapon, visible missile, open cargo, external Jump ring,
glow, external tank, separate aircraft wing, exposed landing gear, carried
vehicle, second main freight door, or altered conversion-aperture perimeter.

## Variant deltas

### `ship-38` Marco Polo — Outer Reach freight fit

- Forward aperture: one solid closed five-leaf folding freight shutter; no passenger
  windows or sanitary vents
- Four crew-stateroom windows only
- Outer Reach avocado `#7A9238` main hull, ochre `#C9902C` cargo body, brick red
  access/door/drive accents, brushed and patch-polished aluminum

  without changing the common aerodynamic skin

### `ship-39` Niccolò Polo — Concord steerage fit

- Forward aperture: five narrow protected window groups above two low sanitary
  vent grilles, expressing a dense occupied deck without glass walls
- Four crew-stateroom windows remain unchanged; no extra cabin-window strip
- Concord warm ivory `#F1E3C2`, royal blue `#2354A3`, vermilion `#E44B2D`, and
  polished aluminum/chrome
- Orderly commercial spacing and clean standardized collars

### `ship-40` Maffeo Polo — Concord standard-passenger fit

- Forward aperture: one continuous six-pane occupancy insert with four narrow
  passenger-cabin windows and two wider common-room windows
- Four crew-stateroom windows remain unchanged, for eight stateroom windows
  total outside the two common-room panes
- Same Concord palette and principal color-block boundaries as Niccolò, with a
  longer warm-ivory occupancy band and brighter polished trim
