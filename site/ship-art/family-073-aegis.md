# Aegis Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 073*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-73`; `ship-73` Aegis
- Shipyard path: Path 8, Redoubt Shipbuilding
- Hull: 300 displacement tons / approximately 4,200 m³
- Dimensions: 48 m long, 20 m wide, and 12.5 m high
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11; non-standard design
- Protection: eleven whole points of crystaliron armor and one increment of
  reinforced structure
- Drives: maneuver J and power J; no Jump drive
- Endurance: two weeks of power-plant fuel
- Control and complement: hardened holographic standard bridge, advanced
  electronics, Model/3-bis computer, and fifteen crew
- Accommodation and support: two staterooms, two crew berthings, armory,
  office, emergency low berth, one medical bed, repair drones, two fuel
  processors, and a 57.25-ton hold
- Main weapons: one 51-ton meson-gun bay, one beam/beam/sand triple turret,
  one missile/missile/sand triple turret, and three point-defense lasers
- Ammunition: 72 standard missiles and 60 sand canisters, all internal

Aegis is a heavily armored system-defense cruiser built to engage larger
intruders. Its meson bay, reinforced structure, advanced electronics, local-
performance machinery, repair and medical support, and useful patrol hold
permit sustained operations inside its assigned system. It cannot Jump.

## Independent shield-vault wedge

The 48 × 20 × 12.5 m streamlined hull is a reinforced “shield-vault wedge”:
blunt faceted armored prow, four nested swept shield shells around a deeply
buried command vault, two thick occupied shoulder masses, enormous axial lower-
forward meson spine, deep continuous keel, protected working waist, and short
broad engineering stern. The wedge and shoulders are filled spacecraft volumes,
not aircraft wings, fins, pods, tanks, or a flat fighter. The tapered envelope
plausibly contains approximately 4,200 m³.

Aegis is a singleton. Never copy Bastion despite shared yard and tonnage;
Bastion's launch hangar and eight-point armor require a different chassis.
Never copy Condottiere/Hawkwood, Monitor's standard casemate, Trident, Warden,
Bulwark, Cerberus, or another Redoubt hull merely because weapons overlap.

Exactly five protected panes form the deeply recessed bridge brow behind the
second shield shell. One of two stateroom windows is visible on the port command
flank and one hidden starboard; crew berthing is windowless. One closed
reinforced personnel/boarding airlock, one armory shutter, one office/service
panel, one plain emergency-berth panel, one plain medical-service hatch, and
one repair-drone slot remain closed and unmarked. No panel carries a cross,
badge, text, or emblem.

One closed 7.5 × 3.8 m two-leaf cargo/patrol-store shutter occupies the mid-to-
aft lower port keel. It serves the 57.25-ton hold and remains clearly separate
from the meson channel. There is no hangar or carried craft.

## Meson architecture and coverage map

The three hardpoint-consuming installations occupy complementary positions:

1. visible axial lower-forward center/port spine: one 51-ton meson-gun bay;
2. visible lower-aft port flank at the shield/keel transition: beam/beam/sand
   triple turret; and
3. hidden upper-aft starboard shoulder: missile/missile/sand triple turret.

The meson bay is 13 m long × 5.5 m high, occupies roughly 714 m³, and drives
the prow and keel architecture. Its multi-deck accelerator channel has a deep
feed trunk, layered radiation baffles, cooling/service panels, and multiple
insulated annular field-coil collars. One recessed meson emitter terminus at
the forward end is a dark circular aperture inside concentric field rings,
explicitly not a projecting conventional gun barrel. There is one emitter only
and no beam, glow, flame, smoke, or visible magazine.

The standard plate shows one of two turrets. Its low shared cup contains exactly
two matching short beam-laser optics and one distinct blunt four-port sandcaster
projector. The hidden starboard cup contains two closed missile racks and one
sandcaster; no part of it is visible and it must not migrate to the crown for
display. Ammunition remains internal.

Three point-defense laser coordinates form a broad coverage triangle:

- visible forward upper-port shield cheek;
- visible lower-aft port keel; and
- hidden upper-mid starboard shoulder.

Each node is a 0.4–0.8 m clean dark-teal optic in an orange/stainless collar,
much smaller than the turret ring. Never arm a bridge detail, meson service
fitting, processor, coupling, door latch, landing pad, sensor, or cargo frame.

## Fuel, drive, armor, and structure

One square port fuel-processor grille and adjacent circular coupling are
visible; the second processor is hidden starboard. Aegis has no recorded scoop.

There is no Jump drive. Never add a segmented radiator, coil-service housing,
external ring, tank, luminous portal, or glow. Four deep maneuver apertures
are visible in the port stern bank and four matching starboard apertures hidden;
two separate ribbed power grilles serve the J/J high-performance machinery.

Eleven armor points and reinforced structure dominate the silhouette through
four nested prow/command shells, deep shoulder plates, overlapping longitudinal
flank belts, heavy meson/cargo/airlock frames, redundant keel ribs, and deeply
recessed machinery. The shells are integrated protection, not detachable armor
pods or decorative ribs.

## Redoubt recognition finish

- Principal shield shells, occupied shoulders and stern blocks: fire red
  `#B83B2E`
- Meson baffles/collars, weapon and PD collars, processor/service, drive and
  recovery accents: orange `#E9782E`
- Buried bridge surround, access/cargo/service panels and selected neutron-
  shadow plates: bone `#D8CCA9`
- Shield edges, meson load paths/coils, access, cargo, weapon, fuel, docking
  and drive interfaces: heavy bright stainless steel
- Bridge glass, meson depth, weapon faces, recesses, seams, slats and grilles:
  restrained gunmetal/dark teal

Monitor supplies Redoubt finish vocabulary only. Aegis retains its independent
streamlined shield-vault wedge, concentric meson accelerator, lower-flank
turret, hidden starboard turret, processor pair, eleven-point armor, and J/J
stern.
