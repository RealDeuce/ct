# Cerberus Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 186*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-186`; `ship-186` Cerberus
- Hull: 400 displacement tons / approximately 5,600 m³, plus 20 tons external
- Dimensions: 66 m long, 21 m wide, and 14.5 m high
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11
- Protection: ten crystaliron armor points, radiation shielding, and armored
  bulkheads around bridge, drives, and magazines
- Drives: Jump-C / Jump-1, maneuver Q, and power Q / thrust 6
- Endurance: one Jump and two weeks of power-plant fuel
- Control: advanced electronics, holographic controls, fib/bis Model/3,
  emergency power, and improved magazine
- Mission support: 30 crew, compact accommodation, armory, two brigs, two-bed
  medical bay, workshop, repair drones, and 0.9 tons stores
- Boat facilities: one 30-ton external clamp carrying one 20-ton Caduceus
  (`ship-185`); no internal hangar
- Main battery: one particle-beam bay and one particle-beam barbette
- Secondary battery: one triple-beam and one mixed missile/missile/sand turret
- Point defense: four laser nodes
- Ammunition: 120 missiles and 40 sand canisters
- Fuel handling: two processors and no scoop

## Independent Redoubt chassis

Cerberus occupies a 66 × 21 × 14.5 m long narrow Redoubt spear-citadel,
deliberately separate from Rampart's shorter broad hull. Three stepped prow
lobes divide command and weapon structure; a pinched protected waist, deep
pressure body, faceted lower keel, asymmetric boat shoulder, and massive stern
complete the streamlined lifting body. It has no wings, tail, or fins.

Ten-point armor uses overlapping plate edges, deep recesses, short protected
load paths, armored command/drive/magazine boundaries, and a radiation seam
band. The bridge is buried under the central prow lobe with exactly six narrow
panes. Paired cheek sensors, an antenna grid, and a corrected three-facet flush
sensor/service block remain barrel-free.

## Heavy-weapon map

The one particle-beam bay occupies the large lower/forward port-shoulder
channel. Its approximately 12 × 5 m multi-deck insulated recess and massive
emitter are the largest weapon architecture on the ship.

The one particle-beam barbette occupies the smaller 7–9 m mid-port-flank
channel. It uses one recessed emitter and remains visibly larger than a turret
but smaller than the bay. Because both heavy weapons are single rather than
bilateral fits, both appear in the standard view; no hidden duplicates exist.

## Turret and PD coverage

Two triple turrets occupy complementary stations:

- the visible outward-canted forward port-shoulder cup is triple-beam, using
  three forward optical emitters in a smooth low housing;
- the hidden ventral/starboard/aft cup carries two closed missile racks and one
  sandcaster.

The hidden mixed station owns lower, starboard, and stern approaches while the
visible beam cup covers forward, dorsal, and port arcs. Preserve this division
and never add a second visible cup or migrate both mounts to the crown. Narrow
side glazing on the visible cup is not an additional emitter.

Four PD lasers form two complementary pairs. The plate shows one tiny forward-
port lens and one aft-port/ventral-lip lens; the starboard/lower partners are
hidden. Service optics and the external-boat clamp do not count as PD nodes.

## External Caduceus

Cerberus has no internal hangar or hangar shutter. One 30-ton clamp on the upper
aft port shoulder carries the canonical 17.5 × 6.4 × 4.6 m Caduceus
(`ship-185`) parallel to the mothership axis, nose forward. The 20-ton launch is
approximately 26.5 percent of Cerberus's length and rests on two short armored
stainless clamp arms with a visible separation line.

The carried craft preserves its established flattened-cylinder geometry,
three-pane cockpit, five-window port rail, airlock, cargo door, closed dorsal
hardpoint, paired aft drive collars, and Venture sunflower/cobalt/orange/chrome
finish. It remains unarmed. It must not cover the bridge, turret, heavy-weapon
channels, Jump radiator, drives, airlocks, or sensors, and no second boat may
appear.

## Drives and fuel recognition

Jump-C / Jump-1 uses one compact non-glowing segmented upper-aft radiator and
two paired field-coil/service housings. Six huge recessed maneuver apertures and
four power grilles identify the Q/Q six-gravity stern. The tiny stores hatch,
boarding airlocks, grapples, emergency access, and repair fittings remain
closed and barrel-free.

Exactly two processor grilles are present. Cerberus has no scoop; do not add a
ventral intake, external tank, or generic glowing Jump ring.

## Redoubt finish

Redoubt Shipbuilding uses fire red `#B83B2E`, orange `#E9782E`, bone
`#D8CCA9`, heavy stainless, and restrained gunmetal. Fire red dominates armor;
orange marks shoulders, drives, and hazard surfaces; bone identifies command
and mission inserts; stainless protects edges, weapon collars, and clamp arms;
gunmetal fills deep recesses. The separately built Caduceus retains Venture
yellow/blue/orange rather than being repainted to match.
