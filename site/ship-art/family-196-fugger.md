# Fugger Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 196*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-196`; `ship-196` Fugger
- Hull: 400 displacement tons / approximately 5,600 m³
- Dimensions: 64 m long, 24 m wide, and 15.5 m high
- Configuration: standard; technology level 9; standard design
- Protection: two armor points from one titanium-steel layer
- Drives: Jump-B / Jump-1, one Jump, maneuver B, and power B
- Endurance: four weeks of power-plant fuel
- Control: basic civilian electronics and Model/2 computer
- Personnel: three crew, four staterooms, and two emergency low berths
- Trade capacity: 261 tons cargo; no external load
- Fuel handling: one scoop and three processors
- Armament: none; four unused hardpoints/fire-control stations

## Independent Concord bulk freighter

Fugger is a 64 × 24 × 15.5 m non-aerodynamic freight box. A small five-pane
command cab, gigantic rectangular centerbody, shallow load keel, and compact
aft drive block make the earning volume visibly primary. Three huge adjacent
closed shutters within one continuous reinforced frame represent the 261-ton /
approximately 3,654 m³ hold. Four small crew windows, two emergency-berth
service panels, one access hatch, landing doors, and cargo-handling sockets are
secondary to that uninterrupted freight face.

The hull shares Concord's balanced modules, long datum lines, standardized door
spacing, chrome collars, flush commercial sensors, and obvious turnaround
access without sharing Hudson or Dory geometry. Every freight and service door
remains closed; no container or payload is exposed.

## Empty hardpoints

Four fixed hardpoint coordinates are present but unused:

- one visible forward port/upper-shoulder covered ring;
- one visible aft port/lower-flank covered ring;
- one hidden starboard-upper covered ring; and
- one hidden ventral covered ring.

The standard view therefore shows two smooth covers and hides two. This small
four-station map is below the large-battery distribution threshold but already
reserves complementary upper, lower, port, and starboard arcs for a possible
later defensive refit. Never add a turret cup, barrel, missile shutter,
sandcaster, or PD lens to the base Fugger plate.

## Drives and fuel recognition

Jump-B uses one compact segmented non-glowing radiator and paired field-coil
housings. Two recessed maneuver apertures and two power grilles mark the modest
B/B stern. One broad scoop field lies on the lower forward hull and exactly
three processor banks occupy protected service positions. Fuel remains internal;
there is no exposed tank or glowing Jump ring.

## Concord finish

Concord Exchange uses warm ivory `#F1E3C2`, royal blue `#2354A3`, vermilion
`#E44B2D`, and polished aluminum/chrome. Ivory dominates the pressure box; blue
organizes the upper cargo and crew band; vermilion marks shutter divisions,
access, emergency, scoop, belly, and drive boundaries; chrome protects the
continuous freight frame, handling sockets, hardpoint covers, and machinery.
Bright period color communicates maintained scheduled freight service.
