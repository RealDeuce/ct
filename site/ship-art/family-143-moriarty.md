# Moriarty Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 143*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-143`; `ship-143` Moriarty
- Hull: 800 displacement tons / approximately 11,200 m³
- Dimensions: 78 m long, 29 m wide, and 17.5 m high
- Configuration: streamlined; technology level 11
- Protection: four crystaliron armor points, stealth treatment, and armored
  bulkheads around bridge, drives, and ordnance magazines
- Drives: Jump-J / Jump-2, maneuver N and power R / thrust 4
- Endurance: one Jump and three weeks of power-plant fuel
- Control: advanced electronics, hardened holographic bridge, fib/bis Model/3
  computer, emergency power, and improved magazine
- Mission spaces: 143.2-ton cargo hold, eight brigs, armory, workshop, repair
  drones, and two-bed medical bay for a forty-person raiding complement
- Main battery: two 51-ton particle-beam bays and two plasma barbettes
- Secondary battery: two mixed missile/sand double turrets, two double-beam
  turrets, eight laser point-defense nodes, 192 missiles, and 80 sand canisters
- Carried craft: none
- Fuel handling: no scoop and no processors

## Canonical chassis

Moriarty is a deep asymmetric lifting-body lozenge whose broad belly, clipped
prow, and large port cargo shutter first suggest an unusually fast commercial
freighter. A compact high-output engineering block, offset upper service spine,
shielded engine mouths, and shallow stealth facets make the deception less
convincing at close range. It has no wings or decorative fins.

The protected bridge sits below an armored brow with six narrow panes. One
ordinary personnel airlock and flush landing-gear doors support atmospheric
operations. The genuine approximately 10 × 5 m cargo shutter remains closed,
unarmed, and visually separate from every concealed weapon aperture. False
freight seams and mismatched service panels may disguise weapons, but they must
never make the real hold inaccessible or turn its door into a gunport.

## Weapon map and coverage

The port three-quarter plate shows roughly half of each mirrored battery:

- one of two particle-beam bays, recessed in a long forward-port false-seam
  channel; its starboard partner is occluded;
- one of two plasma barbettes, semi-recessed in the port shoulder and
  substantially larger than a turret; its starboard partner is occluded;
- two of four double turrets: a forward dorsal beam station and an outward-
  canted port-flank mixed station; and
- four of eight tiny point-defense lenses distributed across dorsal and port
  surfaces, with their starboard/ventral partners hidden.

The two hidden turret stations cover the starboard and ventral/aft hemispheres.
The full fit therefore overlaps forward, dorsal, port, starboard, ventral, and
aft arcs instead of placing the secondary battery along the crown. Preserve
that map even when a viewpoint hides the lower stations. The dorsal turret is
a compact twin optical emitter. The flank turret combines one closed missile
shutter with one blunt sandcaster aperture. Neither resembles the much larger
barbette or multi-deck bay.

Eight point-defense positions form four complementary pairs. Visible nodes are
0.4–0.8 m polished lenses, much smaller than turret rings and never promoted to
decorative dorsal guns.

## Drives and external services

Jump-J is represented by one long non-glowing segmented radiator and two
armored coil/service housings along the upper-aft structure. The open-sided
trapezoidal housings in the plate are machinery, not additional turrets: they
carry no barrels or aiming optics. Four recessed stern maneuver apertures and
two dark power grilles identify the N/R drive plant.

Moriarty has neither a fuel scoop nor fuel processors. Do not infer a ventral
scoop, processing grille, or external tank from generic starship art language.
Small grappling and boarding-service fittings may appear near the airlock, but
the design carries no boat, hangar, docking clamp, or exposed prisoner module.

## Rogue Tide finish

Rogue Tide Yards uses deep aubergine `#4D294F`, hot vermilion `#D94B35`,
mustard `#D4A62A`, and blackened chrome. Broad saturated color blocks give the
ship theatrical dockside confidence while low sheen, dark panel breaks, false
seams, and mismatched replaceable panels preserve its covert character. Chrome
is restricted to protected machinery collars and aperture edges rather than
spread across the stealth surfaces.

The result should look merchant-like at long range and only disclose its
predatory purpose when the shutters, recesses, and unusually complete firing
arcs can be examined.

## Invariants

Preserve the 78 × 29 × 17.5 m lifting-body hull; clipped prow; broad belly;
offset spine; compact stern; six-pane bridge; cargo shutter and airlock; armor
and stealth facets; bilateral bay and barbette coordinates; four-turret arc
map; eight point-defense coordinates; Jump radiator and two coil housings; four
maneuver apertures; two power grilles; landing doors; principal seams; camera;
crop; backdrop; and lighting.

Never move all turrets to the dorsal surface, expose more than two turrets, one
bay, one barbette, or four point-defense nodes in the standard view, arm the
cargo shutter, add a scoop or processor, add a hangar or carried craft, open a
missile shutter, expose ordnance, add a wing, fin, external tank, glowing Jump
ring, flame, text, logo, or watermark.

## Catalog plate and reusable prompts

Use a complete front-port three-quarter view about 12 degrees above centerline,
restrained near-orthographic perspective, 3:2 landscape, warm upper-front-port
key, weak cool aft rim, charcoal sparse starfield, and faint teal plotting grid.
Render original late-1970s/early-1980s gouache-and-airbrush technical art with
bright opaque enamel and protected dark chrome. No planet, motion blur, modern
gray rendering, or recognizable franchise design.

Moriarty fit: preserve every anchor coordinate. Reveal the visible port particle

2. Derive the production plate from that anchor, preserving every major hull
