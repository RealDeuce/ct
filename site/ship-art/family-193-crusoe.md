# Crusoe Visual Manifest

*Status: catalog production family 193*

## Mechanical identity

- Family/member: `family-193`; `ship-193` Crusoe
- Hull: 300 displacement tons / approximately 4,200 m³
- Dimensions: 58 m long, 22 m wide, and 14.5 m high
- Configuration: standard; technology level 9; standard design
- Protection: two armor points from one titanium-steel layer
- Drives: Jump-C / Jump-2, one Jump, maneuver C, and power C / thrust 2
- Endurance: four weeks of power-plant fuel
- Control: basic civilian electronics and Model/2 computer
- Accommodation: twelve staterooms, twelve low berths, and seven crew
- Trade capacity: 92 tons cargo; no external load
- Fuel handling: one scoop and three processors
- Battery: two triple-pulse-laser turrets and one triple-sandcaster turret
- Ammunition: 100 sand canisters

## Independent Outer Reach freighter

Crusoe is a 58 × 22 × 14.5 m non-aerodynamic standard hull. A compact faceted
control block with six bridge panes leads a huge rectangular freight pressure
body, protected longitudinal cylindrical tank shoulders, heavy load frame,
replaceable service pods, accessible conduits, shallow keel, and deep aft
engineering block. The cylinders are protected parts of the whole hull rather
than detachable external tanks.

One broad closed three-leaf shutter and heavy aluminum frame make the 92-ton
hold the central visual fact. Two separated cabin-window groups index the
twelve staterooms without serving as a literal deck plan. Three flush four-
position maintenance banks represent twelve low berths; they are not windows
and reveal no occupants. A small mail/service hatch and boarding airlock remain
closed.

## Turret map and fields of fire

Three fixed hardpoint coordinates cover complementary approaches:

- the visible forward port/upper-shoulder ring carries one triple-pulse cup;
- the visible outward-canted aft port/lower-flank ring carries the triple
  sandcaster cup; and
- the hidden ventral/starboard ring carries the second triple-pulse cup.

The forward cup has exactly three short, heavy, ribbed optical emitters. The
lower-flank cup has exactly three blunt closed multi-port projector faces and
no exposed canister. The hidden pulse station owns lower and starboard arcs;
the visible pair covers forward, dorsal, port, aft, and near-ventral approaches.
Never migrate all three mounts to the crown or expose the third turret merely
to display the inventory.

Crusoe's three-station battery is below the threshold that requires the broad
multi-gallery treatment used by capital batteries, but its two-visible/one-
hidden map still avoids a dorsal-only assumption. Service pods, pipes, sensor
ports, processor grilles, and drive apertures are barrel-free.

## Drives and fuel recognition

Jump-C uses one segmented non-glowing upper radiator and paired accessible
field-coil/service housings. Four modest maneuver apertures and two power
grilles identify the C/C two-gravity stern. One protected scoop field occupies
the lower forward keel; exactly three processor banks sit in separate service
positions. The 72-ton fuel allocation remains within the protected shoulders
and frame, never as detachable spheres or a generic glowing ring.

## Outer Reach finish

Outer Reach Cooperative uses avocado `#7A9238`, ochre `#C9902C`, brick red
`#A63F2F`, and brushed or patch-polished aluminum. Avocado marks tanks and
pressure machinery; ochre dominates the cargo door and service modules; brick
red protects command, turret, drive, and safety surfaces; bright metal carries
the load frame, pipes, guards, and collars. Saturated period color keeps the
rugged ship useful-looking rather than drab.
